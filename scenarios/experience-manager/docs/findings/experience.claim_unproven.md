# `experience.claim_unproven`

> **Severity default:** WARNING · **Capability:** evidence_traceability · **Fix class:** manual

## What it means

An active-page machine claim has no recorded evidence.

## How to fix it

Run the experience phase with capture/reconciliation enabled, or downgrade the claim tier until evidence can be produced.

## Provenance

Emitted by experience-manager's evidence traceability checks (dimension `experience`, advisory cap at ERROR). Mapped by the experience phase maturity descriptor once the provider phase lands.

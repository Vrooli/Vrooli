# Independent review of approved Device Studio handoff

Scope: `internal/ACCEPTANCE.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `PROBLEMS.md`, `docs/operations/DEPLOYMENT.md`, delivery goal/queue/targets, PRD/P0 requirements, experience contract, and `goal/evidence/materials/manifest.json`. This review verifies handoff consistency and identifies remaining release evidence; it does not accept the product. The 1A/2A/3A/4A agreement is approved, parent orchestration with Luna is authorized, runtime validation is current-host only, and product acceptance remains pending.

## Findings

1. **E1 UI work has a required design checkpoint that is not yet evidenced.** `experience/README.md:34-36` requires governed concept and paired desktop/mobile mockup checkpoints before product UI work and says ratification is absent. `goal/QUEUE.md:9` activates an E1 API/CLI/UI scope. E1 directive D3 records the design checkpoint as a condition before UI implementation (`goal/epochs/E1.md:37`), but no completion evidence is linked. API/CLI work can continue; starting the UI before that checkpoint would conflict with the experience contract. **Recommended fix:** attach the accepted concept/mockup checkpoint evidence before E1 UI implementation; keep other E1 work proceeding independently.

2. **The handoff does not yet identify the six required product demonstration projects and their outputs.** Acceptance requires six saved demonstrations, direct PNG/MP4 outputs, and approximately 8–12 second physical-motion clips (`internal/ACCEPTANCE.md:45`). Queue E7 assigns six demo projects but the materials manifest maps only historical laptop/phone references and candidate models (`goal/QUEUE.md:18`; `goal/evidence/materials/manifest.json:21-65`). Existing v6 historical clips do not establish saved product projects or actual-renderer output. **Recommended fix:** track each of the six projects, required output files, source/replay inputs, and motion duration as separate release evidence; keep completion pending until the product outputs exist.

3. **The v6 reference heroes do not meet the accepted still-output size and must not be counted as final outputs.** The manifest records laptop 1672×941 and phone 1536×1024 (`goal/evidence/materials/manifest.json:71-80,214-224`), while acceptance requires stills with a long edge of at least 2560 pixels (`internal/ACCEPTANCE.md:48`). The manifest correctly calls these historical references rather than approved renderer outputs. **Recommended fix:** retain them as direction references and require separately pinned actual-renderer outputs at the accepted size before marking still/demo obligations complete.

4. **Generation and asset rights remain open for product reuse.** The manifest says imagegen model/seed/cost metadata are absent and no redistribution attestation is present for the laptop/phone generated plates (`goal/evidence/materials/manifest.json:13-19,2803`). Product requirements require a supported generation route and receipts/review (`requirements/03-photographic/module.json:94-100`) plus scoped rights/release evidence (`requirements/08-reviews-rights/module.json:10-16`). **Recommended fix:** keep those plates as private historical references pending rights determination, and separately prove in-app generation with complete owner receipts and review; hold any paid live call for a specific allowance (`internal/ACCEPTANCE.md:56,61-63`).

5. **No qualified eight-group library is represented by the present model candidates.** The material handoff records two useful free candidates, while the manifest says exact calibrated models, laptop articulation, phone rotation, and released group assets are missing (`goal/evidence/materials/README.md:7-9`; `manifest.json:2798-2805`). The P0 library requires all eight groups and explicit supported poses (`PRD.md:14-15,23-26`; `requirements/02-library/module.json:8-16,37-44`); acceptance further names monitor/TV, smart display/frame, and vehicle host profiles (`internal/ACCEPTANCE.md:44`). **Recommended fix:** maintain per-group rights, measurement, view/mount/motion, recipe, and review evidence; keep candidate assets unqualified until each required scope passes.

## Findings

This is a real sequencing gap even though E1’s API/CLI work can proceed. **Recommended fix:** complete and record the governed concept plus paired mockup checkpoint before implementing product UI, or explicitly constrain any earlier E1 UI work to the existing project slice and defer wider workbench UI until that checkpoint is accepted.

2. **The selected v6 images cannot serve as the required high-resolution final still outputs.** The material manifest records the laptop hero as 1672×941 and the phone hero as 1536×1024 (`goal/evidence/materials/manifest.json:70-80,214-224`); acceptance requires stills with a long edge of at least 2560 pixels (`internal/ACCEPTANCE.md:48`). The manifest correctly labels both as historical references (`manifest.json:8-10,21-49`), so this is a missing deliverable/material, not a defect in the references. **Recommended fix:** keep these files tagged as direction references and provide a separate actual-renderer still at the accepted output size before counting a demo/output requirement as met.

3. **The generated plates are not cleared product assets, and their old receipts cannot satisfy the in-app generation obligation.** The manifest records that the v6 image-generation receipts omit model, seed and cost, and contain no explicit redistribution attestation (`manifest.json:13-19,2798-2805`). The P0 generation requirement calls for a supported owner route, prompt/control and provider/model/cost receipts, and final-look/calibration review (`requirements/03-photographic/module.json:94-100`); the P0 rights requirement also requires pinned bytes, license and attribution obligations before reusable release (`requirements/08-reviews-rights/module.json:10-16`). **Recommended fix:** keep the historical plates private/reference-only until reuse rights are established; separately exercise the product’s supported generation route with its complete receipt and review. If that route incurs paid provider spend, hold the live call for the concrete allowance required by `internal/ACCEPTANCE.md:56,61-63`.

4. **The material handoff does not yet cover the required eight-group library.** It identifies useful phone and laptop candidates, while noting no qualified exact-device models tied to the v6 plates and no hinge/rotation qualification (`goal/evidence/materials/README.md:7-9`; `manifest.json:2798-2805`). The P0 library requirement calls for reviewed assets and explicit poses across all eight groups (`PRD.md:14-15,23-26`; `requirements/02-library/module.json:8-16,37-44`), and the delivery agreement names the family/mount distinctions (`internal/ACCEPTANCE.md:44`). **Recommended fix:** track source, rights, dimensions, references, supported pose/motion, and review materials per group; keep the two current models as candidates until their scoped qualification is complete.

# Monetization — Device Studio

## Purpose Of This Document

Apply existing Vrooli monetization to device presentation; no new price, subscription rank or bundle membership is authorized by this foundation.

## Role In Vrooli

Device Studio supports launch-video production and reusable visual presentation. The candidate business-bundle role is a depth capability; actual Offer Desk membership/promotion remains operator-owned. T1 is served through monetization/marketing-crew, I1 through reusable tooling and scenario QA. No source guidance or experiment is retired during foundation work.

## Customer / Buyer

App makers and product marketers want polished reusable shots without learning full 3D production. Local operators value private media and reproducible workflows; downloaded-app users value simple installation, optional account-funded AI and curated content.

## Packaging

Local editing/import/composition/export and installed local rendering work without mandatory commercial sign-in. BYOK uses the user's provider account without duplicate Vrooli credit charges. Optional Vrooli-funded inference uses trusted owner authorization/reservation/settlement. Official download entitlement is separate from local capabilities. Preserve private desktop, browser-hosted and explicit remote-worker modes without silently moving data/funding.

Offer Desk owns declared offers/prices; LPBS owns business accounts/subscriptions/credits/downloads; AI Gateway/Image Tools route supported operations; Money Ledger records financial actuals. Device Studio owns only project/job budgets and references to authoritative receipts. Existing funded text does not qualify funded image/video/render paths.

## Pricing Hypothesis

Convenience, curated distribution and optional managed services are the existing value model. No Device Studio price, credit conversion, watermark, resolution paywall, local export quota or hosted-render tariff is proposed. Display authoritative account balances/units and quote/rate versions; unknown usage remains pending.

## Validation Plan

Test optional scoped account linking and account-bound execution; signed-out/offline local use; BYOK selection; insufficient credit refusal; cancel/retry/restart/replay; unknown-cost holds and no silent paid fallback. The whole parent creation budget includes all child agents/calls. Refuse paid dispatch without an enforceable upper bound. Qualify actual download entitlement separately from funded service use. Live spend needs real configured cap/credentials.

## Current Status

Research identified browser callback mismatch, scoped lease-to-spend gap, missing full account surface and usage replay counterexample. No product monetization is implemented or price published. Upstream bugs remain with their owners; see [PROBLEMS.md](../internal/PROBLEMS.md).

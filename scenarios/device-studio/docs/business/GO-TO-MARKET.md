# Go to market — Device Studio

## Purpose Of This Document

Record positioning and qualification before any public launch. This is a proposed product strategy, not permission to publish, message customers or activate an offer.

## Audience And Positioning

Serve app developers, independent makers and product teams that need authentic screen demonstrations within polished device scenes. Promise repeatable shot creation and clear creative control; preserve actual capture authenticity and identify simulated chrome/generated hardware where relevant.

## Channels

Use existing marketing-crew/launch-video and normal bundle discovery once qualified. Candidate examples include product demos and before/after reference comparisons. Do not promise every model, device motion or OS based on schema coverage. No new campaign or outbound message is authorized here.

## Launch Motion

Demonstrate a real saved-project photographic workflow, true phone spin, laptop close and broad representative library before calling the scenario complete. Follow with clean desktop install/offline/account jobs for each offered platform. Curated downloadable delivery uses existing release/commerce owners. A useful early photo milestone can be shown as such without advertising full-motion completion.

## Messaging

Describe photographic fixed-view and 3D physical-motion routes plainly. Explain free asset sourcing, optional cloud/BYOK costs and project privacy. Do not imply generated scenes prove app execution on a pictured device. Avoid speed claims until measured on qualified hardware.

## Validation Experiments

Observe whether a new user can import capture, choose a compatible scene/device, understand AI/cost prompts, render and reopen a shot without expert help. Review output at final viewing size. Measure time-to-first-useful-shot and task success with retained protocol/context, not ungrounded conversion predictions. Route pricing research through the established monetization owner.

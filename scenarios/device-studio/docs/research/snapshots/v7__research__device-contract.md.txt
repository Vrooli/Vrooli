# Device Studio: generalized contracts, creation and free-asset policy

Research assessment, 2026-10-19. This is a proposed contract and release policy, not a new scenario, qualified library or implementation. Scope: read the v7 research brief, living device-domain design, sourced inventories and acquired-file evidence; refresh material primary sources; retain small counterexamples. No assets purchased, accounts created, provider generation invoked, packages installed, services changed or production code written.

## Recommendation

Keep the backend-neutral product → definition → asset → instance → display/content → shot split. It already survives the requested families at the conceptual level. Before implementation, make capability negotiation and **qualification for a specific use** explicit. A schema can describe a folding display without any installed renderer or released asset supporting that motion. A portable GLB can show a device while remaining unsuitable for final video, macro views or articulation.

The first vertical slice can start with the photographic-shot flow and two tightly scoped 3D qualification candidates: the acquired slab phone and already-open laptop. Neither is production qualified today. This is a sequencing recommendation, not a reduction of the requested scenario or a proposal to call static hardware a complete first release. The complete release target must include controlled phone rotation and laptop lid opening/closure, with actual rig and temporal qualification, plus representative broader device coverage. Use foldable/dual-screen, circular watch, wall display and embedded-host contract fixtures immediately, then qualify their actual visual assets. The design's current narrow laptop shot schema must remain experimental, not silently become the public contract.

The existing architecture and limitations are documented in [device-domain.md:19](/home/matthalloran8/.vrooli/evidence/device-presentation-2026-10-18/v2/device-domain.md:19), [topology:115](/home/matthalloran8/.vrooli/evidence/device-presentation-2026-10-18/v2/device-domain.md:115), [creation:143](/home/matthalloran8/.vrooli/evidence/device-presentation-2026-10-18/v2/device-domain.md:143) and its v6/readiness additions. Those are design/evidence records, not runtime proof.

## Primary-source identity checks

**iPhone Duo: independently verified as a documented product in the current source environment.** Apple's [technical specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/) supply open W164.6 × H117.8 × D5.2 mm and folded W84.1 × H117.8 × D11.3 mm, separate inner/outer panels, and rounded-corner diagonal qualifications. The page lists inner 1878-by-2670 and outer 1398-by-2034 pixel resolutions. Preserve the manufacturer's tuple/orientation rather than infer texture rotation from the numbers. These facts establish product identity and named states; they do not establish a free artist mesh's accuracy, hinge geometry, bend radius or implementation compatibility. A dated HTML snapshot and SHA256 are retained in `contracts/apple-duo.html` and `contracts/source-audit.json`. An older asset title remains independent evidence and cannot inherit this verification.

**Fold6 and a round watch expose distinct topology.** Samsung specifies Fold6 H153.5 × W132.6 × D5.6 mm open and H153.5 × W68.1 × D12.1 mm folded, with a thickness measurement qualifier. Record both state and exclusions. [Samsung Fold6 specs](https://www.samsung.com/ph/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold6/specs/) and [Android FoldingFeature](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/window/layout/FoldingFeature) distinguish the continuous fold from an occluding hinge between physical panels. Samsung's [Watch7 source](https://news.samsung.com/sg/samsung-galaxy-watch-ultra-and-galaxy-watch7-empowering-everyday-wellness-for-all) distinguishes 40/44 mm body variants and 432×432/480×480 rasters. A square raster does not make the visible display rectangular, and a marketed case size is not its active diameter.

**Wall display and vehicle claims need host-specific evidence.** The exact Canadian Samsung UN55DU8000FXZC [specification](https://www.samsung.com/ca/tvs/uhd-4k-tv/du8000-55-inch-crystal-uhd-4k-tizen-os-smart-tv-un55du8000fxzc/) reports W1232.1 × H708.8 × D25.7 mm without stand versus W1232.1 × H747.8 × D228.8 mm with stand, and a 200×200 mm VESA pattern. That does not supply every bracket, fastener or cable-clearance requirement. BMW's [2022 i4 primary release](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/canada/article/detail/T0334246EN/the-new-2022-bmw-i4-edrive40-and-i4-m50) describes 12.3-inch information and 14.9-inch control displays under common curved glass, with concealed support. It does not establish exact active-panel curvature, raster mapping, an app's access to the cluster, or a generic mount. Google's [Android Auto layout documentation](https://developers.google.com/cars/design/android-auto/design-system/layout) bases layout breakpoints on the app window. A software ecosystem is neither a hardware size nor permission to paint every host display. Android Automotive OS and Android Auto must remain separate identities.

Raw retrieval succeeded for five source snapshots, including Apple Duo and both creator APIs. Several other direct HTTP requests returned 403 while the browser-backed research tool could read their primary pages; these failures remain in `source-audit.json`. No access controls were bypassed. The Sony alternate TV page was not sufficiently stable for extracting its mount facts, so this report uses the verified Samsung SKU instead.

## Contract stress test

| Case | Preserve in the contract | Failure the compiler/release gate must expose |
|---|---|---|
| Continuous folding display | Stable part graph, joint coordinates/limits, evaluated deforming screen mesh, continuous UV domain, outer/inner surface IDs, explicit layout-state events | Four-corner screen mapping while bending; stretching a portrait capture into a new responsive layout; treating the cover and inner source clocks as one implicit track |
| Two rigid screens | Independent active masks and rasters; explicit logical canvas viewports; hinge gap/occlusion; per-panel orientation | Painting the hinge; inventing missing source pixels; presenting a physical gap as a single continuous panel |
| Circular watch | Circular physical active mask separate from glass and app-safe region; case/strap/lug frames; variant and wrist attachment | Rectangular source corners on the bezel; unreadable content accepted because it mathematically fits; case diameter used as display diameter |
| Wall TV | Exact SKU and stand state; device → adapter/bracket → wall interface chain; clearance and pose; hidden bracket offset | `wall_mountable=true` treated as compatibility; unknown fasteners treated as a pass; an arbitrary regional variant's stand bounds reused |
| Embedded vehicle | Host assembly, trim and steering-wheel occluders; active panels versus glass; system UI versus permitted app viewport; model/year/market/capture provenance | A generic “CarPlay display” size; app content over cluster gauges; hidden support interpreted as floating geometry; marketing image treated as vehicle-execution evidence |
| Curved/deforming surface | Geometry/UV reference with deformation representation, supported state interval and sampling; local target when screen entry is appropriate | Global planar homography; camera entry into an imaginary plane; assuming curvature from the name “Curved Display” |
| Projector, HUD, VR/AR boundary | A distinct presentation target with receiver geometry or viewer/per-eye optical mapping | Treating housing dimensions as apparent image dimensions or mapping content to the headset shell |

These are proposed semantic gates. Ten explicit counterexamples are retained in [domain-counterexamples.json](contracts/domain-counterexamples.json); they are not renderer requests or successful end-to-end device demonstrations. Unknown evidence yields `needs-review`; a proven mismatch or unsupported requested operation yields `incompatible`. A physically supported recipe can still fail readability or craft acceptance. Installation results are visual-scene compatibility, not engineering installation certification.

## What to make explicit before publishing the generalized schema

1. **Identity and revision closure.** Stable IDs for parts, joints, surfaces, mounts and content bindings; immutable referenced revisions and dependency hashes. Rename/adaptation maps are explicit. A changed screen primitive or UV chart invalidates its binding rather than resolving by a coincidental mesh/material name. The acquired phone's screen-name annulus is concrete evidence that names alone fail.
2. **Geometry and evidence are separate.** Keep measured facts and their sources/uncertainty distinct from renderable surface geometry. Unknown bend radius or manufacturer active area can coexist with a provisional mesh, but the mesh is an estimate with a restricted qualification claim. Units, handedness, origin, local frames, configuration, measurement exclusions and source precision remain first-class.
3. **Display topology and mapping.** Surface geometry supports planar and mesh-backed variants; masks, cutouts, UV orientation, glass occlusion and app-safe regions are separate. A logical display maps explicit source viewports to physical surfaces. Define gap behavior (`excluded-source-region`, `separate-layouts`, or declared approximation) rather than an ambiguous “span.” Require actual capture/layout state where native responsiveness is claimed. Unknown future surface kinds should fail with a clear capability reason, not be coerced into a rectangle.
4. **Independent clocks and state.** Output sampling, hardware/camera motion, authentic source timestamps, simulated chrome time and capture-layout transitions are separately identified. Define holds, trims, interpolation, frame boundaries and off/hidden screen policy. The compile output retains the exact resolved sample sequence; no renderer decides its own layout handoff.
5. **Capability negotiation.** Compare requested features against the selected adapter: surface mapping, deformation, joints, camera entry, occlusion, supported material/color path and delivery format. Representation, preview availability, final rendering support, and asset qualification are distinct fields. An unsupported physical motion cannot silently become a 2D crop.
6. **Scoped qualification.** A verdict binds asset/dependency digest, adaptation, renderer/profile, output scale/maximum hardware and screen footprint, permitted view angles/configurations, motion envelope, scene/placement assumptions, evidence and reviewer. Express excluded macro/underside/closed/hinge views. Cache/qualification invalidation follows the affected dependency; changing a shot camera requires checking scope, not automatically discarding every unrelated license fact.

GLB is an interchange artifact. [The glTF 2.0 specification](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html) defines meshes, skins, morph targets and animation mechanisms; it does not define this scenario's hardware measurement, mount or capture claims. Preserve native editing/render sources and test exported target behavior. Neither an exported animation list nor a filename proves a qualified physical rig. Existing v4/v5 evidence already shows that a clean native render can become a portable material/depth-fighting problem in a larger scene.

### Vertical slice versus complete first release

The mandatory motion target is not satisfied by screen-only video or a static portable GLB. The current laptop needs separated rigid lid/base parts, a measured hinge/pivot, supported endpoints/intermediate poses, collision checks and a real opening/closing sample; preserve its current open pose as a useful candidate while this work is done. Phone rotation needs qualified rear/side geometry, changing reflections, temporal aliasing and sustained readable proof intervals. These are release work, not optional polish.

Recommend a realistic complete first-release catalog of six presentation representatives: slab phone, laptop, foldable/dual-screen assembly, round watch, wall-mounted display and embedded vehicle host. Give each a released visual recipe and explicit permitted state/view/motion envelope. Qualify rotation and lid motion on the core pair; the other representatives can begin with stationary or narrowly bounded recipes when that is their actual requested use. A watch does not need strap physics to qualify a stationary studio shot. A foldable's static open state must not advertise animated folding; if animated folding is required for the release, that adds a separate deformation/rig gate. Accurate branded assets or explicitly labeled generic fallbacks follow the user's existing choice, not a new ambiguity.

The research burden rises by capability: round masks are relatively narrow; wall displays add real attachment/clearance evidence; embedded hosts add trim, occlusion and app-window provenance; continuous fold animation adds deformation, handoff and temporal checks. These are qualitative dependency comparisons, not measured delivery-time estimates. All six contract fixtures passing would still not substitute for six usable assets/recipes. Broad final visual coverage and exact physical articulation remain tracked deliverables until qualified.

## A coherent free, commercially usable policy

Separate access price from permissions. The default catalog should admit zero-purchase assets only, while tracking whether lawful acquisition needs an existing account. No new account, payment, token or viewer-asset extraction is implied by an asset lead. Free local tooling does not make CPU time or external generation free.

Use two rights profiles. **Bundled/editable library** assets must permit commercial output, modification and redistribution of their actual model/texture dependency closure. Prefer CC0 and permit CC BY 4.0 with complete attribution. **Project-local/render-only** assets can have narrower redistribution terms when commercially usable and explicitly approved for that project; exclude them from shared downloadable GLB packs and template exports that expose the original. This distinction prevents a useful render license from being mistaken for reusable scenario content.

Primary license findings:

- [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) permits licensed sharing/adaptation subject to its conditions. Retain creator/source/license notices and identify changes in an appropriate delivery credit record. It does not grant patent/trademark rights or endorsement. Keep attribution with model exports and downstream media/project delivery; do not rely solely on editable GLB metadata that a movie cannot display.
- [CC0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en) waives covered rights as specified but retains important limitations, including trademark/patent and third-party rights. Keep provenance even where attribution is not required.
- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) expressly permits commercial use and redistribution of its CC0 asset files. Its page separately excludes site/example-render imagery from that grant and has access/API terms. Do not infer that every preview is a redistributable stock image or that asset licensing overrides acquisition rules.
- [Blendkit](https://www.blendkit.com/docs/licenses/) distinguishes commercially usable higher-level work from reselling its royalty-free assets in the same form, including asset packs. Therefore “royalty free” and “free plan” are insufficient for the bundled/editable profile. Exact terms must cover the intended project/export; CC0 is the simpler packaging route.

Recommended default exclusions: NC licenses for commercial use, ND where adaptation is needed, editorial-only assets, unknown/no license, ripped/rehosted files without a consistent origin, and misleading free preview geometry that only advertises a paid model. Share-alike and custom licenses are not inherently noncommercial; route them to explicit compatibility review rather than an incorrect legal rejection. Current Watch10 catalog rejection (four reported faces plus paid-model links) remains a sourcing warning, not evidence of a usable free full watch. The old inventories and vendor thumbnails remain leads, not newly qualified files.

Each dependency records source/author, original bytes/hash, acquired date/access method, exact license evidence/version and optional license-file hash, attribution/change notice, permitted operations and unresolved rights. Operations include commercial rendering, adapting, bundling the source, exporting an editable scene, publishing preview imagery and sending references/assets to an external AI provider. These permissions must not be compressed into `commercial_use=true`. Screenshots, wallpapers, fonts, logos and photographs have their own provenance; an artist's model license is not blanket permission over every depicted brand or third-party image. Provider-upload terms need review independently of local reference use. None were exercised in this task.

## Import/adaptation and AI creation lifecycle

Retain the design's shared qualification path for imported and authored assets, with these practical checkpoints:

1. Resolve exact product/variant or explicit generic design; pin dimension evidence, usable references, rights profile and intended shot scope. An unresolved title stays unresolved. Reference images can guide local review without becoming default bundled content.
2. Acquire original bytes and complete dependencies through an authorized public path; preserve the untouched source. Preflight formats/extensions, archive paths, size/resource limits and required conversion. Disable imported-script auto-execution. Conversions retain tool/version/input/output hashes and original attribution.
3. Decide import, adaptation or original construction with reasons. Clone an immutable candidate revision; record axis/unit/origin conversion and named part/surface maps. Detect actual screen coverage, not material names. Preserve useful artist materials before replacing shaders; different renderer requirements create explicit adapters.
4. Fit camera/geometry against evidence with known dimensions locked. Store calibrated comparisons, held-out views and honest unknowns. Separate geometry correction from lighting/material improvement. A beauty render is not a dimension test.
5. Let AI propose bounded edits; deterministic owned tools execute them in an isolated workspace. Preserve prompts/model identity when available, source code/edits, budgets, logs, checkpoints and rejected candidates. Resource limits alone are not isolation. Network/download sessions and credentials stay outside the execution workspace. Cancellation terminates the owned work and retains a resumable candidate without promoting it.
6. Compare the candidate against predeclared use criteria. Model, source or renderer changes invalidate affected checks. End as candidate-ready, needs-input, budget-exhausted, failed or cancelled. **Candidate-ready is not released.** Never optimize a score by changing ground truth, dropping difficult landmarks, hiding an incorrect hinge or substituting a generated photograph for the geometry under test.
7. Release only a reviewed package and its permitted usage scope. Native sources, portable exports, display mappings, parameter ledger, dependency/rights closure, render recipes and review evidence stay separately identifiable. A generated photographic plate can pass appearance review without inheriting geometry fidelity; plate replay and nondeterministic generation are separate operations.

These are proposed behavior and acceptance requirements, not a claim that a production agent sandbox or autonomous model-creation service is available. Existing experiment source and receipts support feasibility of specific steps only.

## Release invariants and remaining decisions

A released asset revision must have resolved identity class, valid immutable references, rights/dependency closure, measured or explicitly estimated geometry, qualified masks/UV/orientation, deterministic clock mapping, capability-compatible requested motion and an explicit placement mode. Check complete intended motion and renderer/export target, not only a hero frame. Review at maximum intended screen/hardware footprint; preserve readable required regions, corner/cutout behavior, color transforms and source authenticity. Numeric thresholds derive from source uncertainty and shot purpose, not a fabricated universal millimeter tolerance. Professional craft acceptance and operator approval are separate recorded gates. Current rejected v5 scene craft remains rejected despite useful portability/support proofs.

Before implementation, the parent decision packet should resolve only preferences that alter the target:

| Preference | Options | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Complete first-release catalog and motion scope | A: six representatives, phone rotation/laptop lid motion, bounded declared recipes elsewhere; B: moving core pair first, broader visual catalog in the next release; C: all representatives with full articulation/deformation where relevant | A; makes broad coverage concrete while keeping its unqualified motions explicit. B is smaller but defers requested breadth; C adds substantial rig/deformation and evidence work. This is a release-scope decision, separate from the narrow first vertical slice. |
| AI creation authoring style | A: guided reference/import choice with bounded automated candidate refinement; B: unattended budgeted search/model/refinement ending at human review; C: manual import with AI critique only | A initially; retain B in the target contract. C would omit part of the explicitly requested creation capability if treated as the whole release. |

Free model sourcing and accurate-versus-explicitly-labeled-generic fallback are already settled user constraints, not questions. The proposed shared-library default is CC0 plus properly attributed CC BY, with explicit rights checks for every actual dependency. Current no-new-account/no-spend constraints remain in force. External provider choice/upload permissions belong in the consolidated integration decision packet only if they are still unresolved; local creation/import must remain viable independently.

No user preference can waive a source license or turn unknown geometry into a verified claim. Existing user instructions already settle zero purchases and the need for AI creation capability; do not ask them to reconsider those as open questions.

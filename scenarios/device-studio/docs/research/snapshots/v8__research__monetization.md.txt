# Device Studio: adoption of Vrooli monetization

Investigation, 2026-10-19. Based on the accepted v8 decisions, monetization plan of record, engineering contracts and current owner code. **Implemented** below means the mechanism is present in reviewed code; it does not mean a live payment, installed desktop purchase or Device Studio journey was executed. No production source, catalog, prices, credentials, accounts, provider spend or services were changed. One out-of-scope source-level defect was reported through the required writer.

## Recommendation

Adopt normal Vrooli commerce: paid downloadable bundle distribution and optional Vrooli-funded services, alongside working local and BYOK paths. Device Studio owns shots, creation/render jobs, their budgets and receipts; it must not own a new account system, credit wallet, checkout, price ladder or subscription database. Download entitlement, commercial account linkage, local capability authority and per-operation funding are separate decisions.

Do not make every render a credit purchase. Local composition, editing, importing, portable export and local Blender execution do not incur Vrooli-funded provider charges. BYOK may incur the user's provider bill, but should not also debit Vrooli credits for those same provider units. Vrooli-funded inference or future hosted rendering/storage must be authorized and metered at the trusted owner before consuming its resources. An optional commercial service must not become an automatic paid fallback after a local failure.

This follows the strategy's open-source/local/BYOK posture, not a new business model. [STRATEGY.md:23](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/docs/monetization/strategy/STRATEGY.md:23), [PAID_FEATURES.md:43](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/docs/concepts/PAID_FEATURES.md:43), [accepted decisions](decisions.json).

## Paid desktop distribution versus local capability

**Implemented delivery mechanism:** an LPBS delivery asset has `RequiresEntitlement`. The authorizer first resolves the exact bundle/app/platform asset and, when gated, requires a verified subscription status of active or trialing. Ungated assets skip that check. This is a server-side gate on acquiring that delivered product; it is not a command to charge for every export after installation. The reviewed policy checks status; do not assume it alone proves all desired per-app/bundle/account membership restrictions. Those must be exercised through the selected product's actual delivery composition. [authorizer.go:103](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/internal/delivery/authorizer.go:103), [authorizer.go:160](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/internal/delivery/authorizer.go:160).

**Existing examples:** Web Console and System Monitor both declare `requires_entitlement:false` for local scenario capability. Web Console declares routed `ai_credits` and BYOK; System Monitor has no meters. Web Console documents signed-out local terminal use and optional account UI. Copy the separation, not every legacy authentication detail. [web-console manifest:1](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/web-console/.vrooli/monetization.json:1), [system-monitor manifest:1](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/system-monitor/.vrooli/monetization.json:1), [Web Console MONETIZATION:3](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/web-console/docs/business/MONETIZATION.md:3).

**Proposed Device Studio adoption:** ordinary local editing/rendering remains useful without mandatory commercial sign-in; the curated official desktop download and convenience/service offering follow the declared catalog entitlement. Bundle inclusion and any truly differentiating gates come from the operator-curated owner process, not from an invented Device Studio rank check. Do not introduce resolution watermarks, export quotas, pay-per-local-frame billing or a perpetual license product merely because the engineering schema can express them.

The strategy's commercial delivery tiers are distinct from technical deployment tiers and from plan ranks. Desktop corresponds to commercial bundle-app delivery and technical desktop packaging, not a new subscription tier named “desktop.” Cross-platform architecture does not itself certify signed Windows/macOS/Linux delivery. ## Account linkage is not yet a complete desktop spending path

The project contract separates authenticator identity/local policy from LPBS business-account ownership. Current desktop linking returns a signed lease bound to principal, selected business account, installation, resource, audience, link ID and scopes. The desktop template's new flow redeems a scoped code and retains that lease; shared `VerifyFor`/`CachedFor` enforce binding and expiry. A lease's credit fields are a snapshot, not permission to mutate a private wallet. [PAID_FEATURES.md:13](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/docs/concepts/PAID_FEATURES.md:13), [desktop_link_entitlement.go:12](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/desktop_link_entitlement.go:12), [auth/manager.ts:338](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/scenario-to-desktop/templates/vanilla/auth/manager.ts:338), [lease.go:119](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/packages/entitlementclient-go/lease.go:119).

**Concrete integration gap:** the reviewed metered gateway client expects a consumer access-token context/resolver and calls LPBS `/api/v1/ai/inference`; LPBS reserves its inference/usage endpoints behind `requireUserAuth`. The linked entitlement lease is not established as that Bearer credential. Therefore a successful desktop link is not proof of funded inference from that installation. Coordinate the trusted, account-scoped spend/delegation adapter with the identity owner before promising the paid desktop journey. It must preserve the selected account and local binding rather than copy a website refresh token or infer ownership from email. This is required adapter work, not a reason to invent a replacement login or disable local use. [metered.go:122](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/ai-gateway/api/internal/providers/metered.go:122), [routes.go:724](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/routes.go:724), [routes.go:746](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/routes.go:746). ## Metering, budgets, cancellation and retries

**Implemented foundation:** LPBS usage endpoints derive identity from authenticated context. Reservation code uses transactional checks against usage and pending reservations; successful metered text calls reserve before execution and finalize with actual usage/cost metadata. Provider failure releases the hold. Finalization uses the reservation ID as an event key; a repeated finalized reservation is refused rather than silently charged twice. Release is a no-op for nonpending reservations. [usage routes:724](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/routes.go:724), [reservation_service.go:265](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/internal/commerce/reservation_service.go:265), [finalize:423](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/internal/commerce/reservation_service.go:423), [release:585](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/internal/commerce/reservation_service.go:585), [metered inference:169](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/internal/intelligence/metered_inference_service.go:169).

**Important limits:** a reservation estimate is not an application hard spending ceiling. Existing text policy can settle above the estimate, and provider/cancellation semantics for a long image, model-creation or render job are not established by that chat implementation. Current inference policy maps token usage to internal credit units using owner defaults; that is separate from provider-reported cost and from the retail offer price. Do not copy hardcoded model rates into Device Studio or treat their values as current provider benchmarks. [model_policy.go:22](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/internal/intelligence/model_policy.go:22), [CalculateCost:71](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/landing-page-business-suite/api/internal/intelligence/model_policy.go:71).

**Proposed Device Studio job contract:** before an AI/agent initiating action, disclose execution/funding source, parent/child scope, maximum iterations/concurrency, configured project spend/compute ceiling, media-egress policy and cancellation limits. No automatic paid escalation. Track immutable parent job, child attempts, upstream request/reservation/receipt IDs, quote/rate-policy version, reserved amount, known actual usage/cost, status and reconciliation outcome. Budget admission considers settled spend plus outstanding holds, including nested autonomous work; a UI estimate alone cannot enforce it.

- Cancel before dispatch: stop owned work and release the unconsumed hold through its owner.
- Cancel after dispatch: request upstream cancellation, retain the acknowledgment/receipt, and settle documented consumed work. A provider may still have performed billable work; do not promise an automatic full refund.
- Timeout/lost acknowledgment: mark cost/settlement pending or unknown, query the existing execution/status, and reconcile. Do not assume failure means zero cost or resubmit a non-idempotent paid request.
- Retry delivery/status/settlement with the same operation identity; create a distinct attempt only when genuinely executing additional work. Classify retryable transport/overload separately from insufficient credits, revoked account linkage, unsupported capabilities or exhausted budget.
- Resume rendering from verified deterministic checkpoints/cache entries. A cache hit does not incur a new provider execution charge, though an explicitly selected hosted storage/delivery service may have its own cost.

AI Gateway's inference validator may make two attempts and accumulates usage across them; an unsuccessful final result does not prove no work occurred. That behavior belongs inside the disclosed parent budget. [inference/service.go:62](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/scenarios/ai-gateway/api/internal/inference/service.go:62).

For optional Class B local-capacity reporting, shared durable outbox code preserves `OperationID` and retries asynchronously. It must not block offline local work or retro-bill a backlog. Default recommendation is no new local export/render quota; if the commercial owner later declares one, reuse the owner package rather than inventing it. Offline lease expiry controls only declared commercial affordances, not local project readability. [outbox.go:25](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/packages/monetization-go/outbox.go:25), [PAID_FEATURES.md:160](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/docs/concepts/PAID_FEATURES.md:160).

## Cost transparency and pricing policy

Keep provider cost, customer credit charge, local resource use and retail subscription price separate. Report unknown provider cost as **unknown/pending**, never zero; label estimates, measured values, currency/unit, rate provenance and timestamp. V7 already reported the image route's unknown-cost/parameter issues; do not duplicate those reports or assume a `cost_usd` field proves measurement. Reconcile customer usage against owner receipts and LPBS usage events; Money Ledger receives financial actuals through its own adapters. [v7 integration findings](../../v7/research/integrations.md).

The price policy already exists: Offer Desk's declared `sells_at` graph is authoritative; missing prices remain absent; operator decisions informed by benchmarks set commercial prices. Strategy says delivery tiers buy different delivery/convenience rather than a new scenario feature matrix; BYOK/self-hosting remains available. It discourages default solo-user seat proliferation and gives annual/add-on heuristics. These are strategic rules, not permission to announce a price, discount, trial or credit conversion. [PRICING.md:3](/home/matthalloran8/Vrooli/docs/monetization/strategy/PRICING.md:3).

Device Studio should show the actual selected account/plan/allowance and configured credit display units from LPBS, with a normal account/top-up path on insufficient credit. It should not hardcode a dollar-to-credit conversion or advertise a new rendering subscription. Exact retail price, Device Studio bundle membership and any hosted rendering tariff remain owner decisions, not required homework for this implementation design and not inferred from the accepted “normal monetization” request.

## Adoption and validation gates

1. Reuse normal account linking and commercial delivery; close and demonstrate the scoped desktop lease → trusted account-paid operation adapter with the identity owner. Verify account switching, wrong binding, unlink/revocation and expiry without losing local projects.
2. Declare actual funded operations and keep local/BYOK paths credit-free. Show AI/agent initiation, provider/funding source and bounded autonomous work before dispatch. Check consent for actual media egress independently of sign-in or funds.
3. Exercise reserve → owned execution → actual settlement for one supported funded capability with a configured test cap or mocks first. Refuse insufficient credit before work; do not silently fall back to another paid provider. No live paid test occurred here.
4. Inject cancel-before-start, cancel-after-consumption, timeout-after-provider-acceptance, process restart, duplicate delivery/settlement and budget exhaustion. Prove no blind paid resubmission, no double charge, explicit pending reconciliation, and correct receipt reuse. Include the new older-operation replay regression at its LPBS owner.
5. Test a legitimately entitled download separately from offline local work and authoritative funded services. A patched local lease must not obtain Vrooli-funded work; a missing LPBS connection must not prevent local composition/import/export. Run scoped `monetization-conformance` and relevant consumer/desktop journeys, not only static manifest checks.
6. Confirm source of pricing/display units and rights/cost of any optional cloud operation. Do not turn route availability into spend authority or private BYOK cost into platform credit usage.

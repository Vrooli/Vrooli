Phone rotation and laptop lid motion are required. Other assets advertise only qualified poses/motions. The broader 23-family research catalog remains the domain stress set and future expansion source; it is not a shipping library.

## New primary reference checks

[Apple's 11-inch M4 specification](https://support.apple.com/en-sa/119892) supplies 249.7 × 177.5 × 5.3 mm and a 2420 × 1668 raster. This is an additional variant, not the dimensions of the 13-inch source above. [Google's Nest Hub second-generation specification](https://store.google.com/ie/product/nest_hub_2nd_gen_specs?hl=en-GB) supplies 177.4 × 120.4 × 69.5 mm and 1024 × 600 raster. [Google's first-generation specification](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/7072284?hl=en-in) differs: 178.5 × 118 × 67.3 mm. This makes generation matching material, even where appearance is similar. These are manufacturer envelope/raster facts; active panel corners, bezel depth and support geometry still need reference calibration.

Additional search results were not promoted: the popular [DatSketch iPad Pro](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/apple-ipad-pro-e5ffb3c80b2d4d6690249f8ee2bdafbe) lists a noncommercial license; a [Free3D Fold](https://free3d.com/3d-model/samsung-galaxy-fold-642733.html) lists personal-use terms and its creator explicitly says it was made without blueprints and is not accurate. The free [Kenney furniture kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit) is CC0, but that alone does not meet this project's realistic art direction. [BlendSwap's mechanical dashboard](https://blendswap.com/blend/11857) is a CC-BY traditional gauge assembly, not a modern screen host. These findings support explicit source rejection reasons instead of presenting every free search result as usable.

## Finish condition

The eight groups need concrete reviewed assets/recipes, not just enum values or search links. Model qualification, scene craft and release permission are independent. For each group retain a photographic or full-3D final output at intended footprint, dimensions/reference fit, source/dependency ledger, screen profile and scope of supported poses/motion. Monitor versus wall TV and smart display versus digital frame need separate attachment/assembly cases even if their compiler primitives are shared. The first release must show broad usefulness; unusual optical devices and fully deforming fold motion remain explicitly outside that release's qualified-motion envelope unless separately completed.

The professional 3D scene work remains unresolved by this source refresh. It requires art direction, actual acquired materials, calibrated lighting/camera, believable contact/scale, and human review. More asset search cannot substitute for that implementation loop.

# Monetization

Tidiness Manager does not currently have a standalone monetization plan. Its immediate value is compound internal capability: agents use it to find and resolve maintainability debt across scenarios.

## Potential Future Packaging

- Engineering-health dashboard for scenario fleets.
- Maintainability campaign reporting for teams.
- Integration with issue trackers and CI/CD gates.

## Current Boundary

Do not optimize this scenario around monetization until the maintainability and campaign workflows are stable and validated.

# Monetization

Browser Automation Studio is both an internal end-to-end validation capability and a prospective deployable browser-automation product. The near-term commercial unit is reliable workflow creation, execution evidence, and replay/demo production—not an unbounded hosted browser service.

Before external deployment, establish tenant isolation, retention controls, authentication/authorization, capacity limits, billing enforcement, and operational SLOs. Pricing and packaging remain hypotheses until those controls and customer validation exist.

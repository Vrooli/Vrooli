# Go To Market — Extraction Range

## Audience and positioning

Current audience: Vrooli builders and tool authors who need to compare website-extraction approaches against known answers before using them on real targets. Position this as an internal capability that compounds F2 delivery quality, not as a proven commercial scraping product.

Supported claim: the ground has 40 levels in 10 task families, and the current F10 toolkit extracted 14,547 rows from three compliant public HTML targets. This is technical evidence; it does not establish an external market, production service level, or paid demand.

The operator has identified crowded proposal marketplaces as a high-risk primary channel and sees self-serve, convenient, search-visible software as a possible mitigation. That is portfolio direction, not authorization to launch or market this scenario. Extraction Range itself is a test capability; any future external extraction service or product needs a distinct buyer/use-case decision and review.

## Channels and launch motion

| Channel | Current role | Evidence / gate |
|---|---|---|
| Internal F2 review page | Lets the operator inspect source, hard-ground receipts, real targets, and limitations | Existing D2 decision remains pending; this evidence update is not a duplicate request |
| Self-hosted Vrooli/business bundle | Possible business depth-layer support for developer and solopreneur tools | No SKU mapping, external buyer validation, or pricing decision is claimed |
| Public SEO or hosted extraction service | Deferred | Requires a separately approved product scope, operating-cost analysis, and market validation |

1. Continue all six F2 categories at their full scope while capacity permits.
2. Keep K2 in `waiting-operator` until the already-open D2/scenario decisions are resolved; do not create a second ask.
3. After accepted category evidence, route any product/bundle decision through Offer Desk and operator-curated monetization canon.
4. Only after a separate approval, prepare public positioning and channel experiments. No prospect contact, external publication, or account creation is part of this work.

## Validation experiments

No external marketing experiment is authorized or needed for the current internal proving-ground objective. The active decision is the existing source-visible F10 review: inspect the client tool, 40-level hard ground, three real-site receipts, limits, and hours, then record the existing go/defer choice. External customer demand, hosted-service cost, search conversion, and category acceptance remain unvalidated.

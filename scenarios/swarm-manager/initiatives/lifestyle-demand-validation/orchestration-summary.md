# Lifestyle Demand Validation — Orchestration Summary

**Origin:** Morning Vision Walk, 2026-04-23. Split out from the routines-app initiative to respect the sequencing principle established in the walk: *validate demand before heavy primitive investment*.

**Why this exists:** The routines-app + lifestyle-bundle vision is architecturally coherent but unvalidated externally. TikTok cleaning-guide and Type-B-planner content suggests real demand for "just tell me what to do" products for home / cleaning / maintenance, but that signal has not been tested against Vrooli's specific capabilities or pricing.

Building the routines-app primitive first and *then* discovering demand doesn't exist wastes the primitive investment. Validating first, then scoping the primitive to what demand wants, is cheaper and produces a better primitive.

## Vision framing

### What is being validated

1. **Demand existence.** Are there real buyers for outcome-oriented cleaning / maintenance / inspection / repair guides (digital and physical)?
2. **Audience shape.** Who are they — Type-B planners, new homeowners, parents, renters, other? Knowing the audience shapes content priorities and future scenarios.
3. **Willingness to pay.** One-time purchase price points for a physical guide (e.g., a $15 print-on-demand book). Subscription willingness-to-pay comes later.
4. **Funnel hypothesis.** Does a physical-guide audience convert to SaaS interest, or are they two separate markets? The walk's hypothesis is the first; the test is whether that holds.

### What is NOT being validated

- The routines-app primitive itself (separate initiative).
- The full lifestyle-bundle pricing (premature).
- Other revenue lines (affiliate, subscription); those have their own activation disciplines.

### Test shape (menu; final shape scoped by research sub-item)

- **Cheapest:** landing page with email capture for a described physical guide, drive small paid traffic, measure sign-up rate and willingness-to-pay signals.
- **Mid:** publish 1–2 actual print-on-demand guides on Amazon KDP under a branded imprint, measure organic discovery and conversion.
- **Deeper:** content-first approach — publish short-form cleaning / maintenance content on TikTok / YouTube Shorts in the voice of the intended audience, measure engagement and build an audience before offering product.

The research sub-item scopes which of these (or combination) to run given current capacity.

### Inputs

- Early curated markdown routines authored in the routines-app v0 are the natural source material. Physical guides are a packaging / formatting pass over the same content. Content does not need to be invented for this track — it needs to be authored once and reused.

### Decision criteria

Validation "pass" requires at least one of:

- Measurable paid conversion on a physical guide at margin that exceeds author-cost.
- Organic audience signal strong enough to justify a dedicated content channel (clear engagement traction over N weeks).
- Email-list growth rate strong enough to support a launch.

Validation "fail" means the physical-guide-first hypothesis was wrong; routines-app still has intrinsic value for the operator, but the bundle's consumer-product revenue line stays `candidate` and the primitive's scope is revisited.

## Relationship to other initiatives

- **Runs parallel to routines-app.** Not a blocker, not a dependent. They share content.
- **Informs revenue-line activation.** The consumer-products revenue line in `docs/monetization/revenue-lines/consumer-products.md` has an activation trigger tied partially to signal this initiative produces.
- **Does not gate inventory-app or contact-book-plus.** Those have their own utility independent of demand validation.

## Monetization alignment

- Any monetizable output of this track (e.g., a published book that earns real revenue) is tracked under the `consumer-products` revenue line once that line activates.
- Until then, revenue from this track is treated as a validation signal, not a business.

## Risks

- **Vanity metrics.** TikTok engagement and email list size are easy to optimize into meaninglessness. Decision criteria must center on **paid conversion** or **sustained audience signal**, not follower counts.
- **Content quality shortcuts.** Cheap AI-generated content in a trust-centered niche destroys the brand faster than it builds audience. Quality bar is high; this isn't a farm-content test.
- **Over-investment in infrastructure before proof.** A landing page is enough for v0 of validation. Resist building a "proper commerce stack" before there's anything to sell.

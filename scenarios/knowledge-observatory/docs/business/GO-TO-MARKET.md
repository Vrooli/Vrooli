# Go To Market

## Audience And Positioning

Primary audience: Vrooli operators and enterprise maintainers who need proof
that local knowledge and scenario documentation remain healthy.

## Channels

Initial channel is bundled platform capability. External positioning should
follow after repeated internal use proves measurable maintenance savings.

## Messaging

Knowledge Observatory makes Vrooli inspectable: agents and operators can see
what the system knows, where documentation is drifting, and which repairs are
needed.

## Validation Experiments

Measure before/after time for scenario onboarding, documentation repair, and
knowledge discovery tasks.

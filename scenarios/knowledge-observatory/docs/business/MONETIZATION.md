# Monetization

## Role In Vrooli

Knowledge Observatory is primarily an internal platform capability. It improves
scenario quality, agent orientation, and operational visibility across the
system.

## Packaging

Potential packaging paths include an enterprise observability add-on for local
Vrooli installations and an internal quality gate for monetizable scenarios.

## Pricing Hypothesis

No standalone pricing is established. Commercial value is currently indirect:
reduced maintenance cost, faster scenario generation, and stronger customer
trust in deployed scenario quality.

## Validation Plan

Track whether documentation-health and knowledge-search workflows reduce agent
handoff time and scenario repair time.

[P00005 | 977:1016 | HEADING_1]
Begin with the reader and the evidence

[P00006 | 1016:1354 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Open the project entry and the current commission. Recover the original owner request and amendments, rather than relying on a producer summary. Identify the intended reader, the problem worth their attention, the useful result and the delivery surface. Keep the article’s public reading path separate from the private production record.

[P00007 | 1354:1838 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Write a short angle decision before drafting. Name the audience’s current question, why this article is useful now, and what the author can credibly add. Compare a timely angle with an evergreen one when relevant. Look for a real experience, result or unresolved tension that differentiates this piece from generic advice. Verify a claimed launch or trend; the owner’s recent access does not establish universal availability or popularity. Evergreen subjects do not need a news hook.

[P00008 | 1838:2389 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Collect the narrow source set needed for the argument. Distinguish the owner’s reported experience, source-code or document observations, checked behavior, a hypothetical example and a proposed design. Keep proof limits beside the affected claim. A plan, assistant answer or finished run is not evidence of deployed behavior or time saved. When an example includes actions, trace permission consistently from its proposal through its later decisions. Read-only review, executing a test, AI interpretation and human-user evidence are different scopes.

[P00009 | 2389:2815 | NORMAL_TEXT]
For current product claims, read official sources and record the observed date, rollout scope and uncertainty. Use public discussion only where it reveals a useful audience question, and label anecdotes rather than treating them as adoption evidence. Do not promise engagement. Keep private data and assistant internals out of public copy; a shareable observed case can retain its useful facts without exposing those details.

[P00010 | 2815:2865 | HEADING_1]
Make the argument work before polishing sentences

[P00011 | 2865:3440 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Before drafting, write the story in one plain sentence: the outcome the author wanted, the observed friction that made it difficult, and the useful understanding the reader will gain. Read the title and opening without the commission or project history. They must establish a recognizable situation, a consequential unresolved question and a credible reason to continue before introducing the method. A launch reference, fluent paragraph or comprehensible summary is not a hook by itself. Never invent a dramatic scene, emotion or successful result to supply missing stakes.

[P00012 | 3440:4034 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Develop one concrete case with a consequential choice. Show the actual handoff, before-and-after passage or observed result that carries the argument; label hypothetical material before it can be mistaken for experience. Give each section a causal job in the same story: what difficulty follows, what was tried or proposed, what evidence changed the understanding, and why that matters next. If sections read as independent tips, repair their connection before polishing transitions. Move internal test machinery to the companion when it distracts, retaining the evidence and its limits there.

[P00013 | 4034:4322 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Use first person only for experiences and opinions the owner actually expressed. Where published voice samples are absent, use the owner’s explicit corrections and relevant authored messages cautiously. Generated earlier drafts are material to diagnose, not approved examples to imitate.

[P00014 | 4322:4803 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Check the payoff against the opening promise. The ending should resolve the reader’s question as far as the evidence allows and leave them with a specific judgment or action they can apply. An honest unresolved experiment can pay off with a sharper test; it cannot imply that the desired system already works. Keep useful specificity, plain language and clear contributions. Remove procedural clutter, invented metrics, generic AI claims, repeated slogans and ornamental contrast.

[P00015 | 4803:4841 | HEADING_1]
Develop the images with the narrative

[P00016 | 4841:5288 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Assign each visual a reading job. A cover should make the article’s specific subject and central tension recognizable; a diagram should clarify a relationship, choice or recovery path. For an existing product, inspect actual current official product, UI or brand images before designing. Record their source, what they depict and whether their date is known. If depicting this dot, inspect its current avatar rather than inventing its appearance.

[P00017 | 5288:5877 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Create distinct cover directions using the verified references as visual inputs where relevant. Compare the actual outputs with those references for recognizable shape, color, character features and context. Do not call a generic loop on-brand merely because its prompt names the product. Use image generation for raster illustration; preserve exact diagrams, geometry or text in editable vector form where appropriate. Keep original sources, editable masters, useful alt text and captions. Label conceptual art and synthetic UI; do not fabricate product screenshots or imply endorsement.

[P00018 | 5877:6177 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Place the selected cover and inline images in the complete article. Check the sequence at the real reading width. Test a narrow composition when it affects legibility; a reduced landscape chart may need a separate stacked version. A mockup or conceptual illustration must not imply working software.

[P00019 | 6177:6644 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Keep images and claims aligned across variants. Verify actors, labels, arrows, branch conditions and captions as carefully as sentences. For process illustrations, check whether the drawing accidentally turns concurrent or event-driven work into a serial dependency. Distinguish work awaiting permission from useful preparation already permitted; a wait branch needs a reason and a resumption event. Apply these timing checks only where the topic involves a process.

Adapt only for a useful destination

[P00021 | 6680:7111 | NORMAL_TEXT]

X serves founder discovery and conversation; a short useful observation or thread should stand alone. LinkedIn is appropriate when engineering judgment helps prospective clients or hiring readers evaluate the owner. A full article belongs in the project’s durable publishing plan, but private preparation does not authorize publication.

[P00022 | 7111:7367 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Change the opening, amount of context, image crop and next action to fit the destination. Preserve the same factual boundaries. Do not require identical cross-posts, a fixed cadence or every channel. Keep X link-only when the project prohibits opening it.

Render a private text-and-image preview, check reading order and clipping, and state which native platform behaviors were not tested. A local preview is evidence about that composition, not the live feed, actual link treatment or phone-native behavior.

[P00024 | 7620:7674 | HEADING_1]
Use independent review to improve the actual delivery

[P00025 | 7674:8249 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Keep source validation and story evaluation distinct. The source-aware reviewer checks the request, factual experience, permission boundaries and evidence limits. The article-only reviewer first reads the title and opening and states the promised benefit, stakes and reason to continue; after the full article, they explain its causal thread and whether the ending delivers that benefit. Then check comprehension, prose and images separately. A clarity pass must not be reported as evidence that the hook works, that readers will engage, or that the owner accepts the story.

[P00026 | 8249:8648 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Ask where interest weakens and which section feels unearned, as well as what is confusing. Require a passage-level explanation and a concrete repair, not a generic score or approval. Inspect the actual assembled destination and recheck the changed opening, bridges, ending and layouts. A reviewer who helped write the revision can provide an informed recheck, but is no longer a fresh reader of it.

[P00027 | 8648:8953 | NORMAL_TEXT]
For each consequential finding, record the passage or asset, what the reviewer observed, its consequence and the disposition: corrected and rechecked; remaining limitation beside the affected claim; or no change with a concrete reason. A completed checklist or reviewer agreement is not owner acceptance.

[P00028 | 8953:8987 | HEADING_1]
Make feedback change the next use

[P00029 | 8987:9211 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Classify feedback as an observed defect, preference, new requirement or unresolved question. Preserve the owner’s words and the target version. Separate what was reported from what was reproduced and from a suspected cause.

[P00030 | 9211:9560 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Correct the authorized artifact, then decide independently whether its local method needs a change. If sound guidance was not followed, improve the point of use or the check; do not add another generic rule by default. Keep a preference local unless the owner generalizes it. Shared capability changes require their own applicability and authority.

[P00031 | 9560:10135 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Keep the current guide discoverable from the marketing entry, with its revision and test limits. Give a fresh writer that entry and a different suitable topic, asking for a short plan, an opening and one rendered visual in context. Preserve neutral scope, permission and resource limits in the handoff, including whether delegation is permitted; withhold expected answers and known diagnosis, not these constraints. Check whether the writer found the current guide, applied relevant lessons and retained strengths. This is a bounded transfer test, not a second full article.

[P00032 | 10135:10502 | NORMAL_TEXT]
If the same weakness recurs, repair the method or its use and repeat the affected check. When the owner reviews the article, apply substantive corrections and recheck the relevant part of the reuse example. Until then, call the owner-feedback portion pending. One successful use supports one case; reduced owner effort or mature production requires further evidence.

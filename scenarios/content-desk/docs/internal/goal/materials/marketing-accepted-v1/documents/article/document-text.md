Dots are designed for ongoing responsibilities. [OpenAI’s product guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot) describes giving your dot a goal, setting boundaries, and receiving work and decisions for review. My experiment is about making that arrangement concrete in my own work. I haven’t established that it runs reliably over time.

## Craft versus the original C references

The core C direction survives: warm ivory, cobalt rules and primary actions, dark type, restrained red, and serif prose inside compact application chrome. Current Review is especially strong: exact content can be read comfortably, the queue is clearly separate, the evidence remains next to the decision, and authority copy no longer treats r2 as an unsaved proposal. Plan reads as a calendar; Create as a writer's workspace; Learn as an evidence ledger. These would not become interchangeable pages merely by swapping titles.

Today is cleaner and more literal than the original textured C poster. Its flat synthetic artifact treatment loses some of the original print-like richness, but gains a legible relation to the source and avoids passing decorative artwork off as product evidence. M21 demonstrates that real permitted source imagery can recover richer visual character without forcing generation or decorative art onto operational pages. This is a deliberate tradeoff, not evidence that the flat preview is the only permissible art direction.

The operational specimens are generally sparse and easy to scan at full intended size. Some would benefit later from richer working controls, such as an inspectable source pin during intake or actual field/dropdown affordances in mapping and filtering. The current images establish composition and state semantics, not exhaustive interaction design for every listed control.

No unexpected clipping, overlapping controls, missing glyph boxes or accidental text truncation was found in the final set. Intentional abbreviated titles, such as the article row in Content, are supported by open-record routes. Phone type, source passages, decision buttons and bottom navigation remain readable at the intended 390-pixel width. Article, thread and storyboard do not have complete phone editing specimens in this set; their responsive behavior is therefore still a documented implementation/design-validation obligation rather than demonstrated parity.

## Color and delivery limits

Using the actual specified sRGB colors, the sampled light-theme ratios are approximately 6.05:1 for muted text on ivory, 8.70:1 for cobalt on ivory, and 6.39:1 for red on ivory. For the dark specimen, main text on background is 15.65:1, muted text on background 9.98:1, muted text on the source rail 8.71:1, main text on the selection fill 9.74:1, and the primary button pair 9.67:1. These pair checks support the observed readability; they are not a complete WCAG audit of every pixel or state.

**Native integration still needs its own exact-export check.** A full 1440-unit desktop compressed to a 6.5-inch/468-point document width turns a 14-unit label into roughly 4.55 points. Full desktops should serve as orientation figures, with readable focused crops and native explanatory text for consequential controls. The current high-resolution PNGs cannot establish that the eventual embedded dossier or PDF is readable. The exact exported native PDF, atlas links and final figure sizes were outside this lane and have not been passed here.

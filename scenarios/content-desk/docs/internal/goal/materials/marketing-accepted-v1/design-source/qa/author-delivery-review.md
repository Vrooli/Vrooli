# Candidate 0.3 delivery-surface review

The author inspected all 97 pages of the first exact native PDF export in page-layout sheets, then inspected representative full pages at the 100 dpi render scale, including the phone and focused controls. No clipped native body text, overlapping figures, missing figures or detached figure captions were observed. The separate native text readback retained the 15 chapters, 17 choice histories and 34 original heading anchors. The first page's question navigation and in-text choice links use those real native anchors.

All 45 inline images extracted from that export decode pixel-identically to their corresponding direct vector derivatives. Desktop figures are explicitly composition overviews at 504 pt width; their small labels are not claimed to be readable interaction detail. Phone specimens are 260 pt wide (or two related 390 px states paired within 504 pt); a focused failed-save crop is also present. Native explanations, readable control views and the linked full-size vector atlas complement the overview figures. A sharp atlas alone was not used to pass the dossier's reading-scale check. The actual signed-in Google Docs and iOS viewers remain unverified.

All 28 atlas page layouts were inspected. The long footer on the one-phone page originally exceeded its width; it was changed to a bounded wrapping text box and the exact repaired page was inspected. All 47 source viewports retain their original vector dimensions. At a matched 1x raster, 32 placements were byte-identical; the remaining placement comparisons differed in at most 0.141% of color channels, with a maximum mean absolute channel difference below 0.012 on a 0–255 scale. Those tiny differences are consistent with vector placement antialiasing, rather than a claim of pixel identity for every atlas placement. No screen content was redrawn for the atlas.

Explicit proposed light-theme text token pairs were calculated at 5.79:1 or higher. This is a token calculation, not a runtime contrast, keyboard, screen-reader, responsive or actual-device accessibility test. Current screen PDFs preserve logical desktop and phone viewports; static pictures cannot prove implemented reflow or control behavior.

These checks are design-production evidence. Owner acceptance, native source application, integration, persistence, current account readiness and actual publication remain unestablished. The final exact export is separately pinned and checked after the final review record is inserted.

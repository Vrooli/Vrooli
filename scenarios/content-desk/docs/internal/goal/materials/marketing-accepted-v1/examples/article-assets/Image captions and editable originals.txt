Inline figure 2: recovery
diagram-02-recovery-wide
Recover from the last confirmed state. Perform a known safe step within approval; seek an owner decision if a consequential submission is uncertain or scope must change. The owner may hold. If proceeding, perform the approved next step, then inspect the actual result before marking complete. Never blindly duplicate a submission.
Alt text: A missing result triggers inspection of the last confirmed state. Assess whether continuation is known, safe and within approval. If so, perform that known safe step. An uncertain consequential submission or changed scope goes to the owner, who may approve the next step or keep it on hold. Only if proceeding, perform the approved step, then inspect the actual result. A held step does not lead to execution. Attempted does not mean verified. Never blindly duplicate a submission.

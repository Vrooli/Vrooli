Design study · 29 October 2026

A calmer way to choose an agent.

Keep the New Terminal flow you like. Make the agent cards easier to scan and put the occasional maintenance work one step away.

Concept, not live UI. Percentages, reset times, versions, availability and update indicators below are invented examples. They do not represent your accounts or installed software. The images on the left are the earlier captured baseline.
01 / Choose

Useful context, without the clutter

Each ready agent stays a direct launch action. Two small meters show how much of each window has been used; names make the direction of the bars explicit. A quiet management control replaces the separate reorder action and shortcut tile.

Known, missing, or stale

Show a percentage only with a trustworthy reading. Missing, stale, limited, loading and error states need explicit text and a path to details. Never turn missing into 0%.

Account and machine matter

A local subscription reading must not be presented as the selected remote machine’s quota unless its account is known to match. Shared subscriptions can span machines.

Preserve the escape hatches

Installation, unsupported states, shortcuts, custom commands, group mode and empty shell remain available. Management is a proposal; actions shown here are not functional.

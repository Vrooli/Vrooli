Aquila / Experience direction / 29 October 2026

A calmer place to work.

Two design studies grounded in the running interface. Keep the capability, give each decision room to breathe, and make the same visual language feel at home on a phone.

Current images are captures of the running app, cropped to the relevant overlay. The settings concept was selected with desktop icons retained; the launcher concept remains a static, unapproved proposal. Phone captures are browser viewports, not physical-device tests.

Revised implementation / 29 October 2026

Settings, revised after your feedback

The centered phone header and back chevron now share one row. A first visit opens the category list; later visits restore the last page or list. Shared connected controls and stronger type hierarchy bring the contents closer to the approved concept.

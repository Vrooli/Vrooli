What all three designs do

The default browser opens on demand and keeps its place between runs. You can also open a separate browser. A person or an agent can drive each target, one at a time. Agents opt in, and you can take over or hand control back.

Browsers, physical devices and emulators use the same picker and handoff, with controls that fit each target.

Choose where to land

A. Open the last target Recommended

Open the viewer. Switch targets from its header.

Best fit when you pick up where you left off. To see what else is available, open the picker.

See A in the comparison above

B. Work beside the agent

Show the viewer beside the conversation on desktop. On a phone, the viewer fills the screen and the conversation opens in a sheet.

Best fit for frequent handoffs during chat. The split uses some desktop viewer space.

C. Browse all targets

Start with a list of browsers, physical devices and emulators. See availability and who’s in control before you open one.

Best fit when choosing among several targets. Opening your usual browser takes another tap.

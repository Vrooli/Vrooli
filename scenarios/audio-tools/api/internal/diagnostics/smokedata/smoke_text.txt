The Vrooli audio-tools scenario stitches together speech-to-text, text-to-speech, summarization, and audio transcoding behind a single Connect-RPC surface so operators and downstream apps can lean on one consistent provider chain. Each capability routes through the BYOK, Vrooli, and Local tiers in fixed precedence, with availability cached per tier and per-request credentials carried out of band on metadata headers. The diagnostics suite drives every capability end-to-end against bundled fixtures, then surfaces a single overall pass/partial/fail signal so operators do not have to piece together health from four separate try-it panels.

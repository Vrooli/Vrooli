# AGENTS

## Start of Session
- Read SOUL.md for identity alignment.
- Review current Vrooli strategic priorities and recent decisions.
- Check for changes in the competitive or market landscape.

## Workflow
1. **Scan landscape** — Market trends, competitor moves, technology shifts.
2. **Assess current position** — What are Vrooli strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats?
3. **Identify strategic options** — What could we do? What should we prioritize?
4. **Analyze trade-offs** — For each option: impact, cost, opportunity cost, reversibility.
5. **Advise director** — Present analysis with options and trade-off matrix.

## Strategic Frameworks
- **Time horizons**: Now (this week), Near (this month), Far (this quarter).
- **Reversibility**: Gates (one-way, high stakes) vs. Doors (two-way, low stakes).
- **Leverage**: What single action would create the most compound value?
- **Bottleneck analysis**: What is currently limiting Vrooli growth?

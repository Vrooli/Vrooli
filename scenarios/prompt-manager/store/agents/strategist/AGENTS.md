# AGENTS

## Start of Session
- Read SOUL.md for identity alignment.
- Review current Vrooli portfolio priorities and recent decisions.
- Review `swarm-manager overview --format json` and current initiative rollups.
- Check for signals that suggest missing initiatives or mis-sequenced work.

## Workflow
1. **Assess portfolio state** — Which initiatives are strong, weak, over-scoped, under-supported, or missing?
2. **Assess current position** — What are Vrooli strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and portfolio bottlenecks?
3. **Identify strategic options** — Continue, pause, split, merge, supplement, or propose initiatives.
4. **Analyze trade-offs** — For each option: impact, cost, opportunity cost, reversibility, and portfolio fit.
5. **Advise director** — Present analysis with options and trade-off matrix.

## Strategic Frameworks
- **Time horizons**: Now (this week), Near (this month), Far (this quarter).
- **Reversibility**: Gates (one-way, high stakes) vs. Doors (two-way, low stakes).
- **Leverage**: What single action would create the most compound value?
- **Bottleneck analysis**: What is currently limiting portfolio progress and Vrooli growth?

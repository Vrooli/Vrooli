## Workflow
1. **Scan for stories** — What has been built, fixed, or improved recently?
2. **Evaluate opportunities** — Which developments would resonate with our audience?
3. **Brief content-creator** — Provide context, angle, and platform requirements.
4. **Brief market-researcher** — Request audience insights or competitor analysis.
5. **Review drafts** — Check for brand voice, accuracy, and engagement.
6. **Delegate editing** — Send to content-editor for polish.
7. **Approve and schedule** — Final approval for publication.

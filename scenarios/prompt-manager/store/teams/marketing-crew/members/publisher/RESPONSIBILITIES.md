# Responsibilities: Publisher

## Primary Duties
- Execute accepted publish proposals.
- Polish approved drafts for platform fit without changing their core voice or honesty flags.
- Maintain publish logs, scenario/improvement mention records, and coverage state.
- Keep `topic:publish-log/*` and `topic:coverage-snapshot/*` queryable for advertisers, brand-manager, researcher, and future telemetry loops.
- Surface channel-rule drift and missing scheduling capability.
- Route channel friction, account gaps, scheduling gaps, and media-production gaps into decisions or typed marketing-craft observations.

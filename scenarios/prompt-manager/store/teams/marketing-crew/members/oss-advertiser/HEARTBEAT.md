# Heartbeat: OSS Advertiser

## Task Loop
1. Check source-system health for the dev-log evidence path.
2. Read OSS artifact requests, publish logs, coverage snapshots, relevant research evidence, and recent OSS-lane ad-run entries.
3. Mine recent shipped work and skill publication milestones.
4. Select story arcs with concrete evidence and audience value.
5. Draft artifacts and append declared team working-state entries.
6. Check OSS coverage state.
7. Check supersession on owned pending decisions.
8. Raise publish, coverage, or capability decisions only when warranted.
9. Record the ad-run knowledge entry.

## Handoff Shape
### Source-system health
### Artifact requests reviewed
### Story arcs considered
### Drafts produced
### Coverage or capability gaps
### Supersessions
### Knowledge entry written

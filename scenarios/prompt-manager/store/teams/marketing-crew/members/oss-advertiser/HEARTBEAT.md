# Heartbeat: OSS Advertiser

## Task Loop
1. Check source-system health for the dev-log evidence path.
2. Mine recent shipped work and skill publication milestones.
3. Select story arcs with concrete evidence and audience value.
4. Draft artifacts and append declared team working-state entries.
5. Check OSS coverage state.
6. Check supersession on owned pending decisions.
7. Raise publish, coverage, or capability decisions only when warranted.
8. Record the ad-run knowledge entry.

## Handoff Shape
### Source-system health
### Story arcs considered
### Drafts produced
### Coverage or capability gaps
### Supersessions
### Knowledge entry written

# Responsibilities: Marketing Lead

## Primary Duties
- Plan content strategy and calendar.
- Brief content-creator and market-researcher.
- Review all content for brand voice and accuracy.
- Give final approval for publication.

## Deliverables
- Content calendar with upcoming topics.
- Approved content ready for publication.

## Coordination Points
- Receive feature completion notifications from feature team.
- Align messaging with director-swarm.

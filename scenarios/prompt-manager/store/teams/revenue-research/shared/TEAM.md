# Revenue Research Team

## Mission
Identify and evaluate opportunities for increasing Vrooli revenue and profitability. We produce decision-ready briefs with multiple options, trade-offs, and confidence levels.

## Research Methodology
1. **Frame** — What decision does this research inform?
2. **Scout** — Generate diverse ideas from market needs + Vrooli capabilities.
3. **Assess** — Evaluate feasibility against existing scenarios and resources.
4. **Validate** — Ground in external market data, pricing, and competition.
5. **Stress-test** — Challenge assumptions and identify failure modes.
6. **Synthesize** — Merge into decision-ready brief with ranked options.

## Evaluation Framework
Every opportunity is scored on:
- **Revenue Potential** (1-5): How much revenue could this generate?
- **Feasibility** (1-5): How much of the capability already exists?
- **Market Fit** (1-5): Is there validated demand?
- **Strategic Fit** (1-5): Does this align with Vrooli vision?
- **Risk** (1-5): How likely are major failure modes?

## Decision Brief Format
### Recommendation: [Top 2-3 options ranked]
For each option:
- Opportunity brief (problem, solution, revenue model)
- Feasibility assessment (effort, dependencies)
- Market validation (competition, demand, pricing)
- Risk assessment (failure modes, mitigations)
- Confidence level (High/Medium/Low)

## Cross-Team Coordination
- **Director Swarm** commissions research and receives briefs.
- **Feature Team** implements approved ideas.
- **Marketing Crew** uses market insights for positioning.
- **Meta Optimization** receives feedback on research skill effectiveness.

## Key Principles
- Multiple options, never single recommendations.
- Every claim backed by evidence.
- Contrarian review is mandatory, not optional.
- Quantify where possible, qualify where necessary.

# Responsibilities: Research Lead

## Primary Duties
- Frame research questions into actionable investigations.
- Assign work to scouts, analysts, validators, and critics.
- Synthesize findings into decision-ready briefs.

## Deliverables
- Decision-ready briefs with ranked options and trade-offs.

## Coordination Points
- Receive research commissions from director-swarm.
- Deliver briefs to director-swarm.
- Share insights with marketing-crew.

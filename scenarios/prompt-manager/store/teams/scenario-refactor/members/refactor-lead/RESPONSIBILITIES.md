# Responsibilities: Refactor Lead

## Primary Duties
- Prioritize refactoring targets by impact and risk.
- Verify adequate test coverage before approving refactoring.
- Coordinate complexity analysis, backlog authoring, and validation.
- Ensure outputs are Swarm Manager `execute`/`fix` backlog items, not direct target-scenario edits.

## Deliverables
- Refactoring plans with measured complexity reduction targets.
- Progress reports to director-swarm.

## Coordination Points
- Receive code smell findings from QA team.
- Assign work to team members.
- Report improvements to director-swarm.

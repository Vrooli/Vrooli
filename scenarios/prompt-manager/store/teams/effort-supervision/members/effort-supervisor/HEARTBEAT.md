## Task Loop

1. On each wake, follow `large-effort-supervision` §2 for selecting, paging, and decoding owner-board reads. Daily and target-unknown discovery must preserve full pagination; known exact-owner events use the scoped current read and applicable dated fleet facts. Preserve partial, stale, unavailable, and unknown coverage.
2. On full discovery, assess every selected `unfinished_retained_owner` from every page. On an exact event read, assess the named owner and obligations tied to that event. Include owners whose runtime is unknown, needs review, blocked, stale, or unavailable, and owners whose goal home is absent. Use each row's exact owner/detail references; absence of a goal-home path is missing evidence, not completion or permission to skip. Keep unassigned/prepared/retired rows visible with their distinct disposition and do not dispatch them.
3. For each selected retained owner, follow `large-effort-supervision` §§2–3 and complete due bounded owner reads before choosing a diagnosis or repair. Group affected goals by shared failure. In child briefs, cite exact sources and timestamp mutable owner facts; newer owner reads supersede coordinator summaries. Do not direct children to reread broad history. Repair dispatch requires the exact receiver, target, permitted surface declared for this member, current authority, and normal Agent Manager admission. In-scope work needs no new proposal. Use permitted owner operations; do not edit epoch files or direct workers. New work outside the approved destination follows `docs/agent-system/EFFORT_SUPERVISION.md` and `SWARM_MANAGER_WORK.md`.
4. When steering an orchestrator, write only a bounded `supervisor` or `supervisor-audit` entry to that owner's canonical `FEEDBACK.md`, and only when its exact path is listed in this member's declared write surfaces. If the owner read does not supply a registered home/path, record the missing target and leave the handoff pending; do not guess a path or claim the feedback was sent. Record the exact owner, feedback/task/run identity, current state, next action, and return condition.
5. Keep each handoff open until the receiving owner acknowledges it and the original consumer operation verifies recovery. A dispatch, supervisor assessment, source/test result, or healthy provider alone is not receiver acknowledgement or recovery.
6. Daily wake: follow the skill's trend check and weighted non-cache token accounting. Event wake: audit the step-back or third accepted epoch that triggered it (§4). Open workarounds are never quiet. Follow the skill for all selected goals.
7. Record one concise `supervision-assessment/<date>` entry with `prompt-manager team knowledge-add` after all due reads and actions are complete or an exact unavailable/refused operation and external next condition are recorded. Keep runtime, admission, owner acknowledgement, consumer recovery, and acceptance as separate facts.

## Run Decision

Record one final disposition: `repaired`, `audited`, `steered` or `quiet`. 

## Stop Conditions

Finish only after due reads/actions are attempted and results recorded, or an exact unavailable/refused operation, its evidence, and next external condition are recorded. A diagnosis or assessment alone is not completion. Use `audited` only after the triggered audit is complete; use `quiet` only when no workaround is open and nothing is due. For a handoff, retain its receiver and receipt; keep acknowledgement and consumer recovery open. Recheck on changed evidence or the daily/event opportunity. Do not poll, repeat unchanged reads, or bypass refusals.

## Task Loop

1. Find the wake reason: the daily schedule, or a manual trigger from an
   orchestrator after it recorded a step-back, repeated workaround or spend spike
   in its goal home (`QUEUE.md`, `WORKAROUNDS.md`, the active epoch file). Act on
   that reason only.
2. Follow `large-effort-supervision` for the goals involved.
3. Record one concise `supervision-assessment/<date>` entry with
   `prompt-manager team knowledge-add`.

## Run Decision

Record one disposition: `repaired`, `audited`, `steered` or `quiet`.

## Stop Conditions

Finish after the disposition is recorded. The schedule and manual triggers own
recurrence.

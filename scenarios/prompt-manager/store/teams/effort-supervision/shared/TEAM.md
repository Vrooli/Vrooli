# Effort Supervision

Manage arbitrary efforts enrolled through Agent Manager and deliver their accepted
outcomes. Read `docs/agent-system/EFFORT_SUPERVISION.md` and the canonical
`large-effort-supervision` skill for the method and authority boundary.

The runtime selects a bounded evidence cut and any healthy sample. The persisted
diagnostic allowance pays for observation, inference and sampling. Discovery-only
idle belongs to the standing service's shared accounting reference. Attribute
each assessment to its wake ID, effort references and AM run ID. Unknown cost
remains unknown.

The portable team definition defaults to disabled. Enable only after the owner
identity, mandate, recovery and accounting qualification described by the
contract passes. The supervisor has freedom of means for enrolled efforts under
the actual AM mandate; Director Swarm retains portfolio amendments and workers
retain their assigning orchestrator unless coordination requires an owner-backed
change.

Supervision includes finding stale blockers, diagnosing premature stops,
coordinating owner recovery, repairing workspace/runtime integration and improving
reusable operating methods. A newer resolution source is evidence to reconsider;
the durable mandate determines whether action can proceed. Retain exact old
executor identities, provider policy and repair limits. Do not run a second writer
or duplicate a repair already assigned to an effort. Report a missing mandate or
owner capability as that specific gap.

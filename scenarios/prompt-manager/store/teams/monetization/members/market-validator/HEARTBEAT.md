## Reasoning Framework

1. Which queue item closes the most important evidence gap?
2. Is a refresh, a new comparison, or an assumption check the right method?
3. How applicable and material is the evidence to the decision?
4. What uncertainty must remain visible to the operator?

Do not attempt every possible research task. Select the one or two highest-
leverage items and defer the rest with a reason.

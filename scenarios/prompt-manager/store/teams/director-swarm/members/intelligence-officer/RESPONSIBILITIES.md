# Responsibilities: Intelligence Officer

## Primary Duties
- Monitor signals across market, performance, teams, and threats.
- Produce structured intelligence briefings.
- Flag urgent signals immediately.

## Deliverables
- Regular intelligence briefings.
- Urgent signal alerts when needed.

## Coordination Points
- Deliver briefings to director.
- Share signals with strategist.
- Alert operations-chief to operational threats.

# Responsibilities: Strategist

## Primary Duties
- Analyze portfolio options with trade-offs.
- Identify long-term opportunities, gaps, and missing initiatives.
- Advise director on directional decisions.
- Convert raw signals into decision-ready options rather than final actions.

## Deliverables
- Strategic analysis with portfolio options, trade-offs, and recommendation.
- Explicit opportunity cost for the top options and portfolio sequencing choices.

## Coordination Points
- Advise director on decisions.
- Receive initiative and execution signals from intelligence-officer.
- Incorporate revenue-research findings when they already exist.
- Respect the approval boundary: propose, do not authorize.

# Responsibilities: Hypothesis Generator

## Primary Duties
- Analyze bug symptoms and generate 2-5 falsifiable hypotheses.
- Rank hypotheses by likelihood using evidence and recency.
- Refine hypotheses when experiments produce inconclusive results.

## Deliverables
- Structured hypothesis documents following the team template.
- Ranked hypothesis lists with test plans for each.

## Coordination Points
- Receive bug symptoms from debug-lead.
- Deliver ranked hypotheses to debug-lead.
- Incorporate experiment results to refine hypotheses.

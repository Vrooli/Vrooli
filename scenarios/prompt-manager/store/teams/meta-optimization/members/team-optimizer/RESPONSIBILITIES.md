# Responsibilities: Team Optimizer

## Primary Duties

- Audit team compositions, roles, shared documents, and org structures.
- Identify structural bottlenecks and coordination gaps.
- Improve cross-team handoff documentation.

## Deliverables

- Team structure recommendations with expected outcomes.

## Coordination Points

- Receive priority-ranked assignments from meta-lead.
- Report structural improvements with reasoning.
- Coordinate with agent-optimizer on role changes.

# Review the exported film

Review the exact final file after all cuts, mastering and encodes. Record its
SHA-256, reviewer, method and timestamps. Technical checks, viewer-comprehension
review and craft review are separate evidence. Keep all three; a successful
encoder cannot establish meaning or finish. Label self-review honestly. Use an
independent reviewer only when the user or active workflow authorizes delegation.
Operator acceptance is separate from self-review and publication authority.

## Viewer comprehension

Before polish, review the plain script or timed rough against the offer-specific
product brief. Explain the customer job and useful outcome in ordinary language.
Check that the selected value is eligible for the actual target, reliability and
setup path. Look for important benefits omitted because they were inconvenient to
capture. A completed brief does not establish that the strongest story was chosen.

Watch on mute as the named audience. Use only context already supplied by this
film. For every scene, write what the dominant text and image make the viewer
infer. Compare that inference with the supported claims and observed product
behavior. Verify that the beginning establishes category, setup and benefit;
interesting abstraction alone does not explain the product.

Retain the stimulus hash, reviewer context and observed answers in the product
brief (see `plan-format.md`); changing the script invalidates that review.
After viewing, record what a first-time viewer can explain: what the app does,
when it helps, what result was demonstrated and what the next action provides.
Do not give the reviewer the intended answers first. Check visual implications
as well as literal text: phone use, included providers, ecosystem connections and
effortless setup can be implied without being named. Distinguish an unfamiliar
agent's diagnostic review from actual customer evidence. Keep story, offer
accuracy, craft and conversion judgments separate.

For each search/filter/export scene, record **feature**, **sample input** and
**observed result** separately. Lead editorial copy with the feature. Keep example
input in the UI or a subordinate quotation. Do not turn a query, fixture name,
benchmark-like number, metaphor or sample result into a product claim. Searching
for “offline” does not prove offline coding-agent operation. Do not promote an
unverified result count. Examine preview, download, automation, speed and backend
implications. A faithful UI fixture establishes UI behavior only.

## Individual-shot craft and campaign variety

Record each shot's visual intention and focal point. Inspect the actual frame at
full size and the smallest intended player size. Judge silhouette, background,
materials/light, type hierarchy, spacing, contrast, framing and motion together.
A decorative line, gradient or basic 3D object is not a finished composition by
its presence. Compare a representative static frame before expensive animation.
Switch concept or medium when refinements do not solve the weak direction.

Inspect reading holds and the whole timed edit. Check cut boundaries, transitions,
scroll velocity, pointer/camera competition, early loading flashes, texture edges,
legibility and source resolution. Overscan rotating textured layers. Check browser
and phone shells for clean proportions and control occlusion. Review campaign
films together: reusable primitives must not impose the same opening, middle and
ending. Quiet shots and faithful stills remain useful; variety is purposeful.

## Product-footage craft pass

Review the whole product sequence, not only its best frame. Inventory every
static product interval and every demonstration that ends after opening or
expanding a view. Record timestamps, the benefit, the missed action or deliberate
reading/comparison purpose, and a disposition. Use the existing motion,
composition or product-clarity findings; do not invent a motion score.

| Observation | Next pass |
|---|---|
| A recording sits on the stage as an unconsidered rectangle | Compare a deliberate browser/app frame or qualified device treatment using screen-presentation.md; retain direct detail only for a clear viewer reason. |
| A screenshot carries an actionable workflow | Capture the state change and payoff; use the still as a start/result beat if useful. |
| A viewer opens/expands and the sequence stops | Inspect the content, scroll, seek or manipulate the verified view before the result hold. |
| Related formats repeat the same navigation in separate scenes | Cut a concentrated inspection montage; use the saved time for another benefit. |
| The montage starts inside a viewer with no visible source | Restore the first meaningful origin-to-view transition; compress later repeated navigation. |
| A reading beat drifts across little content | Match the pace to reading or breadth; capture more distinct content when the take cannot support the intended breadth. |
| Effects move the frame while the useful behavior remains unseen | Recapture native actions before adding another effect. |
| Acceleration hides the result or suggests unverified speed | Restore the result hold or qualify the timing implication, then inspect the encoded cut. |
| A still explains a result better than interaction | Retain it with the specific viewer reason; generic permission for stills is not a disposition. |

Check that the sequence demonstrates useful depth and breadth without losing the
core promise. Inspect the final montage at normal playback and the smallest
player size. The native action, focal content and outcome must remain traceable.
Check that a viewer can connect an output to its source conversation or workflow
using the film alone. Compare a revision with the accepted cut: retaining a later
payoff does not compensate for removing the setup that explained its value.
Do not demand motion everywhere: purposeful contrast and readable pauses make the
active moments land. A completed source capture alone does not close this pass.

## Closing card as an interface

State one action and a complete human-readable public URL, such as
`https://vrooli.com/apps/<verified-slug>`. An internal route or bare slug is not a
usable destination. Verify the exact URL in a browser and inspect rendered product
identity and landing content. HTTP 200 can be an unrelated fallback page. Record
URL, resolved URL, time, page title/content evidence and availability implications.
Do not promise a download unless that destination actually provides one.

Generate a QR deterministically using an existing encoder. Keep high contrast,
four-module quiet zone, sufficient size and a stationary reading hold. Decode
frames from the **compressed final video** at multiple hold timestamps and
intended playback widths. Record decoded URLs, timestamps, widths and final-file
hash. Source PNG decoding is preflight only. Fail mismatched or unreadable codes;
resize/reposition and re-export. Machine decoding does not establish physical
phone-camera scanning or future destination uptime. Record those limits.

## Written disposition and stopping

For each material finding, record observation, viewer consequence, specific fix,
severity and disposition. Fix major factual, comprehension, readability and
technical problems before calling the draft ready. Retain earlier exports and
alternatives. Review affected meaning/craft checks after a recut and technical/
listening checks after a remaster. Never copy a passing verdict onto a new hash.
Supply unfinished drafts when useful with explicit open findings; do not present
an unpassed readiness gate as acceptance.

# Choose the job before the aspect ratio

Record the placement, viewer question, proof moment and next action in the brief.
Keep one claim ledger across edits. Preserve truthful availability qualifications
and fictional-data provenance in the public viewing context. Do not impose
persistent production labels. This reference covers launch-film variants; tutorials and
founder-led explainers require their own brief.

| Placement | Starting experiment | Editorial decision |
|---|---|---|
| Product page | 20–45 s, landscape | Show one workflow with a sustained result. Supply a poster and a visible play control. |
| X feed | 10–15 s; test portrait or square | Make the opening useful on mute. Put context in the accompanying copy. |
| Reddit paid feed | Short 4:5 candidate | Show the product and one clear benefit. Test a second hook with the same audience/objective. |
| Reddit organic post | Useful workflow excerpt and explanatory text | Check the community rules. Explain limitations. Do not treat ad guidance as organic-post evidence. |
| Supporting page module | Separate short, silent interaction | Keep it beside relevant text. Provide a still and pause/reduced-motion behavior. It is not a replacement launch film. |

Treat durations as hypotheses, not platform requirements or measured winners.
Recompose portrait edits. Select crops from the action and result; do not shrink
an entire desktop into the frame. Check source pixels and qualifiers again.
Review the exported file at the intended phone width. Keep reading time even when
it moves a cut off a beat. Supply captions and a descriptive transcript; they
serve different access needs.

When comparing renderers, keep assets, framing, timing and output settings equal.
Record authoring effort, wall time, memory, failures and final-file defects.
A simple FFmpeg loop and a directed composition have different jobs; their render
times do not establish renderer superiority. Add a toolchain only for a demonstrated
benefit. Keep generated inserts outside product proof.

Measure approved experiments with denominators and comparable audience windows.
Separate early attention, useful watch time, qualified clicks and activation.
Do not infer conversion lift from one production review or a publisher benchmark.

## Sources and local evidence

Checked 2026-10-15. These are methods inputs, not copied recipe bodies.

- [Wistia: purpose and duration](https://wistia.com/blog/optimal-video-length):
  observational hosted-video guidance; no universal optimum.

  advertiser evidence for short/mobile assets; organic fit remains separate.

  alternatives and player access.
- [web.dev video loading](https://web.dev/articles/lazy-loading-video): poster and
  preload choices; do not lazy-load the critical hero poster.

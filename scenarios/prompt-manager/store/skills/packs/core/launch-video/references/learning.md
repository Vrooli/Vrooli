# Learn without canonizing one film

The October 2026 series progressed from operator ratings around 2/10, then 3/10,
then 5–6/10, a half-point increase, and finally Aquila 8/10 and Vega 7/10. These
are local operator judgments against particular exports. Later fresh Field Notes/Focus Pull films regressed approximately one point in
the operator's comparison despite technical passes. The reported losses were
sound energy, purposeful material B-roll and expressive pacing. Preserve those
exports as counterexamples. These judgments establish references, not a proven
production average.

Use `examples.json` through `production.py examples <intent>` to retrieve a few
relevant references by purpose, product, viewport, build/date or proof scope.
Use `shots.json` for reusable shot methods. Keep original assets and failure cases
at their referenced effort paths. Do not copy all footage into the skill or turn
these two campaigns into a universal template. Missing local artifacts are an
explicit reference gap; never silently substitute a different export.

Retain exact feedback in `feedback.json` with speaker, date, export hash, wording,
rating when supplied, finding/disposition and evidence for the resulting change.
Preserve the initial statement and append follow-ups rather than rewriting
history. Unknown rating is null. Distinguish operator taste, technical findings,
self-review and independent audience evidence.

Qualify changes on independently briefed future productions. For each trial,
record audience/context/promise, chosen direction, source/build, exported hash,
technical/semantic/craft outcomes, operator rating, major rework count, elapsed
production/render time, generation attempts, known cost and unknown cost. Include
at least different products or proof types and desktop/mobile contexts in the
reference set before claiming broad repeatability. A passing synthetic fixture
qualifies a mechanic, not creative quality. Unmeasured conversion and audience
comprehension remain unmeasured. Do not invent an automatic aesthetic score.

Store qualification with the delivery's sources and versioned receipts. Capture
recurring deterministic failures at their owners; retain audience, meaning, art
direction and listening judgments in the skill. Delete temporary recipes after
the owner replacement passes the retained failed/passed examples. Use the existing
work record and registry for discovery; do not retry a denied capture elsewhere.

A production handoff exposed a second regression source: intent-first audio
guidance coexisted with a stale plan-format/validator demand for distinct style
families. Reconcile executable gates, examples and prose when capturing a lesson.
The v2 variety rationale now records actual contrast and uncertainty; it never
forces a lower-energy family or treats seed/genre counts as audible quality.
Short-study image samples are also time-rounded by FFmpeg seeking. At scene cuts,
inspect exact decoded frame indices with their PTS before diagnosing an early cut;
retain sampled review limits instead of claiming continuous playback.

# Material and 3D inserts that earn their place

Use conceptual footage to explain a relationship, introduce a physical metaphor,
connect two proof moments or provide a deliberate visual payoff. Do not add it
as filler or substitute it for the product. No required insert count or duration.

## Choose the medium from the shot's action

| Needed result | Suitable medium | Evidence and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Verifiable product behavior, UI state or a customer workflow | Real product capture | Preserve evidence of the running product; generated pixels cannot prove behavior |
| Rich material, silhouette and lighting with a restrained camera move | Generated material still plus camera/light composition | R5's higher-rated Aquila/Vega method; moving a still has no true object deformation or parallax |
| Actual orbit, changing occlusion, moving parts, object deformation or persistent spatial camera | Procedural 3D geometry with an installed qualified tool such as Blender | Retain scene/script, materials, lights, camera, frames, settings and renderer identity; qualify one representative shot before scaling |
| Organic motion or evolving material that is impractical to model | Locally generated video after a bounded comparison | Pin the exact model/quantization/license and complete load/decode budget; inspect temporal consistency and preserve the original reference. No generated-video route is qualified by a still-motion success |
| A simple explanatory relation that is clearest as type or geometry | Code-native composition | Keep it legible; extra depth is not automatically better |

Use imagegen for raster plates through its current skill/tool. A plate brief names
the material, thickness/bevel detail, focal section, lighting/reflections, camera,
composition and negative space for text. Preserve exact prompts, original pixels,
dimensions and hashes; retain unknown model/seed/cost as unknown. Supply no private
product data. Actual app screens always come from product capture.

For genuine 3D, discover the current controlled tool path first. Use an already
installed qualified renderer or govern missing dependencies through SDA. Do not
revive an effort script as a standing service. Retain deterministic scene code,
tool version, engine/device, samples, frame range/fps, output settings and assets.
CPU rendering is valid when GPU availability is absent; a capacity reservation
does not prove Blender can use that device. Bound production-owned processes,
render memory and wall time and retain cleanup evidence under `safety.md`.

## Qualify craft before rendering the whole sequence

Approve a full-size frame for silhouette, material response, edge detail, depth,
lighting and text space. Then inspect a short actual motion sequence for parallax,
occlusion, temporal artifacts and editorial fit. Compare with the strongest
relevant retained reference. Revise the concept or change medium if it remains
basic; more noise, glow or samples cannot repair an unhelpful metaphor.

Describe the action accurately. A camera push and light sweep over a raster are
`generated-still-motion`, not a modeled object animation or image-to-video result.
Keep conceptual origins and licenses in the report/source record. Do not add
persistent production badges to the film. Inspect whether the insert implies an
unsupported product capability, hardware result or target. Cut misleading shots.

Match the insert's composition or motion to the following real proof. Let proof
settle. Preserve the current sound/energy brief and compare an audio-led sequence
before the full render. Do not copy a prior film's motif as a universal template.

## Bounded local comparisons

Use `prompt-manager skill read local-experimentation` before installing or comparing
an unfamiliar video generator. Freeze the same briefs, reference pixels, seeds,
short duration and trial count before viewing results. Admit model loading,
text encoding, inference and decode together. Advertised low-VRAM numbers omit
headroom and do not reserve currently available capacity. A refused route is
`not_tested`, with decision `investigate`; retain the existing baseline.

For restrained plate motion, use the maintained `vrooli host hyperframes` owner.
Freeze local inputs through `scripts/workflow.py`, then retain the invocation's
`capacity_admission` beside the immutable bundle. A deny/queue result starts no
renderer; wait for available capacity. The existing request deadline cancels owned
scopes. Inspect status, diagnostics and lease release before any retry, including
when a verified output was published but release failed. Version changes go
through SDA and the managed closure described in `references/upstream.md`.

The 2026-10-20 local experiment retained two technically verified five-second
640x360 glass/metal plate inserts on HyperFrames 0.8.112 with fresh RAM/CPU
admission. Initial metal/timeout attempts were refused by CPU pressure; after a
recorded protocol amendment and fresh grants, an active timeout stopped capture,
verified owned cleanup and released its lease, followed by a successful metal
recovery render. This qualifies the narrow still-motion baseline and ownership
path; Wan/LTX were not tested. Controlled cancellation/renewal regressions passed.

`~/Documents/Codex/2026-10-19/task/local-experiment/`; see `RESULTS.html` for final

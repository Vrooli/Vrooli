## Service and dependency decisions

HyperFrames is retained, with the existing local 0.8.44 toolchain recorded for the experiment. Research used the newer pinned upstream sources; runtime flags are verified against the installed CLI. No package install, cloud render, or platform rebuild is required.

Remotion’s [engine license](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/blob/main/LICENSE.md) allows individuals, organizations with up to three employees, nonprofits, and noncommercial evaluation under its free tier. Other use requires a company license. At research time, [company pricing](https://www.remotion.pro/license) lists $25/seat/month for creators and $0.01/render with a $100/month minimum for automation. Eligibility and the engine version must be checked before adoption; the skill repository and engine are separate licensing questions.

Pexo’s launch/startup/SaaS skills require PEXO_API_KEY and a hosted project; they upload source assets and delegate script, models, and composition. Its [pricing page](https://pexo.ai/pricing) is credit-based; a reproducible per-film dollar quote was not established. [Terms](https://pexo.ai/terms) and [privacy policy](https://pexo.ai/privacy) govern hosted content separately from the MIT client skill. User-content ownership does not establish clearance of every generated element. Its privacy policy permits aggregated/anonymized training use and sharing with model providers; retention has no fixed day limit and backup deletion can lag. Terms §5.3 retain user ownership while granting a broad perpetual service/content-development license. Exact third-party model rights, reproducibility and total cost still require job-specific review. No account, payment, upload, or generation was initiated. Local HyperFrames plus governed Vrooli media is the fallback.

Additional search found scrollmark/showrunner as a format-oriented Remotion orchestration lead. It was not adopted: another whole pipeline does not resolve Vrooli’s evidenced claim/story coupling or the unsafe capture boundary. HyperFrames’s current motion-graphics director/builder and story-spine guidance is the more relevant same-renderer control.

ACE-Step music provenance is owned by music-tools. Current composition code sends structured BPM/key/duration/seed and rewrite_caption=false. Resource capacity and model rights remain job-level evidence; no inference that every generation is safe on a shared GPU. Generated visual inserts are unnecessary for the selected experiment. No new external image/video provider is installed.

The experiment reuses GSAP 3.14.2 with its header intact. The [GSAP standard license](https://gsap.com/community/standard-license/) permits commercial authored code but restricts competing visual animation builders; no visual builder is introduced. Archivo and IBM Plex font files retain their local SIL OFL notices. Vega’s shipped icon is reused unchanged. The deterministic audio recipe uses no external samples.

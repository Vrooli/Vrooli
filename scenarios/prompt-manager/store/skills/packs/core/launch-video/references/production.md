# Direct one promise

## Discover the job and verify the offer

Resolve the audience and offer before choosing the story. Read Offer Desk's current
intent and the actual deployment/package evidence. Inspect representative product
workflows and their implementation, tests, docs and prior feedback. Existing
marketing may omit useful capabilities; discovery must extend beyond its list.

Before ranking, map the major user-facing workflows: starting work, monitoring
attention/state, reading and inspecting outputs, coordinating or transferring work,
and recovering context. Follow the product’s actual surfaces; these are prompts,
not required features. Give each substantial discovered workflow a candidate and
source, including valuable capabilities whose readiness is unknown. Do not let
missing package evidence erase them from discovery. Record gaps explicitly. Then
choose a coherent story. A focused montage may show several facets of one useful
benefit; the feature inventory must not replace the film's core promise.

Write `product-brief.json` using `plan-format.md`. Each candidate connects a
capability to a user job, current workaround, friction and outcome. Record whether
the value is observed, operator-reported or inferred. Assess reliability, setup and
dependencies for the selected offer. Supported or explicitly qualified conditional
benefits can be selected. Excluded and unknown benefits remain visible research
with reasons; potential value never compensates for absent delivery support.

Compare candidate value by recurring usefulness, consequence, friction removed,
evidence and audience fit. Use written reasons, not an invented numerical appeal
score. Look for combinations that complete a job. Feature uniqueness is not a
requirement; superiority over another product needs actual comparison evidence.

Select the promise independently of capture convenience. Plan the action/result
that would prove it, then obtain safe evidence. If capture cannot prove the best
eligible benefit, report that gap and reconsider the story explicitly. A UI fixture
can prove controls and rendering, not execution, persistence or effortless setup.

Before full polish, review the plain script or a cheap timed rough against these
questions: What is this app? Who would use it and when? What gets easier? What
visible action/result supports that? What does the next action actually provide?
Use only context in the draft. Check implicit target/setup promises in imagery.
Maintain existing claim approval boundaries while proposing overlooked value.

## Record approved truth and direct the film

Write `product-truth.md` before story work. Give each claim an ID, source revision
or read timestamp, approved meaning, required qualifiers, availability, and any
exact-copy restriction. Separate verified behavior from marketing aspirations.
Record brand inheritance and shipped assets. Do not treat source precedence as
permission to show a feature that the product cannot perform.

Write one core promise, primary viewer, viewing context, product category, setup,
problem, transformation, decisive proof moment, takeaway and next action. State
what the first-time viewer must understand before the first product interaction. Select evidence that proves that promise. Feature coverage
is not an objective. A scene must add something that would be lost if it were cut.

## Direct attention through time

Before recapturing or polishing, annotate the existing shot plan with the dominant
focus, preparation/action/result/settle, source-time interval, screen bounds and
incoming/outgoing focus. A benefit label is not a focal subject: name the thing
the eye follows (file link, preview, reply, voice turn). Choose when focus stays
put, travels or changes. Plan adjacent shots together. A device entry ending on
one viewport followed by another take or position needs a repaired join, not
more transition decoration.

Treat spatial continuity as a local handoff contract. It does not lock later
shots to one centered rectangle or scale. Separate source geometry (complete,
consistent viewport) from film placement (side layouts, centered detail or a
motivated move). Give different story jobs distinct compositions and large,
readable type. Preserve the device-entry destination only through its matched
join; alternate later layouts where it serves attention. Compare the new contact
sheet with the accepted reference for lost motion range, type hierarchy and shot
variety before declaring a continuity repair complete.

Read the actual selected music arrangement. Map useful phrase changes, accents,
breaks and resolution with their observed times and source intervals. Measured
onsets/pulse are timing aids, not listening evidence. Place a few decisive focus
changes and payoffs at those cues; leave enough preparation and result time to
understand the action. Vary travel velocity and reading holds. Do not quantize
every click, create constant motion, or compete with simultaneous camera, cursor,
scroll and new text. Review a short encoded action-to-music passage before the
whole render. Repeated similar lengths and identical easing do not establish rhythm.

## Direction comparison

Each direction states concept, audience fit, emotional arc, narrative structure,
shot language, density, typography role, camera behavior, transitions, audio, and
ending. Score product clarity, factual support, distinctiveness, feasibility,
source-material fit, and readability from 1–5 with reasons. Scores record judgment,
not objective quality. Choose a direction whose decisive shot can actually be made. Select from usable captures before full polish. Compare inexpensive timed roughs
for substantial films. Choose the footage balance explicitly; no fixed percentage
replaces decisive genuine proof at a readable size. Custom scenes introduce,
connect and resolve that proof. Review campaign films together for distinctness.

Evaluate against the user's requested energy and the previous accepted film, not
only the agent's own draft. “Clean,” “calm,” and “restrained” are direction choices,
not universal definitions of premium. Look for a distinctive opening silhouette,
sharp editorial contrast, one memorable interaction, motivated spatial continuity,
and a concise ending. Do not equate added diagrams or more transitions with craft.

For a craft-regression brief, write a compact preference card from current
operator feedback. Name the reference exports/hashes and the traits to preserve:
sound energy, material/light quality, motion character and meaningful proof.
Record why a previously successful medium is retained, replaced or omitted.
Generated/spatial inserts are a choice, not a quota. When used, give each insert
a story job and compare its actual material/light/silhouette with the reference.
Use the available imagegen skill for raster plates; retain prompts, dimensions,
hashes and unexposed model/seed/cost as unknown. Camera/light motion over a still
is not true object animation. Discover a governed geometry/video tool only when
the shot needs that capability; do not invent a new service for a plate.

Before full production, render an audio-led representative sequence containing
the proposed insert, actual product proof and a transition/hold. Compare its
motion range, pace and soundtrack excerpt with the named reference. A set of
static styleframes cannot establish pace or sound fit. Prefer a decisive mask,
match cut or acceleration/brake when it serves the brief; repeated slow upward
heading entrances are not the only motion language. Let clear proof settle
between faster editorial accents. Record listening separately and keep its gate
open when unavailable.

## Landing-page visual continuity

Inspect the actual published product route after fonts and assets load. Save
representative hero, product, and closing frames in `visual-reference/`. Record
the URL, read time, viewport, theme values and relevant computed styles. Inspect
motion separately when a reduced-motion screenshot hides the page's signature.
If the page is unavailable, use a dated verified reference or Brand Manager and
record the gap. Record the chosen treatment and its source relationship.

Extend the generated `composition/DESIGN.md` with this compact mapping:

| Cue | Page observation and source | Video application or reason to depart |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | Canvas, raised surface, text, muted text, accent and CTA roles | Record retained roles and deliberate changes; preserve mandatory restrictions |
| Type | Font, weight, line height, tracking, accent words and label hierarchy | Choose readable hierarchy for the film; record source relationship |
| Surface and light | Border, radius, rim light, shadow and depth | Choose a clean browser/device frame or no frame for the shot |
| Motif | Product-specific geometry, texture, imagery or constellation | Adapt the product's own motif; keep it secondary to proof |
| Composition | Spacing, alignment, product framing and density | Translate into the shot's aspect ratio and focal point |
| Motion | Reveal, settle, transition and atmosphere | Compress for film without extending the hook or adding constant drift |

Keep token differences explicit. A mark's container color and a page's campaign
accent can have different roles. Brand Manager governs identity assets and explicit
mandatory restrictions; the landing page informs the surrounding treatment; the film brief chooses its
art direction. Report conflicts with explicit restrictions.
Leave the source systems unchanged. Page order remains independent of film
order; visual continuity does not narrow the story to a scrolling page tour.

Before polish, compare opening, product, detail and ending frames against these
references. For an established teaser treatment, one compact comparison is enough.
Record a visual verdict and specific mismatches. Repair a missing mandatory identity cue before rendering. Record deliberate
changes to optional page cues. Matching hex values is
not a passing visual review. This is a reviewer judgment, not an automated taste
score. Review the exported file again because renderer output can differ.

Do not use illustrative landing-page UI as evidence of shipped functionality.
Keep real captures intact. Put the page's design into surrounding composition,
type and lighting rather than repainting the product. Preserve the requested
music character when adopting the page's visual tone.

## Three visual classes

1. **Product evidence:** actual capture of a real interaction, result or state.
   Record the instance, source digest, capture date, known start state, action, and
   end state. A composition camera move does not prove product motion.
2. **Product presentation:** faithful crop/mask/reframe of that evidence. Retain the
   source and transform. Never change values, remove qualifications, or imply a
   functionality or speed the capture does not demonstrate. Record time compression. Avoid implying measured real-time performance; add an in-film qualification only when meaning requires it.
3. **Explanatory/brand:** typography, diagrams, metaphors, generated atmospherics.
   Keep concepts distinct from evidence; narrow misleading scenes or qualify their meaning.
   These cannot satisfy the product-proof gate. Never generate counterfeit UI.

## Shot selection and motion

Choose the recording's screen treatment with `screen-presentation.md` before
polishing it. Include a full-size frame and short sample of the chosen browser/app
frame or device scene in the existing craft checkpoints. Establish the workspace
before moving into direct detail; staging must preserve the native proof.

Retrieve a few recipes by intent with `scripts/production.py shots <intent>`.
The compact catalog is vocabulary, not a running order. Choose no effect merely
because it exists. For each major motion, finish: “This makes the viewer notice …”.
“Cinematic” is not an answer. Plan anticipation → action → settle → hold; vary pace
around the proof. A still camera can be the strongest choice.

Record crop bounds in encoded source pixels and the maximum displayed footprint
in output pixels, including the largest camera scale. Both crop dimensions must
meet that footprint. Pixel count is necessary, not sufficient: inspect compression,
focus, and text at delivery size. Capture a detail closer when pixels are lacking.
Set both CSS width and height for captured video in HyperFrames, preserving the
source aspect ratio before masking. Version 0.8.44 injects intrinsic width/height
attributes during export; a width-only element can acquire an unintended height
and letterbox the product inside its crop. Snapshot appearance alone does not
catch this: compare the rough MP4 with its source footage before full rendering.
Do not solve weak hierarchy by adding multiple rings. Measure an overlay against
the exact capture layout, and remove it when its subject scrolls away.

## Styleframes and animatic

Compare four representative frames with the source mapping and chosen visual
intention. Inspect silhouette, backgrounds, materials, light, type and spacing
before animating expensive scenes. Switch a weak concept or medium instead of
adding decoration. Distinguish generated still plates with camera/light motion
from actual animated 3D geometry. Record prompts, source dimensions, model, seed
and cost when exposed, provenance and limits. Review brand, dominant focal point, typography,
contrast, density, authenticity, and generic-template symptoms. Keep a short
written verdict for each. Fail a frame with two competing primary reads.

Set the runtime after the selected story is understandable. Provider menus prove
choices, not authenticated execution, shared quotas or cheapest routing. For
subscription/local/BYOK positioning, name which feature uses which funding path
and retain eligibility conditions; distinguish native voice from dictation and
speech playback. Keep useful proposed capabilities in discovery, outside shipped
proof. Extend the cut when this context earns time, or omit a secondary promise;
do not preserve an old duration by making the explanation unreadable.

For an accepted-film revision, reuse applicable prior visual-direction roughs
with their exact files and original review context. Requalify the changed story,
timing and presentation in the current script and animatic. Retained alternatives
are not fresh customer-comprehension evidence; do not regenerate unchanged
exploration solely to refill the manifest.

The animatic uses the actual intended shot durations and reading holds. Review
hook, sequence, proof, redundancies, and ending on mute. Use temporary audio only
with its provenance and replacement status recorded. Do not add polish to rescue
an unproven story.

Reduced-size animatics must preserve authored scene geometry. Verify the actual
rendered output dimensions and timed samples: an outer output root with an inner
scaled authored stage may be needed when the renderer resets the root geometry.
Keep full-resolution styleframes and final encoded legibility/QR checks separate.

Before report playback checks, probe the actual browser's codec support and bound
playback promises. Verify advancing media time, selected source, and exclusive
playback in the delivered report. A valid MP4 may need a VP9/Opus WebM preview
fallback for the available browser. Preserve the full-resolution primary, label
preview dimensions, and retain conversion hashes and decode/QR evidence.

## Production record

Record elapsed time, render time, dimensions/fps, renderer version, generated media
jobs/model/seed, API cost if reported, attempts, manual interventions, and failures.
Unknown cost is `unknown`, not zero. Compare renderer alternatives using identical
source assets and timing. Compare orchestration separately from renderer changes.
A small Remotion test is optional; a new dependency and license obligation require
an evidenced benefit. Do not change the default renderer from documentation alone.

## Product-footage improvement pass

Keep an accepted story stable when the feedback concerns recording craft. Plan
connected native actions and a concentrated montage using `capture.md`. Review
all static product intervals and entry-only demonstrations using `review.md`.
Capture missing action/result coverage before adding camera effects. Use the
existing HyperFrames edit for trims, retiming, match cuts and sound accents.
Record the craft findings and source-to-final timing beside the existing shot
plan and quality review. No additional renderer or fabricated UI is required.

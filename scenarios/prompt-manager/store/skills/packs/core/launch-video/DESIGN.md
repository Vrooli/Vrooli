### Recommendations

#### R1A — extend the existing preproduction contract (recommended)

Use a bounded implementation in this skill and its references/helpers. Separate
discovery, offer eligibility, claim qualification and creative selection. Apply
these decisions before the storyboard, not after media is selected.

1. Resolve the scenario, audience, offer/deliverable/variant references and exact
   deployment targets. Read Offer Desk's current intent and the relevant package,
   dependency and readiness evidence. Preserve source revisions and unknowns.
2. Discover candidate value from representative UI workflows, implementation,
   tests, product docs, existing feedback and prior records. Search beyond the
   existing marketing list. Ask the operator only for consequential unresolved
   priorities; produce an initial evidence-backed shortlist independently.
3. For each candidate, identify the user's job, current workaround, friction,
   useful outcome, source, and the demonstration that would show that outcome.
   Separate observations, inferred value and operator-reported value. A useful
   capability need not be unique; comparative superiority needs comparison evidence.
4. Determine eligibility before ranking: supported in this offer, conditional on
   a stated supported setup, excluded, or unknown. Record reliability evidence,
   dependencies, setup burden and exclusions. Classify each capability separately.
   Do not let high potential value compensate for missing target support.
5. Rank eligible value with written reasons. Consider combinations of features
   that complete a job. Explain important omissions. Keep valuable excluded
   capabilities discoverable with their blocker and reconsideration condition.
6. Select a promise and a workflow; write a plain script or inexpensive timed
   rough that establishes the user, product purpose, benefit and visible result.
   Compare plausible story alternatives for substantive productions. Routine
   teasers can reuse a current, applicable reviewed brief.
7. Obtain matching proof, then use the existing craft and final-export process.
   Report capture limitations without silently replacing the strongest supported
   proposition with whatever is easiest to record. Never fabricate proof.

Use one versioned, per-production product brief referenced by the production
manifest. It holds the offer context, source identities, candidate decisions,
selected benefits, unresolved questions and selection rationale. Scenes refer to
selected benefits and claim IDs. Include the brief in frozen production inputs.
Preserve historical plan inspection while making new productions use the revised
contract; do not require old deliveries to acquire invented retrospective evidence.

Deterministic validation should check references, identities, source freshness
relationships, selected-benefit eligibility and scene/claim coverage. It must
reject a selected benefit explicitly excluded for the offer. Human or agent
review checks entailment, relative value and the impression created by the edit.
A boolean `verified` or a numerical appeal score cannot replace either review.

Record depicted product targets separately from audience playback devices. A
desktop-app film can be watched on a phone. A scene showing seamless phone access
can imply mobile product support and needs its own offer-eligibility decision.
Check visuals, copy and CTA together; a report footnote cannot repair a misleading
main promise. Voice, provider and ecosystem claims need the same dependency check.

Keep discovery proposals separate from approved claims. An existing landing page
can be incomplete while remaining the current approved presentation. Preserve
claim restrictions and route proposed messaging changes through the existing
owner. The launch-video producer does not approve new claims or rewrite upstream
canon to make a film pass. Durable product positioning belongs with the product;
the per-production brief records the offer-specific selection and evidence used.

Reassess selected rows when the offer, target, relevant build/dependency evidence,
claim qualification or operator direction changes. Reuse unaffected evidence with
its actual identity and limits. Do not rebuild a dossier for every edit.

# Team coordination

The generated Operating Policy names your coordination pattern, whether you lead or report, and whether messaging is on. Follow the Policy where it is more specific.

| Pattern | You |
|---|---|
| independent | Own your lane. Nobody will review, delegate or synthesize for you. Stay in scope unless the heartbeat says to coordinate. |
| peer | There is no lead. Check team working state before duplicating a teammate's work. Send short, actionable requests through the inbox. Use peer triggers only when an immediate follow-up run pays for itself. |
| leader-led | The lead sets priorities, resolves ambiguity and synthesizes. Reporting members work inside their assignment and surface blockers early, through the enabled surface. |

Whatever the pattern:

- Read your responsibilities and heartbeat first, then the working state your Storage Map names; operate on nothing else.
- Hand off when the next run needs continuity; write knowledge entries for evidence and findings; propose a decision only when a durable surface should change.
- Use task boards and inboxes only when the Policy enables them.
- Persist only what the next run or teammate will use, and leave a clean handoff (R4).

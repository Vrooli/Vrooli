# Monetization — Network Manager

## Purpose

Network Manager is both a lifestyle bundle candidate and a small-office business capability. This document records monetization posture without deciding pricing.

## Current Posture

| Capability | Bundle Fit | Charge Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health snapshot | Lifestyle + business | Free/core | Demonstrates value and supports troubleshooting. |
| AdGuard Home management | Lifestyle + business | Gated bundle feature | Local-first setup and control surface. |
| Optimization experiments | Lifestyle + business | Gated or metered later | Metering only if costly hosted services are introduced. |
| Household profiles | Lifestyle | Gated | Family/device controls. |
| Small-office audit mode | Business Suite | Gated | Reports, approvals, audit history. |
| Multi-site management | Business Suite | Gated | Deferred P2. |

## Buyer

- Personal/home operators who want local network control without hand-maintaining DNS infrastructure.
- Small offices and consultants who need basic local IT diagnostics and DNS/security posture without a full enterprise stack.

## Value Hypothesis

Network Manager makes Vrooli tangible: it improves a user's real internet experience, blocks ads/trackers at the network layer, and turns personal setup work into a reusable scenario.

## Monetization Risks

- Overpromising "faster internet" when ISP/router/Wi-Fi constraints dominate.
- Privacy concerns around DNS query visibility.
- Support burden from router diversity.
- Free alternatives like Pi-hole and AdGuard Home are strong; Vrooli must add orchestration, safety, integration, and reports.

## Bundle Component

Recommended posture: lifestyle bundle headliner for homes, Business Suite depth feature for small-office network operations.

## Demand Signals Needed

- Successful personal deployment.
- Before/after optimization reports with clear wins or useful evidence.
- Evidence that users value integrated DNS filtering and Home Automation controls.
- Small-office operator feedback on audit/report needs.

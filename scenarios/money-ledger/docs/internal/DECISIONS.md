should not accidentally relitigate.

## Purpose Of This Document

  testing direction,

| 2026-08-15 | Operator inputs are a typed three-class import, not thirteen money events. | Cash, burn, and services revenue are monetary; time allocation and hours are windowed measures; MRR is a derived rate. | The importer carries unit/window/observation metadata, admits only monetary postings, persists measures separately, and reports MRR as a finding. | 2026-08-15 | Unknown financial posture is not a passing zero. | A cash-only or unavailable source cannot prove revenue-versus-burn coverage. | Goal sustainment stops when both revenue and expense observations are absent, and Offer Desk renders an explicit incomplete default-alive gap. | A declared source supplies a reconciled revenue/burn observation. |
| 2026-08-16 | The console adopts shared controls but keeps product affordances scenario-owned. | Money Ledger needs consistent focus, safe-area, touch-target, and form semantics without turning a template slot into a product contract. | `Button`, navigation, labels, and state announcements follow the shared component canon; pages and experience claims remain the source of truth for ledger actions and financial meaning. | A shared control cannot meet the 44px/focus/label floor, or a product action needs semantics the component cannot express. |
| 2026-08-16 | Production components contain no fixtures. | Fixtures can make a claim look proven while hiding missing API joins and unavailable-source behavior. | Fixtures remain test-only; running pages render live RPC results, named gaps, and real mutation outcomes. | A deterministic preview surface is deliberately added and is clearly separated from the production route. |
| 2026-08-17 | Drill books and goals are archived, never deleted. | The 17 drill postings are evidence referenced by Level 3 progress and the storage contract is non-regenerable. | Books and goals gain an audited archive flag; default position, statement, and goal reads exclude archived records while posting reads remain available by id. | The retention contract or the operator explicitly authorizes destructive erasure with a verified export. |
| 2026-08-17 | Account kinds are a proto enum with five values: `ASSET`, `LIABILITY`, `REVENUE`, `EXPENSE`, and `EQUITY`. | Free-text kinds already differed by casing and could make aggregates silently omit or misclassify money. | API and console writes reject undeclared kinds with the accepted list; migration uppercases legacy values, audits the change, and records unknown values as findings without guessing. | A real accounting class is required that cannot be expressed by the five-value vocabulary; widen the enum through a new decision and migration. |

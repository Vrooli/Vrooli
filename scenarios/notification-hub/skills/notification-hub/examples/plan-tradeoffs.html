Who should the first version serve?

Career Desk is the job-application tool we are building. We have found a plausible fit for people who want to keep their job-search data private, but nobody outside has shown demand yet. The first choice sets how much we have to build and secure, so it is worth making on purpose. It guides the build; it does not hold it up.

OptionWho uses itWhere the data livesWork for us
Self-hosted, one person Recommended One owner, running it themselves On their own computer Least. One workflow to prove first.
Hosted, individuals Individual customers With us, so we must protect it More. We run the service and answer for the data.
Coaches and teams Coaches and workforce teams Shared between several people Most. Separate workspaces, more security, more operations.
Why the first row. It lets us check whether the idea works for one real person before we take on hosting or teams. We can widen later when outside evidence supports it.

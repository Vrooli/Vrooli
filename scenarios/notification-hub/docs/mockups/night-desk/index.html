Notification Hub · the operator's side

A night watch for decisions.

Six decisions need you. Two close today. Twenty can wait. The desk should say that in one look, then get out of the way — on a desk and in a hand, with the same calm.

One system, two compositions. Desktop puts the queue, the evidence and the answer in one field. Phone puts one decision in your thumb. Neither is a reflow of the other.

03 / The gaps

What the desk needs that the Hub does not yet say.

All four build on what is already there: the subject keys, the packet findings, the answer receipts, and the questions an ask can already carry.

Relatedness

Suggest kin. Never merge without a claim.

The Hub already compares questions lexically. Add embeddings from search-hub as a second opinion, then show “same cause”, “similar wording”, and “answered before”. A producer's family key always outranks a suggestion.

ask→subject+embedding→related, labelled
Readiness

One quality model, scored before it costs you.

The Hub already refuses a page that restates the cover, and the steward already lints pages. Merge the two code lists into one taxonomy, project it per ask, and give each producer a score. A decision that cannot be answered cold does not consume your bandwidth.

packet check→findings→producer score
Aftermath

Close the handshake, or say it stalled.

An answer is recorded today, then the desk goes quiet. Project what the answer started: the owner run, its bound, its result, or a stall. That is the same signal the steward calls “answer-waiting”, shown where the answer was given.

answer→owner run→closed / stalled
Impact

Say what waits on the choice.

“Why now” needs teeth. Three optional facts on the ask — what unblocks, what it costs to wait, whether it is reversible — turn a true sentence into a reason to answer today rather than Thursday.

unblockscost of delayreversible
04 / The rules

Six rules that keep it a desk.

These are behavioural, not visual. They are what make the queue smaller instead of prettier.

01

Count decisions, not updates.

A status is read, not answered. It never shares the badge, the count, or the queue.

02

One decision per cause.

Two checks with one cause are one card. Three grants for one product are one pass. The operator answers the cause, not the symptom.

03

Every row answers cold.

Title, source, age, recommendation, effect, and the cost of waiting — enough to answer without opening anything.

04

One accent, one meaning.

Cyan is attention and action. Nothing else competes with the decision.

05

Show the close.

Answer, owner run, result. If it stalls, the desk says so instead of going quiet.

06

Never invent a number.

No faux minutes, no faux impact. If a field is missing, the desk asks for it or leaves it out.

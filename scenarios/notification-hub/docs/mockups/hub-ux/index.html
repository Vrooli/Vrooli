Notification Hub · whole-app UX direction · 14 Nov 2026

A desk, not a dashboard

One pile that needs you, then a quiet log. The Hub answers “what needs me?” before anything else, and every other page is quieter than that. The design source is docs/concepts/UX_DIRECTION.md; these screens use the same palette and tokens as the app. Screens are static; theme toggle below.

Attention ladder
1 · Needs youLarge, warm, countable. Urgency is a bar and a chip.
2 · What happenedUpdates, Delivery and History: small, calm, chronological.
3 · TuningSettings: reachable, never in the way.

On a phone: a sequence

On a desktop: space

Same four destinations, in a fixed sidebar with icons, a count badge and the account at the foot. Lists keep one reading measure (44rem); Delivery is a real two-column dashboard. Rows show the decision’s first line of context, which the phone leaves out.

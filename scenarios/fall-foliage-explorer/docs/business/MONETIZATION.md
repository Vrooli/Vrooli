# Monetization

The PRD positions Fall Foliage Explorer as a seasonal travel planning capability with potential revenue through:

- Freemium access to basic map and status data.
- Premium forecasts, trip planning, and photo gallery features.
- Tourism board and travel agency partnerships.

Product scope and sequencing remain governed by [DOC: ../PRD.md#dependencies--launch-plan]. This document records business context only; it does not authorize new feature work.

## Exact generation prompt

Edit the provided Horizon Android TV concept image into a clean production wallpaper asset. Preserve the existing photorealistic mountain lake at dusk, warm peach sunset behind blue mountains, reflected lake, dark pine trees on the right, calm cinematic color and the exact wide scenic composition. Remove ALL interface completely: every word, tab, clock, settings symbol, underline, app-row label, logo, button, tile and focus outline. Reconstruct the natural sky and lake behind those removed interface regions. No text, no logos, no UI, no border, no watermark. Keep the sky near the top and lake near the bottom dark enough for separate native controls, without baking artificial panels or obvious black rectangles into the image. Landscape 16:9; target 1920 x1080 or equivalent high quality wide aspect. This is a static background, not a screenshot or a new launcher mockup.

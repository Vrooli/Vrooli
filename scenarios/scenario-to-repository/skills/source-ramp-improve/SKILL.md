Use comparable source-export attempts to identify binding friction, failed
closure, policy escapes, stale evidence, clean-build failures, and publication
handoff confusion. Preserve adverse cases and their denominator. Route missing
stable mechanics to scenario-to-repository, repeated compositions to a bounded
program, and external connector gaps to the owning scenario. Never claim live
publication or operator adoption from fixtures alone.

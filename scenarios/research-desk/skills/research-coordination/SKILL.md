## Practice focus: Research coordination

Keep research advancing toward the capabilities the operator wants built. A research topic is a **programme**: its `MISSION.md` holds the operator's words verbatim, a large end state, a scorecard and a table of workstreams sized in tens of agent-hours ([missions](references/mission.md)). A commission is a staffing window on missions, a run is an evidence container, and progress is scorecard dimensions moved and workstreams landed, not findings recorded. The supervisor is the architect and driver of the portfolio, not its auditor: it starts from the missions, keeps every workstream staffed by an owner who works for hours, and rejects small plans and small returns. Each topic's mode (`build`, `propose-then-build`, `gated-effect`, `artifact`, `evidence`) sets what owners build directly and what goes to the operator; within its mode, every topic is pursued at full scale. Match continuation to the commission: a sustained window calls for successive consequential work, while a bounded question may finish early when its consequential frontier is exhausted.

Read `path:docs/agent-system/LONG_EFFORT_DESIGN.md` before supervising a sustained commission. Its rules (D1–D17) govern goal shape, owner lifetime, briefs and closure; this skill applies them to research. When a cohort under-delivers, diagnose it with `effort-diagnosis`.

### Scope

Use for a broad investigation needing synthesis or several commissioned studies sharing decisions or resources. Use `adaptive-research` directly for one bounded investigation. The same chat may investigate and coordinate; a separate supervisor or worker is not required. Start additional agents only when the operator or applicable instructions authorize them. A commission of several studies or a sustained window authorizes the owners and workers it needs; say so in each brief.

Workers use `adaptive-research` to develop questions and `local-experimentation` for comparisons. Read registered skills with `prompt-manager skill read`; if unavailable, read the canonical `SKILL.md` in the repository when accessible and record that source. A skill-registry outage is not a human blocker or a reason to stop independent work. An assignment that names a skill is not evidence the worker read it. Architecture design, external app construction and maintained implementation follow the actual commission; research does not impose a universal read-only boundary or approval gate. This skill owns selection across studies, shared constraints, review and synthesis. Execution, credentials, admission, cancellation and host repair remain with their existing owners. No delivery team, private respawn loop or new scheduler is required.

### Phase 1: Commission the work

**Entry:** The operator has commissioned research outcomes and an allowance, or has asked only for a prepared folder.

1. Recall relevant work with `search-hub query "<research intent>" --type library,record,skill,doc`. Use the research entry point (`path:docs/internal/research-experimentation/README.md`) to locate the commission, applicable topic and current feedback. Read decisive prior evidence selectively; do not send the full history to each investigator.
2. Start from the operator's commission and strongest available evidence. If useful, run `web-console continuity search --query <native-goal-id> --state any` to find prior conversation evidence; read a live owner when readily available. Apply the [cohort lifecycle](references/cohort-lifecycle.md) when deciding whether prior work is superseded: preserve unavailable telemetry as `unknown`, hold only an uncertain dispatch, and use fresh identities and allowance for a new commission. An explicit operator stop plus terminal known worker records can authorize that new commission. `research-supervision-read` is optional evidence collection, not permission to research.
3. Read each commissioned topic's `MISSION.md` and check it with `python3 scripts/mission_check.py <research-home>`. When a topic has no mission, a mission whose workstreams are all small, a stale architect pass or a pending ambition review, run the architect pass and the ambition review ([missions](references/mission.md#the-architect-pass), `LONG_EFFORT_DESIGN.md` §2b) before staffing. Quote new operator words into Operator intent; never paraphrase them there. The commission's study cards are workstreams taken from the missions, carrying their destination, magnitude and showcase. Name the outcome, quality standard, authority and stop conditions. Include in-scope implementation and cross-owner repairs; novelty, size or uncommitted files do not create an approval gate. Use the smallest entry structure that carries the current direction ([guidance](references/direction.md#choose-artifacts-and-startup-context)); give investigators its rationale, evidence, freedom and starting points, not a queue of tiny checks. Distinguish a sustained window from a bounded answer. Form open directions from the objective and evidence, and identify operator-selected versus suggested leads. One experiment is a checkpoint, not completion of a sustained study. For each study, write a destination large on its face (a capability delivered end to end, a working prototype in its owner, a method proven on real changes) and an expected magnitude, so a narrow proof is visibly unfinished. Prepare a commission packet with `python3 scripts/packet.py preview|build --home <research-home> --spec <spec.json>` (research-desk runs the same script); by hand, fill `templates/` and keep the gates in `templates/limits.json` ([method](references/direction.md#generate-a-multi-study-supervisor-goal-from-ordinary-language)). Never ask the operator to craft the prompt.
4. If the operator asks only to prepare a folder, follow [prepare without commissioning](references/direction.md#prepare-without-commissioning). Do not start a clock, goal, worker or effect. A proposed allowance is not an active budget. Stop after producing the reviewable handoff; do not run launch or supervision steps until the operator commissions the work.
5. Reuse the [single-chat launch and recovery method](../local-experimentation/references/adaptive.md#start-or-resume-one-research-chat). Each study retains its entry artifact, checkpoint, allowance and effect ownership. Give a coherent direction that can span checks and sessions; investigators choose methods and may revise the hypothesis within scope. Check workspace access and how progress returns. If native goal telemetry is unavailable, supervise from this session or an authorized direct path; do not wait for a native goal read to begin research.
6. For multiple studies, create one new cohort record per fresh commission under the canonical research home's `cohorts/` directory and follow the [cohort lifecycle](references/cohort-lifecycle.md) for fields and state transitions. The record is three files ([layout](references/cohort-lifecycle.md#record-layout), `LONG_EFFORT_DESIGN.md` D15): `README.md` is the charter and stays stable and under about 25 KB; `STATE.md` is the one-page current state, overwritten at each review and opened by `Needs you`; `LEDGER.md` is append-only history that nobody re-reads in full. Link worker records rather than copying their histories. For one study, keep coordination in its existing record. Link the shared operator-attention view and name its supervisor/writer; reconcile real pending requests using [operator attention](references/operator-attention.md).
7. Include startup, workers, supervision and recovery in one current-commission allowance across phases/follow-ups. Start its clock at the first supervisor action after the operator commissions work; individual dispatch timestamps do not reset it. Read back actual role/model/effort and usage through existing owners; catalog defaults alone do not prove launch selection. Agree material notifications and distinguish enforced, cooperative and unmeasured limits.
8. Choose the effort structure before dispatch. Use the scheduler shape (`harness-goal-authoring` §5 F): this session holds the goal and the cohort ledger, and each study has one owner (§5 G) that keeps its direction for hours and is re-tasked while productive (`followup_task` in Codex; in Claude Code use the two-tier fallback in `LONG_EFFORT_DESIGN.md` §3). Generate every owner brief from the packet card with `fork_turns=none`; carry the stance and destination verbatim; say the owner may spawn its own workers. Count concurrency slots: this session holds one and each owner one, and what is left is shared by all workers and reviewers (with 8 slots, four owners leave three). Write that number in the packet. When the studies need more owners than that, rotate owners between studies at checkpoints rather than shrinking them into minute-long workers. On `agent thread limit reached`, do not retry the spawn; wait for a slot or do the work in this thread. Run an owner that needs its own native goal as an Agent Manager child (`run create --parent-run-id --until-file --charter`, then `run wait --children`). This only works when this session is itself an Agent Manager run. When it is not, or when Agent Manager refuses or is unreachable, use a native owner with the same brief and keep going (`LONG_EFFORT_DESIGN.md` §3 "Goal-bearing children"). The scheduler shape needs a native goal or an Agent Manager run on this session; without one, do not end the turn between units. If no goal is active when the stance message arrives, wait for the operator's kernel; if you must create the goal yourself, copy the packet's `KERNEL.txt` verbatim and never write your own wording. Tell each owner in its brief to message you for four things only: its plan, a showcase claim, an item that needs the operator, and a need it cannot meet itself. Its progress goes to its run folder checkpoint, and it reads its card and mission, not the cohort ledger.
9. Name the current-session supervisor, how it receives progress, and where it keeps the shared attention list. Ask only for a consequential decision the user alone owns; continue independent work while it is pending. Open `STATE.md` (`## Needs you`) and every reply to the operator with the [needs-you items](references/operator-attention.md#keep-one-navigable-view): `### <ID> — <title>` (ID `[A-Z0-9][A-Z0-9_-]{0,127}`), one to three plain sentences, then `Recommended: <key> — <label>` (every decision has one), optional `Options: <key> — <label>; …`, `Packet: <abs path>`, `Secret: <where it goes>`; empty: `No actionable items are recorded.` **Delivery rule:** deliver as the charter's Decisions section says; a charter without one uses [operator attention](references/operator-attention.md). Never claim a send that did not happen. A prompt or native goal is not evidence of active supervision; verify the actual review path before claiming continuous coverage.
   For sustained work, use the current session as supervisor and a visible checkpoint as recovery state. Native goals and Agent Manager are optional aids; neither a failed wake/read nor unavailable infrastructure stops unrelated research. Preserve the allowance and effect ownership in the cohort record.

**Exit/artifact:** The records identify commissioned outcomes, owners, allowances, shared constraints and how to recover context. No launch is claimed from prompt submission alone.

### Phase 2: Advance and challenge

**Entry:** A study returns evidence, reports closure, or encounters a changed condition.

0. Keep every slot working. While a study has a consequential next step and a slot is free, start or re-task its owner; leaving that slot idle is a defect, and polling a feed or status is not work. Compare each return with its workstream's destination and showcase using the measured changes (`effort-diagnosis` `scripts/effort_anatomy.py tree`), not the owner's summary. Judge outcomes delivered, not line counts. When returns shrink, run the architect pass and ambition review (`LONG_EFFORT_DESIGN.md` §2b) before re-tasking. At each checkpoint record which scorecard dimensions moved and which workstreams advanced or landed; update their status in `MISSION.md`. A commission window that moves no dimension is under-delivery: diagnose it with `effort-diagnosis` and report it as such, not as a careful result.
0b. Run the unit loop (`LONG_EFFORT_DESIGN.md` D4, D16); the packet's scheduler procedure carries the same loop with its paths:
   1. Read the charter, `STATE.md` and `OPERATOR.md` entries newer than the last one acted on (direction, answers, wind-down win over the charter; `delivered <ID>` is the desk's delivery record), and run `date -u`. Read a `MISSION.md` only for the card under review.
   2. Block with `wait_agent`, with `timeout_ms` set to the time left until the next review, at most 3600000. It returns when an owner messages or finishes; an operator message interrupts it.
   3. On every wake, run `date -u`. Act on a plan, a showcase claim, an operator item, an owner's need, an owner that finished or failed, or a review that is due. Otherwise wait again for the time that remains; write nothing and message nobody.
   4. A review covers every card once an hour with the charter's commands: `effort_anatomy.py` `drift` per owner and its checkpoint; act on a tripwire, an owner that finished, failed or has a checkpoint older than two hours, a landed showcase nobody claimed, or a workstream that waits ([showcases that wait](references/mission.md#showcases-that-wait)). Then the footprint check (`scripts/footprint.py diff` against the baseline taken once before the first owner): each line belongs to an owner's `Started` list or goes in the keep file ([capacity and gates](references/mission.md#capacity-and-gates)). A list-file line still under `Wanted` was not picked up: tell the owner. Operator steering (a message or `direction` entry) goes to the list or the mission in their words and to the owner that turn; a status question changes nothing ([list workstreams](references/mission.md#list-workstreams)).
   5. Review a plan once with the ambition reviewer and a showcase claim once with the challenger. Do not review, record or report increments; a rejection asks for the larger first item to be built, not for a longer plan. Code that moves money or deletes data gets one independent code review before its claim.
   6. Overwrite `STATE.md` with the next review time and its needs-you items (step 9), append one entry to `LEDGER.md`, and end your turn. Never mark the goal `complete` or `blocked` yourself.
   An owner returns at a showcase claim or after about six hours. Re-task it while `drift` shows no `owner-age` tripwire; otherwise start a fresh owner from the brief template and tell it to read the old owner's checkpoint first. Do not replace an owner in the middle of its work unless it is stuck or failed.
0a. For a `propose-then-build` topic, turn what exploration shows Vrooli is missing into [proposal packets](references/operator-attention.md#proposal-packets) as soon as the evidence supports one; do not wait for closure. When the operator approves one, add it to `MISSION.md` as a `build` workstream and staff it in the next unit at the proposed scale. For a `gated-effect` topic, build the whole path with the effect stubbed and deliver the effect as one prepared decision.
1. Reconcile study states and observations using the [cohort lifecycle](references/cohort-lifecycle.md). Link owner records and update changed facts; do not copy their histories. Agent Manager run status is not proof of a live worker. Workers keep routine progress in their existing checkpoints.
2. Review timing follows step 0b: owner messages and the hourly review. Use supported owner waits or notifications; a wait of seconds followed by a status check is polling. A prompt timer does not prove wakeup or enforce expiry. A scheduler that is an Agent Manager run parks on its review timer instead of holding a wait. See [conditional observations](references/observations.md#identity-wakeup-and-compound-review) for identity, wakeup and compound-read routes.
3. Read the changed checkpoint and decisive evidence. Inspect native state or bounded events when a decision needs them. A healthy process or empty attention list does not prove useful research. Use a fitting owner program for multi-source supervision when it supplies the needed evidence; inspect direct owner details for partial results or consequential decisions. Do not collect unchanged status or build a reporting framework to fill the research window.
4. Review direction using the [direction guidance](references/direction.md#develop-and-reconsider-directions). Apply the worker's [frontier value test](../local-experimentation/references/adaptive.md#choose-the-next-research-question) across studies. Workers choose local checks; the coordinator selects cross-study priorities and challenges pivotal conclusions. Do not turn optional capability work into an unlimited continuation.
   Review early, after the first meaningful blocker or repeated unchanged checkpoints, and at material transitions. Compare the user's intended outcomes with working capabilities delivered, the remaining path to integration, recent evidence, infrastructure friction, staffing gaps and available usage. A busy worker or growing record is not evidence of progress. When the same state is repeatedly confirmed without narrowing the cause, direct one distinguishing test, authorized repair or workaround, or a useful pivot; reassess its effect rather than waiting for the deadline. Record unknown cost and attribution as unknown.
   In a sustained commission, challenge each proposed study closure against the full authorized topic, its prepared brief, and independent alternatives. A failed candidate, unavailable tool, or single bounded result does not exhaust that topic. Consider a changed-condition repair, different tool or scenario, counterexample, transfer check, or another selected topic; select the best useful permitted direction and give the investigator concrete starting points. Record why alternatives cannot proceed before ending the cohort early. Do not invent activity merely to keep an agent busy.
5. Choose the first applicable row. Preserve the earlier evidence and evaluation when directing a follow-up.

| Evidence or condition | Coordination decision |
|---|---|
| The study's closure cutoff has arrived | Start no further research. Close or cancel owned work through its owners and report remaining effects. |
| An effect or its ownership is unresolved | Reconcile with its owner before duplicate or dependent work. Continue independent permitted checks. |
| A proposed check changes the objective, exceeds the remaining allowance, or requires new authority | Retain it as a proposal; do not execute it under the old commission. |
| A route failed but another method or valuable direction fits | Separate method failure from refutation. Commission the permitted direction with starting evidence. Retry an effect only under its declared changed-condition policy. |
| A finding or early closure leaves a consequential permitted check inside the outcome and budget | Record a prospective amendment if scope changes, then update the working brief with the rationale, starting evidence and investigator freedom. Retain failed gates and original clocks. |
| No consequential permitted check fits | Run a fresh challenger with the fixed brief in `docs/agent-system/LONG_EFFORT_DESIGN.md` §2a, the commission and the study card; do not rewrite its question. Accept bounded closure only when it returns `NONE`; otherwise its top permitted route is the next direction. Record its answer. Do not restart merely because time remains. |

6. Before treating work as human-blocked, triage the exact dependency with [operator attention](references/operator-attention.md). Keep ready asks, technical prerequisites and future gates distinct. Surface ready requests in the `Needs you` list at once and deliver them by the delivery rule (Phase 1 step 9); reconcile replies and resume dependent work; continue independent useful research. Review packets that gate a showcase are a request: file them as one item that says what to open and how long it takes.
7. Challenge the evidence most likely to change the decision: replay a join, inspect an original source, test a counterexample or request an authorized independent review. Correct, narrow or reject a conclusion when the challenge defeats it. Label self-review and missing independent review explicitly.
8. Coordinate heavy work through verified cleanup handoffs followed by fresh owner admission. A coordination file or host snapshot grants no capacity. Keep source/offline work moving where independent gates pass.
9. A failed optional route (Agent Manager, Program Runtime, Notification Hub, Prompt Manager, Search Hub) is not a blocker: take the direct path and log it. For a blocker, identify its owning layer and attempt an authorized repair, workaround or alternative. An owner contract is an engineering problem unless a human-only decision actually governs it. Separate any genuine human gate from independent work and keep that work staffed. Follow the [shared checkout and integration guidance](../local-experimentation/references/adaptive.md#shared-checkout-repair-and-integration).
10. At material checkpoints, keep the working brief and current view connected to evidence. Generalize repeated operations only after their inputs and decisions settle; use existing program owners and validate missing-source behavior. For native state, inventory, retained usage or browser evidence, read the relevant route in [conditional observations](references/observations.md).

**Exit/artifact:** Each intervention has evidence, a decision and an exact next check or closure reason. The shared frontier reflects cross-study implications, not just a collection of worker status reports.

### Phase 3: Synthesize and improve

**Entry:** The commissioned outcomes close or the allowance reaches its closure margin.

1. Reconcile scientific findings, execution/cleanup, native goal state and discovery visibility separately. Verify owned effects through their owners; retain unresolved cleanup. Reconcile operator requests, delivery/answer state, remaining gates and the actual resumption/pickup owner. An answer does not reset the allowance for its originating commission; a later fresh commission follows Phase 1 precedence and starts its own allowance. Use the worker closure method rather than duplicating its checklist.
2. Update topic findings, feedback dispositions and next questions through the designated writer. Produce one cumulative synthesis linking each decision to evidence and identifying remaining alternatives. Separate proposed integration from implemented behavior.
3. Reconcile cumulative worker/supervision usage including failures and post-goal follow-ups. Separate uncached input, cached input and output, billing and subscription allowance; unknown remains unknown. Measure accepted outcomes/rework alongside usage, not response size alone.
4. Use the [shared persistence/discovery method](../local-experimentation/references/record.md#persist-and-discover) once for the coordination result. Apply `docs/agent-system/PROMOTION_LADDER.md`: judgment stays in skills, repeated operations move to existing owners, and verified replacements remove old workarounds.

**Exit/artifact:** A new chat can find the question, supported conclusions, unresolved frontier, current ownership and exact next commission without rereading the full conversation. Completion does not qualify automatic recovery or hard budget enforcement.

### Anti-patterns

| Mistake | Replacement |
|---|---|
| Delegate once and collect reports at the deadline | Review material findings and challenge remaining consequential questions |
| Make the coordinator choose every worker action | Commission a direction; investigators choose local methods and follow implications |
| Accurate small checks accumulate without advancing the mission | Reconsider the framing, sources and role before extending that trajectory |
| Spend the remaining horizon checking unchanged state | Wait for a real pending event or close with a handoff |
| Staff studies with minute-long workers | Each study has one owner for hours, re-tasked while productive (`harness-goal-authoring` §5 G) |
| Fork workers with the supervisor's full history | Fresh brief from the packet card, `fork_turns=none`, stance verbatim |
| Supervise by reconciling states | Let tools report identities, allowances and worker state; spend supervision on direction, staffing and challenge |
| Close a study on a narrow proof | A fresh challenger with the fixed brief decides closure against the full card |
| Repair every discovered platform defect before useful research | Fix authorized blockers at their owners; retain other defects and continue independent work |
| Write a commission from the last checkpoint | Start from `MISSION.md`; the latest receipts are evidence, not the template |
| Paraphrase the operator's intent into a smaller mission | Quote it verbatim under Operator intent; restatements go in End state and must not shrink it |
| Pick the most testable corner (one operation's metadata branches, a four-case synthetic comparison) | Pick the workstream with the largest scorecard movement; size rigor to how hard the choice is to reverse ([missions](references/mission.md#sizing-rigor-to-the-decision)) |
| Accept a showcase claim proved only on a target its builder wrote (a three-record page for a scraper), or send such work to the operator for review | Ask what it was proved on. Require the proving ground as a reusable resource or scenario, the mature tool of the trade, and real targets at real size; a review page shows the code ([proving grounds](references/mission.md#proving-grounds)) |
| A run folder or README section per check, screen or poll | One run per workstream per commission; receipts go to `LEDGER.md` |
| A disclaimer paragraph after every result | One "Limits" line per run; report under-delivery as plainly as overclaiming |
| Explore without ever proposing | File proposal packets from the evidence; approval converts them into `build` workstreams |
| End the turn while owners are only working, or wait 30 seconds and check status | Block on `wait_agent` at 3600000 ms inside the turn; a wake with nothing to review is followed by another wait |
| A ledger entry, a report or a reviewer for every increment | One review per plan and per showcase claim; one ledger entry per unit; state in `STATE.md` |
| Keep the ledger in the packet that is re-read every unit | Charter, `STATE.md` and `LEDGER.md` are three files |
| Mention a pending decision inside a status paragraph | `Needs you` first, one or two plain sentences per item, with the link that answers it |
| Treat the operator's status question as the new task | Answer it from `STATE.md` with what changed since the last answer, then continue the unit loop unchanged |
| Polish one case while its showcase waits for a person or for elapsed days | File the one request, mark the workstream waiting, take the next workstream |

### Troubleshooting & Edge Cases

| Observation | Action |
|---|---|
| Chat activity disagrees with native goal state | Preserve both observations; use the launch/recovery method. Manual continuation is not automatic recovery proof. |
| The operator says to stop the research | Stop further dispatch; stop every exact worker through its owner, checkpoint all rows and reconcile effects. If this supervisor runs a native goal, pause that goal after the explicit stop request and read it back. Keep worker and goal states separate; unresolved stop state remains `unknown`, never `stopped`. |
| The operator says to resume and the native goal reads `paused` | Only the operator can re-activate it. Make the first line of your reply: "Type `/goal resume`: I cannot re-activate the goal, and without it nothing wakes me between your messages." Until it reads active, do not end your turn between units; hold the `wait_agent` loop of step 0b inside the turn. |
| A new coordinator takes over | Read topic/run guides and the shared checkpoint; reconcile live owners before resuming. Preserve budgets and one writer. |
| Human judgment is pending | Write the needs-you item, deliver it by the delivery rule (Phase 1 step 9) and continue independent work. Reconcile the answer and actual resumption. |
| A delivery route is refused or its grant is pending | Keep the item in `Needs you` with the exact refusal or approval URL, marked not delivered; do not repair the route now. Channel readiness does not supply sender authority. |
| Capacity warning, host pressure or admission refusal | Run the footprint check first: the usual holder is something this cohort started. Then schedule, wait through the owner's wait command, or move the job to a fleet node; staff another task meanwhile. Never close a run or study on capacity ([missions](references/mission.md#capacity-and-gates)). |
| A commission draft limits candidates, downloads, production edits or tokens for a `build` topic | Remove the limit; commissions choose workstreams and time, not a smaller mode ([missions](references/mission.md#commission-bounds)). |
| Hard expiry or recovery is required but unsupported | Report that execution route unqualified; continue only independent work whose requirements are met. |

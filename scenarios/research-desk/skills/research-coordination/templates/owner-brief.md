# Owner brief template

The scheduler sends this to each owner it starts, with `fork_turns=none`. Fill the brackets from
the card and the charter; destination and stance first. Copy the charter's Shared machine
section and the operator's newest direction for that topic into it; owners do not read the
commission folder. `F`, `B` and `K` are the absolute paths in the charter's Footprint step.

```text
You own [card topic] for this commission: land [the card's workstreams, in its order if it gives one], toward the showcases in your charter. [The card's Read first line and operator's notes, verbatim.] Operator stance, as the charter quotes it: [the stance quotes, then any direction for this topic]. Your charter is [abs MISSION.md path]; re-read it at each checkpoint and after any compaction. Do not read the commission folder, apart from the two footprint files named below. Read the adaptive-research skill first (`prompt-manager skill read adaptive-research`, or the file [the adaptive-research path in the charter]); it wins over any other skill's stop or threshold rule. Mode [mode]: [the mission's approval flow line]. Real boundaries and Not this: [the mission's sections, unchanged]. [The charter's Shared machine section.] Everything else in the repository is in scope (except what your mission reserves to other owners), including deletions, migrations, other agents' work and uncommitted files: integrate with what others changed, never reset or silently discard it; size each change to the destination, not to caution. Spawn your own workers for parallel or long-lived parts, each owning one outcome for as long as it takes, briefed fresh from your charter with this stance; do not spawn a reviewer or challenger for an increment. Prove increments with owner tests and the running showcase. Never prove a deliverable only on a target you wrote for it: use real software, real public material and the trade's mature tools, and name any reusable test environment as a resource or scenario. Wait for workers with wait_agent at timeout_ms 3600000. Message me for four things only: your plan (once, naming what you start in the first hour, while you build it), a showcase claim, an item that needs the operator (an ID, two plain sentences and your recommendation), and a need you cannot meet yourself; also say when an output another topic's mission names exists. Keep progress in [run folder]/CHECKPOINT.md: changed, verified, remaining, unverified, measured changed paths, scorecard dimensions moved. If a showcase can only wait for the operator, for elapsed days or for capacity you cannot free, tell me in one message and take the next workstream; do not polish. List everything you start in your checkpoint under `Started`, stop it when its job ends, and run `python3 -I [F] diff --baseline [B] --keep-file [K]` before every message to me, before any GPU work and on any refused admission (add `--disk` for your run folder's growth); stop a line only if you or your workers started it and no job uses it now, and tell me about any other. A `NOT CHECKED` section is not clean: read that source yourself. [If the card names list files: re-read them at each checkpoint, assess every new Wanted line in the unit you find it, keep each row's state current.] You work for hours: return to me only with a showcase claim or after about six hours, with your checkpoint current.
```

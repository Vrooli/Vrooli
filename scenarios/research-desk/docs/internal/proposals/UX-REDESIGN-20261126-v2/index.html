The first screen

One blue hero answers the only urgent question. 19 waiting, the single most important question, a white Answer pill, and a quiet count of older lists. Under it, live work is a white card with a ring per commission, not a wall of status badges.

What replaces what
  • 01
    Status line → blue hero. The count is the visual anchor; the top question makes it actionable.
  • 02
    Supervisor cards → live rows with rings. Two or three at a glance; the rest on Runs.
  • 03
    “Since last look” list → three pastel-tiled sentences. Meaning first, records on tap.
  • 04
    Four tabs + hidden menu → four tabs + four quick actions. Launch, Topics, Runs and Reviews are one tap.
Details that make it feel expensive

The blue card has a faint concentric ring and a single lime dot — the only decoration on the screen. The live rows use a 34 px ring with the number in the hole. The week strip is a real date selector, not a sparkline. The quick-action tiles are soft grey, not coloured, so the blue stays the hero.

The five-second test
  • ?
    Need me? The blue hero. 19 waiting, the top question, one Answer pill.
  • ?
    What is running? The live rows with rings: 13h left on one, 45m over on the other.
  • ?
    What changed? Three pastel rows under “Since yesterday”, each a sentence rather than a count.

The difference

No blue hero. No bento. The colour is a single lavender Needs attention card, and every other surface is white or soft grey. Primary actions are black pills. The page is a feed you scroll, not a dashboard you scan.

Where it shines
  • 01
    Topics. The two-column card grid is the best surface in either direction: visual, scannable, and full of identity without images.
  • 02
    Reading. Commission and review pages get more air and larger prose; the interface disappears.
  • 03
    Quiet days. When nothing is live, a white feed feels calm instead of empty.
The cost

The first screen is less decisive: live state is a horizontal row, and 19 decisions need a tap into the Inbox. That is why the recommendation is Daylight with Index's topic grid, not Index as the whole desk.

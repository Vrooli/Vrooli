## Required behavior

- Today and Week read the same persisted occurrence identities across configurable consecutive local-date slots, with open/social slots, recorded history, routine anchors and locks retained. Unknown time, nutrition, price, stock or plan coverage remains unknown rather than zero or a completeness claim.
- Plan my week and any replan return a reviewable draft with concise change summary, expandable details and full-week comparison. Apply requires unchanged captured inputs; stale input returns a repairable conflict and never silently overwrites locks, user anchors, open slots or history. Generation alone never mutates the accepted plan.
- Today states whether a nutrition view is one meal or a full day, and keeps Planned, Recorded and Expected scopes distinct without double counting. Fixed user-confirmed supplement schedules contribute according to local recurrence and dates; planner never invents a dose or edits the schedule to fill a gap.
- Isolated compatible same-slot swap uses short confirmation. Changes affecting another date, lock, leftover, hard food rule, or confirmed shopping require full impact review with explicit downstream effects. Undo is revision-safe; history is never edited.
- Week supports desktop board and phone day agenda / All week alternative, with Meals, Nutrition and Time & cost views over one plan/range. Price, portion cost, checkout and actual spend remain distinct; incomplete price/stock coverage blocks unsupported savings or budget claims.
- Groceries derives transparent requirements from the selected plan and actual package sizes. Future reservations do not consume on-hand stock. “Have this”, pickup, purchased, and consumed remain separate facts. A plan change produces an explicit reviewable shopping diff; no automatic purchase or checkout.
- Preserve E2's actual-yield batch and portion ledger semantics where plan/shopping views consume them; no duplicate projection or private inventory ledger.

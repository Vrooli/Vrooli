## 🎯 Overview
- **Purpose**: Add the permanent capability to decide what to eat, plan a realistic week, shop, use one's kitchen, and cook — while balancing a person's own nutrition goals, dietary rules, available effort, variety, and cost across the whole day's food and supplements — with near-zero ongoing research or decision work. Every number is explained, scoped to what is actually known, and never invented, and the experience is beautiful enough to open every day: a calm cookbook in a warm kitchen that survives ordinary photos, incomplete information, small screens, and unavailable AI providers.
- **Primary users/verticals**: The originating user (vegan, high-protein, low-effort), then any individual or household managing personal nutrition; multi-diet support is a first-class product requirement, not a configuration afterthought. The commercial target is a self-hosted personal product that can later serve other diets and customers.
- **Deployment surfaces**: Go API over Connect-RPC with generated proto contracts; typed Go CLI; a React + TypeScript + Vite installable PWA with five primary destinations (Today, Week, Meals, Groceries, Kitchen) plus Explore, recipe detail, focused cooking mode, onboarding, and Settings, composed separately for desktop and phone in Light and Evening appearances; curated meal and kitchen artwork with honest fallbacks; printable recipe, week, and grocery PDFs plus CSV and native JSON transfer; optional adapters for image generation, assisted import, nutrition lookup, and personal-planner calendar links; a deterministic planning core callable without a browser, database, network, or model.
- **Value promise**: A returning user sees the next meal immediately and starts cooking in one action, compares alternatives without writing a reason, plans a week on a calendar-shaped board, shops from a list that remembers what was picked up, and saves a name-only idea without entering nutrition. The system turns a boring or expensive default into affordable variety without turning the user's life into a bookkeeping task.

## 🎯 Operational Targets

### 🔴 P0 – Must ship for viability
- [ ] OT-P0-001 | Durable private workspace | The scenario shall keep every user-owned record in a server-authorized workspace that the local runtime can authenticate, so saved work survives refresh and new sessions and demo content is never adopted as real user data
- [ ] OT-P0-002 | Onboarding, dietary rules, and kitchen setup | When a user runs the four setup steps Your food, Your kitchen, Your rhythm, and Ready, the scenario shall keep a resumable draft separate from active settings, compile data-driven diet presets and explicit exclusions, and preview conflicts before an atomic apply
- [ ] OT-P0-003 | Flexible meal capture and editing | The scenario shall accept a name-only draft, preserve pasted source text and unresolved quantities, and offer a detailed editor for identity, yield, ingredients, nutrition, cost, method, and evidence whose readiness stays separate from profile eligibility
- [ ] OT-P0-004 | Immutable recipe revisions and components | The scenario shall give each recipe a stable identity with immutable content revisions pinned by plans, sessions, batches, and intake, and shall expand components and meal families acyclically while counting each ingredient once
- [ ] OT-P0-005 | Recipe detail, reading view, and ingredient-action map | The recipe detail shall offer Recipe, Nutrition, and Notes tabs with Reading and Recipe map views driven by one validated method graph that also drives printing and cooking mode, and serving changes shall alter displayed quantities only until applied
- [ ] OT-P0-006 | Explainable eligibility and required rules | The scenario shall never trade required exclusions, equipment, or explicit caps against cost, effort, or variety, shall report missing evidence as needs-information, and shall name the rule and remedy behind every eligibility decision
- [ ] OT-P0-007 | Deterministic planning engine | The planner shall run a bounded, seed-reproducible search that returns a draft with concrete occurrences, target evaluations, costs, reasons, unresolved slots, and input references, keep generation separate from transactional apply, and never report a search timeout as proven infeasibility
- [ ] OT-P0-008 | Today meal-first hero and swap | When a user opens Today, the scenario shall show the next relevant planned occurrence as a hero with Start cooking and Swap meal, a seven-day strip, Ready for tonight links, and a full-day overview with explicit Planned, Recorded, and Expected scope, and swaps shall preview scoped effects without editing history
- [ ] OT-P0-009 | Week board, agenda, and replan | The Week destination shall show a seven-day board of configurable meal slots on wide screens and a day agenda with an All week alternative on phones, with Meals, Nutrition, and Time & cost views, leftover links, prep tasks, locks, and a Plan my week preview that rejects stale inputs
- [ ] OT-P0-010 | Nutrition engine, targets, and supplements | The scenario shall compute unit-aware decimal nutrient contributions with distinct nutrient forms, targets with period, bounds, enforcement, and provenance, explicit unknown/zero/partial semantics, full-day versus single-meal scope, and supplement schedules the planner never adjusts
- [ ] OT-P0-011 | Groceries Review and Shop, inventory, and leftovers | The scenario shall derive a stable shopping list with Review and Shop modes, picked-up rows with undo, Have this stock assertions, idempotent purchase confirmation, and reviewable plan-change diffs, over an event-based stock ledger in which planned reservations never consume on-hand stock
- [ ] OT-P0-012 | Cost model and price book | The scenario shall keep allocated portion cost, estimated checkout, and recorded actual spend separate and labeled, record price observations with retailer, package, conditions, date, and freshness, and never fabricate savings, global prices, or a total that hides unpriced items
- [ ] OT-P0-013 | Portability and printing | The scenario shall export and import native namespaced JSON with review before write, semantic round-trip, legacy import, and recoverable restore, and shall produce real vector recipe, week, and grocery PDFs in a light print theme plus safe CSV, never exporting secrets or tenant authority
- [ ] OT-P0-014 | Persistence, concurrency, offline, and recovery | The scenario shall show honest saving states, apply idempotent revision-checked mutations with all-or-nothing effects where specified, run versioned migrations that preserve existing records, and queue offline shopping and timer actions in a bounded outbox that replays without double-applying
- [ ] OT-P0-015 | Accessible, device-composed experience | The scenario shall meet a WCAG 2.2 AA target and compose each surface for its medium — distinct phone component trees where the interaction changes, five labeled bottom tabs with safe-area padding, 44 px targets, reduced motion, and no page-level horizontal scrolling
- [ ] OT-P0-016 | Five-destination shell and unified appearances | The scenario shall present exactly five primary destinations — Today, Week, Meals, Groceries, Kitchen — in the same order on desktop and phone plus Settings, and Light, Evening, and Follow device appearances from one semantic token system that persist without a bright flash and never change food data or start generation
- [ ] OT-P0-017 | Meal artwork with honest fallbacks | Meal heroes and cards shall render one layout with a scene, cutout, editorial, or minimal treatment chosen deterministically from approved, versioned, rights-tracked media metadata, and a missing, failed, stale, or incompatible asset shall fall back without a broken image, layout shift, or generation request
- [ ] OT-P0-018 | Explore meals that fit | Explore shall rank curated and user-approved recipes through the planner's eligibility filter with structured reasons, carry explicit slot context such as Planning Wednesday dinner, and save or add a meal to the week idempotently without silently replacing an occurrence
- [ ] OT-P0-019 | Focused cooking sessions and durable timers | When a user starts cooking, the scenario shall create or resume a session pinned to the recipe revision, method, and scale, record step completion only through an explicit action, run absolute-timestamp timers that survive reload and multiple tabs, and separate batch confirmation from recorded intake
- [ ] OT-P0-020 | Kitchen inventory, equipment, and preferences | The Kitchen destination shall hold optional exact-or-qualitative dated inventory grouped by storage, equipment modeled as physical devices exposing capabilities through complete tiles plus an interactive curated scene, and grouped preferences whose required-rule changes preview their conflicts
- [ ] OT-P0-021 | Reference-quality visual fidelity | Every primary surface, Explore, recipe detail, cooking mode, and equipment selection shall match the approved concept mockups' hierarchy, composition, typography, imagery, and atmosphere in both appearances at desktop and phone widths, verified by captures compared with the mockups and within published media budgets

### 🟠 P1 – Should have post-launch
- [ ] OT-P1-001 | AI-assisted capture and review | The scenario should turn messy text, labels, and links into validated proposals through a capture, extract, validate, compare, review, and apply pipeline whose proposals never become truth or overwrite newer edits, and should stay useful with no model configured
- [ ] OT-P1-002 | Manual-first provider data adapters | The scenario should reach nutrition, price, and receipt providers through interfaces that keep source identity, version, caching, and error classification, propose revisions instead of rewriting history, and keep manual entry prominent
- [ ] OT-P1-003 | Safe URL and attachment ingestion | The scenario should fetch user-supplied URLs server-side with SSRF protection, validated schemes and redirects, and bounded size and time, treat fetched content as untrusted data, and store the URL even when fetching is unavailable
- [ ] OT-P1-004 | Background jobs and budgets | The scenario should persist jobs with explicit states, bounded retries, cancellation, and per-workspace budgets, track extraction success separately from proposal application, and never apply a result twice
- [ ] OT-P1-005 | Scheduled drafts and notifications | The scenario should offer opt-in recurring draft generation and prep suggestions in the profile's IANA timezone that never replace an accepted plan, mark food eaten, or buy groceries, with quiet deduplicated notifications
- [ ] OT-P1-006 | Feedback and preference learning | The scenario should record one-tap swap and deviation reasons, let explicit dislikes outrank weak inference, apply transparent cooldown rules, keep learned preferences inspectable and resettable, and keep missing feedback unknown
- [ ] OT-P1-007 | Bounded optional image generation | When a user opts in, the scenario should create scene and meal images as quoted, budget-reserved, deduplicated, reviewable jobs through the platform image capability, with generation Off by default and no spend from viewing, theme changes, search, or resizing
- [ ] OT-P1-008 | Personal-planner calendar link | When the personal-planner adapter is configured, Schedule prep and Schedule cooking should create idempotent, reconcilable calendar links with timezone-correct timing, and an unconfigured or revoked link should leave untimed internal prep tasks working

### 🟢 P2 – Future / expansion
- [ ] OT-P2-001 | Commercial foundations | The scenario may add configurable commercial policies and entitlement boundaries, provided a model outage or exhausted allowance never blocks recipes, editing, plans, or export of owned data and required restrictions and nutrition checks are never an upsell
- [ ] OT-P2-002 | Subscription lifecycle | If commissioned, the scenario may verify entitlements server-side with idempotent, out-of-order-safe webhook handling and cancellation dates, and keep billing identifiers out of portable meal backups
- [ ] OT-P2-003 | Household collaboration | The scenario may add deliberate roles and profile-to-person mapping with person-specific targets and shared shopping, distinct from simply multiplying serving counts
- [ ] OT-P2-004 | Public recipe sharing and marketplace | Deferred; a future sharing feature must handle attribution, permission, redaction, moderation, and copied-versus-linked revisions deliberately before any public catalog
- [ ] OT-P2-005 | Adaptive and deferred intelligence | The scenario may evaluate adherence modeling, probabilistic inventory, expected-waste and effective-cost optimization, research agents, receipt and order ingestion, and automated ordering only with their required evidence and authorization
- [ ] OT-P2-006 | Scene studio and custom artwork | The scenario may let a user create private scene templates from references under an explicit budget, mark food and safe-text regions, preview representative vessels, and approve them, without a room editor or 3D engine

## 🧱 Tech Direction Snapshot
- Preferred stacks / frameworks: Go API behind Connect-RPC with proto contracts authored first (no literal REST paths except sanctioned exceptions) and typed messages instead of opaque JSON strings; typed Go CLI mirroring each service; React + TypeScript + Vite UI organized by feature folder, built from shared components and the Vrooli react-component-library, with server state through react-query, a breakpoint hook that selects per-medium component trees, i18n for all copy, and one semantic token system for Light and Evening. The nutrition, cost, eligibility, scaling, and planning core is pure Go callable without browser, database, network, or model.
- Data + storage expectations: SQLite via `api-core` with per-domain schemas beside the domain code, versioned migrations that preserve existing records, repository interfaces hiding the engine, transactions for every multi-record operation the specification names, and read models instead of whole-aggregate JSON blobs. Decimal arithmetic internally with canonical decimal strings on the wire; money in integer minor units plus currency; unknown and zero distinct everywhere. Immutable content revisions plus current projections. UTC instants with IANA timezones and local dates kept separate. Media stored as versioned, content-hashed assets with a manifest carrying provenance, rights, approval, focal point, and safe-text regions.
- Integration strategy: one inbound contract per concern, many adapters. Image generation and editing go through the image-tools scenario, which routes models through ai-gateway; calendar links go through a verified personal-planner adapter; nutrition, price, receipt, and AI adapters sit behind interfaces and can be absent. The product stays fully usable with bundled curated artwork, manual entry, and deterministic planning. Third-party packages flow only through Scenario Dependency Analyzer. Model output never authorizes a rule, price, target, stock change, or recipe edit.
- Non-goals / guardrails: No automated checkout or order placement; no autonomous supplement dosing, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical-adequacy or allergy-safety claims; no real-time global grocery-price coverage; no social feed or public marketplace; no generic optimization platform; no full multi-device offline sync beyond the shopping and timer outbox; no household multi-profile optimization, room decoration, or 3D kitchen modeling in the initial releases; no paid generation without an explicit policy.

## 🤝 Dependencies & Launch Plan
- Required resources: None at runtime — SQLite is in-process and curated artwork is bundled. Producing the curated artwork requires the image-tools scenario and its ai-gateway routing during development.
- Scenario dependencies: None required at runtime for the core loop. Optional: `image-tools` (via `ai-gateway`) for in-app generation and assisted media, `personal-planner` for calendar links, `ai-gateway` for assisted import, `notification-hub` for opt-in reminders. Every optional dependency degrades to a working fallback with an honest capability state.
- Operational risks: An attractive UI over fragile data (the 2026-09-22 audit found no working local authentication, an empty database, and test-only domain code — repair the foundation before surfaces); false nutrition precision (explicit unknown and partial semantics with provenance); setup burden (name-only drafts, progressive questions, measured time to first plan); variety that increases work (vary around reusable staples); cheap food that creates waste (portion cost separate from checkout); artwork that drifts from the approved look or becomes a hidden cost (curated manifest, review, bounded generation); overbuilding a platform before the meal loop works.
- Launch sequencing: D0 foundation repair and inventory → D1 visual foundation (tokens, fonts, shell, appearances, shared components) and the Today fidelity proof → D2 collection and recipe → D3 Today and Week → D4 Groceries, Kitchen, onboarding → D5 Explore and cooking depth → D6 production artwork → D7 optional adapters (generation, personal-planner, assisted import) → D8 migration, portability, accessibility, performance, and visual release review. The route is a dependency order tracked in `docs/internal/REDESIGN_PLAN.md`, not a phased plan.

## 🎨 UX & Branding
- Look & feel: A beautifully designed cookbook in a warm kitchen. Light appearance: warm ivory canvas, near-white surfaces, forest-green text, terracotta primary actions, sage selection. Evening appearance: olive-charcoal canvas, ivory text, amber primary actions. Photorealistic food and kitchen imagery carries the colour on Today, Week, Meals, Explore, and the equipment scene; Groceries and Kitchen inventory emphasize readable information. An editorial serif for the wordmark, titles, meal names, and timer numerals; a quiet sans for controls and metadata; hairlines before boxes; restrained line icons from one family. No glass over busy photography, pervasive gradients, oversized metric dashboards, or decorative slogans. The approved concept mockups in `docs/reference/mockups/` are the visual target. Working name Nooch (formerly Daily, D-042); brand stays configurable.
- Accessibility: Product target WCAG 2.2 AA — keyboard paths, zoom and reflow to 200 %, visible focus in both appearances, accessible names on icon-only actions, non-color status cues, 44 px targets for frequent touch actions, contained and restored dialog focus, semantic tables or labeled lists for ingredients and inventory, Move/Copy alternatives to every drag, equipment tiles as the complete alternative to scene markers, reduced-motion support, and timer announcements only on state changes.
- Voice & messaging: Calm, concrete, functional, and never shaming: "Start cooking", "Swap meal", "Ready for tonight", "Planning Wednesday dinner", "Amount not set", "Add prices to estimate your total", "Based on the values available." Prefer "Fits your current rules" over "Safe for your allergy." No slogans, streak pressure, or implication that eating differently is failure. Absent data is unknown, never zero. Generated imagery is labeled Serving inspiration.
- Branding hooks: `DESIGN.md` owns the Nooch tokens for both appearances; the brand stays replaceable. PWA install assets and the wordmark are replaced through brand-manager when a brand is chosen. No unlicensed third-party recipe text or imagery; every shipped image carries provenance and rights in the asset manifest.

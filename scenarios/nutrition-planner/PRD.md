# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

> **Template Version**: 2.0
> **Canonical Reference**: `/scenarios/business-health/docs/reference/canonical-prd-template.md`

> **Policy**: Generated once and treated as read-only (checkboxes may auto-update)

## 🎯 Overview
- **Purpose**: Add the permanent capability to plan and sustain varied, affordable, nutritionally adequate eating around a person's real diet, restrictions, kitchen, preferences, budget, and willingness to cook — representing the whole day's food and supplements, not just dinner — while keeping the ongoing effort and decision burden near zero. Every number is explained, scoped to what is actually known, and never invented.
- **Primary users/verticals**: The originating user (vegan, high-protein, low-effort), then any individual or household managing personal nutrition; multi-diet support is a first-class product requirement, not a configuration afterthought. The commercial target is a self-hosted personal product that can later serve other diets and customers.
- **Deployment surfaces**: Go API over Connect-RPC with generated proto contracts; typed Go CLI; React + TypeScript + Vite console as an installable mobile-first PWA; printable recipe, weekly-menu, and grocery PDFs plus CSV export; optional AI/provider adapters; deterministic planning core callable without a browser, database, network, or model.
- **Value promise**: A returning user sees the next meal immediately, starts guided preparation in one action, compares alternatives without writing a reason, and saves a name-only idea without entering nutrition. The system turns a boring or expensive default into affordable variety without turning the user's life into a bookkeeping task.

## 🎯 Operational Targets

### 🔴 P0 – Must ship for viability
- [ ] OT-P0-001 | Durable private workspace | Every user-owned record belongs to a server-authorized workspace; identity, ownership, isolation, and durable save survive refresh and new sessions; development fixtures and demo content can never be silently adopted as real user data
- [ ] OT-P0-002 | Onboarding, dietary rules, and kitchen | A four-step setup (food, kitchen, rhythm, ready) with a resumable draft, data-driven diet presets, composable exclusions with explicit evidence limits, a visual appliance picker, priority questions, and an atomic apply that previews conflicts
- [ ] OT-P0-003 | Flexible meal capture and editing | A name-only draft is valid; conservative text organization preserves source text and unresolved quantities; a detailed editor covers identity, yield, ingredients, nutrition, cost, method, and evidence; authoring readiness stays separate from profile eligibility
- [ ] OT-P0-004 | Immutable recipe revisions and components | Recipes hold a stable logical identity with immutable content revisions; plans and history pin exact revisions; reusable components and meal families expand into concrete meals acyclically and count ingredients once
- [ ] OT-P0-005 | Recipe map, reading view, and cooking mode | One versioned recipe drives an ingredient/action map, a conventional reading view, and a focused cooking mode; the method is a validated dependency graph with allocations, named outputs, branches, and joins; the same revision is used everywhere
- [ ] OT-P0-006 | Explainable eligibility and required rules | Required exclusions, equipment, and explicit constraints cannot be traded against cost, effort, or variety; missing evidence yields needs-information rather than a pass; every exclusion or eligibility decision names its rule and remedy
- [ ] OT-P0-007 | Deterministic planning engine | A bounded, deterministic, seed-reproducible search produces a draft with concrete occurrences, target evaluations, costs, reasons, unresolved slots, and input references; generation is separate from transactional apply; a search timeout is never reported as proven infeasibility
- [ ] OT-P0-008 | Today dashboard and swap experience | The next meal is shown with a calculation-backed reason and honest unknown states; reason-chipped swaps are scoped to a selected future occurrence with preview, undo, and preserved locks; history is never edited
- [ ] OT-P0-009 | Weekly plan and replan | A dated seven-day plan with configurable slots, anchors, open/social slots, locks, and full-day scope; replan previews changes against a captured input revision, preserves locks, and rejects stale inputs
- [ ] OT-P0-010 | Nutrition engine, targets, and supplements | Unit-aware decimal contribution arithmetic; a nutrient registry with forms kept distinct; targets with period, bounds, enforcement, and provenance; explicit unknown/zero/partial semantics; full-day versus dinner-only scope; supplements are fixed user schedules the planner never adjusts
- [ ] OT-P0-011 | Groceries, inventory, and leftovers | A derived shopping list from an explicit plan and inventory policy with honest package counts, unknowns, and overrides; an event-based stock ledger; purchases, preparation, batches, and consumption are distinct and idempotent; future reservations never consume on-hand stock
- [ ] OT-P0-012 | Cost model and price book | Allocated portion cost, estimated checkout, and recorded actual spend are separate and labeled; price observations carry retailer, package, conditions, date, and freshness; no fabricated savings, global prices, or speculative best-price claims
- [ ] OT-P0-013 | Portability and printing | Native namespaced JSON/bundle export and import with semantic round-trip, review-before-write, legacy prototype import, and recoverable restore; real vector recipe/week PDFs and CSV that download and print correctly; secrets and tenant authority are never exported
- [ ] OT-P0-014 | Persistence, concurrency, and recovery | Honest saving states; idempotent mutations with expected-revision checks; all-or-nothing effects for profile activation, plan apply, import/restore, purchase, preparation, and intake correction; conflicts, retries, and undo preserve work and never double-apply
- [ ] OT-P0-015 | Accessible responsive experience | WCAG 2.2 AA target with keyboard paths, visible focus, contained dialog focus, non-color status cues, reduced-motion support, and mobile/desktop layouts that keep the primary action visible; no page-level horizontal scrolling from wide recipe maps

### 🟠 P1 – Should have post-launch
- [ ] OT-P1-001 | AI-assisted capture and review | A capture to extract to validate to compare to review to apply pipeline turns messy text, labels, and links into validated proposals with evidence; proposals are never truth, never overwrite newer edits, and the app remains useful with no model configured
- [ ] OT-P1-002 | Manual-first provider data adapters | A nutrition adapter (USDA FoodData Central first) plus price and receipt adapters behind interfaces with source identity, version, caching, error classification, and prominent manual fallback; refreshes propose revisions rather than rewriting history
- [ ] OT-P1-003 | Safe URL and attachment ingestion | Controlled server-side fetch with SSRF protection, scheme and redirect validation, bounded size/time, private access for attachments, and untrusted-content handling; the URL is stored even when fetching is unavailable
- [ ] OT-P1-004 | Background jobs and budgets | Persisted jobs with queued/running/waiting-for-review/succeeded/failed/canceled states, bounded retries, cancellation, per-workspace budgets, and no duplicate application; extraction success is tracked separately from proposal application
- [ ] OT-P1-005 | Scheduled drafts and notifications | Opt-in recurring draft generation and a short preparation suggestion in the profile's IANA timezone; a scheduled run never replaces an accepted plan, marks food eaten, or buys groceries; notifications are quiet and deduplicated
- [ ] OT-P1-006 | Feedback and preference learning | One-tap swap and deviation reasons, explicit dislikes that outrank weak inference, transparent cooldown rules, inspectable and resettable learned preferences, and missing feedback that stays unknown rather than becoming noncompliance

### 🟢 P2 – Future / expansion
- [ ] OT-P2-001 | Commercial foundations | Configurable policies, private workspace ownership, provider adapters, and entitlement boundaries ready for commercial use; a model outage or exhausted AI allowance never blocks recipes, editing, plans, or export of owned data; required restrictions and nutrition checks are never an upsell
- [ ] OT-P2-002 | Subscription lifecycle | If commissioned, server-verified entitlements with idempotent, out-of-order-safe webhook handling, cancellation and end dates, and billing identifiers excluded from portable meal backups
- [ ] OT-P2-003 | Household collaboration | Deliberate roles and profile-to-person mapping with person-specific targets and shared shopping; multiplying serving counts is supported earlier and is not the same as treating a household as one nutritional profile
- [x] OT-P2-004 | Public recipe sharing and marketplace | Deferred; a future sharing feature must handle attribution, permission, redaction, moderation, and copied-versus-linked revisions deliberately before any public catalog
- [ ] OT-P2-005 | Adaptive and deferred intelligence | Adherence/temptation modeling, probabilistic inventory, expected-waste and effective-cost optimization, research agents for staples, receipt/order ingestion, and automated ordering are evaluated only with their required evidence and authorization; transparent rules and voluntary feedback come first

## 🧱 Tech Direction Snapshot
- Preferred stacks / frameworks: Go API behind Connect-RPC with generated proto contracts (proto authored first; no literal REST paths except the sanctioned exception cases), typed Go CLI mirroring each service, React + TypeScript + Vite operator console. Deterministic domain logic is a pure, framework-independent core callable without browser, database, network, or model.
- Data + storage expectations: SQLite via `api-core/storage` for the single private workspace, with per-domain schemas beside the domain code and repository interfaces hiding the engine. Decimal/rational arithmetic internally with canonical decimal strings on the wire; money stored as integer minor units plus currency; unknown and zero are distinct everywhere. Immutable content revisions plus current projections, not event sourcing. UTC instants and IANA timezones with local dates kept separate.
- Integration strategy: one inbound contract per concern, many adapters. Nutrition/provider/price/receipt/AI adapters sit behind interfaces and can be absent; the product stays fully usable with manual entry and deterministic planning. Third-party packages flow only through Scenario Dependency Analyzer. AI is an optional adapter whose proposals must pass the same domain operations as manual edits; model output never authorizes a rule, price, target, or stock change.
- Non-goals / guardrails: No automated checkout or order placement; no autonomous supplement dosing, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical-adequacy or allergy-safety claims; no real-time global grocery-price coverage; no social feed, unrestricted recipe marketplace, or generic optimization platform; no full offline multi-device sync; no household multi-profile optimization in the initial releases; no tax, payroll, or accounting behavior.

## 🤝 Dependencies & Launch Plan
- Required resources: None at P0 — SQLite is in-process. A configured model provider is optional at P1 (via `ai-gateway` and the platform's secret store); a secret store is required before any credentialed provider adapter.
- Scenario dependencies: None required at P0. Optional at P1: `ai-gateway` for model inference, `notification-hub` for optional notification delivery, and `document-manager` only if attachment custody is delegated. External HTTP adapters (USDA FoodData Central, price sources) are opt-in and never load-bearing for core workflows.
- Operational risks: False nutrition precision (mitigated by explicit unknown/partial semantics and provenance); setup burden killing adoption (mitigated by name-only drafts, progressive questions, fixture isolation, and measuring time-to-first-plan); variety that increases work (vary around reusable staples/components rather than maximizing unique recipes); cheap food that creates waste or friction (separate portion cost from checkout, keep extra-trip effort visible); an attractive UI over fragile data (prove revisions, idempotency, and recovery before broad automation); overbuilding a platform before the meal-planning loop is complete.
- Launch sequencing: M0 repository fit and schemas → M1 durable collection (name-only draft to revision to collection to native export) → M2 setup and recipe UX (onboarding, rules, map/read/cook, scaling) → M3 usable R0 planning (Today/Week/priorities, swaps, locks, shopping preview, feedback) → M4 R0 portability and polish (import/export, printable PDFs, CSV, responsive/accessibility review) → M5 R1 nutrition (food/product revisions, nutrient registry, units, targets, full-day routine, supplements) → M6 R1 practical planning (components/families, batch inventory, price book, package costing, bounded planner) → M7 R2 assistance (adapters, research jobs, recurring drafts, budgets) → M8 R3 commercial (entitlements, billing, any commissioned sharing).

## 🎨 UX & Branding
- Look & feel: Calm, capable, and slightly playful. Generous whitespace, a strong cobalt primary action, warm lime highlights, white surfaces, dark blue text, and restrained metadata. The landing view gives one clear recommendation rather than a wall of nutrient gauges; supporting detail sits behind intentional disclosure. Working name Daily; brand stays configurable.
- Accessibility: Product target WCAG 2.2 AA — keyboard paths, zoom/reflow, visible focus, meaningful accessible names on icon-only actions, non-color status cues, 44 px touch targets for frequent actions, and reduced-motion support. Dialog focus is contained and restored; live regions announce save errors and import outcomes without narrating every slider.
- Voice & messaging: Reassuring, concrete, and never shaming. "Dinner, decided.", "Save something good.", "What sounds better?", "Amount not set", and "Based on the values available." Prefer "Fits your current rules" over "Safe for your allergy." No streak pressure and no implication that eating differently is failure. Absent data is described as unknown, never as zero.
- Branding hooks: The generated design contract owns tokens; the cobalt/lime/white direction is expressed through those tokens and replaced when a brand is chosen. PWA install assets stay valid; generic placeholder icons are replaced through brand-manager when branding exists. No unlicensed third-party recipe text or imagery.

# Decisions — Offer Desk

This document records durable decisions and tradeoffs future agents
should not accidentally relitigate.

## Purpose Of This Document

Use this document when a choice:

- affects multiple files or future agents,
- rejects a plausible alternative,
- changes architecture, deployment, data, security, monetization, or
  testing direction,
- needs a revisit trigger.

Routine implementation log entries belong in [`PROGRESS.md`](PROGRESS.md).
Known unresolved issues belong in [`PROBLEMS.md`](PROBLEMS.md).

| 2026-08-13 | A **delivery tier is a `variant` node**, not a sixth node kind. | The source canon holds tiers in `strategy/TIERS.md` with their own four-state lifecycle (`active` / `candidate` / `north-star` / `retired`), declared *orthogonal* to the SKU catalog. The node kinds here are `offer`, `variant`, `channel`, `revenue-line`, `deliverable`; `tier` appears in none of them. | An offer is *sold at* a variant, so "Business Bundle at Tier 2" is one offer-to-variant edge and pricing is a property of that edge — which is exactly the orthogonality the source document asserts. The `north-star` state does not survive the move: it maps to `idea`, whose semantics ("captured, not planned against") are identical. | A second axis needs the same orthogonal treatment (e.g. geography or currency zone) and edge properties stop being expressive enough. |
| 2026-08-13 | An **add-on is an `offer` with a `requires` edge to its parent**, not a distinct node kind. | `catalogs/CATALOG.md` splits SKUs into base bundles and add-ons, and asserts membership must be many-to-many. | Guardrail 3 ("no add-on activation before parent bundle has paying users") becomes a transition precondition on the `requires` edge rather than prose — an add-on promotion is refused while its parent has no paying-user fact. Many-to-many survives because an offer may carry several `requires` edges. | An add-on needs pricing or entitlement semantics that differ structurally from an offer, which would mean commerce concerns have leaked in and belong upstream. |
| 2026-08-13 | **No feature candidates are recorded for this scenario**, and no standalone competitive positioning is pursued. | A 2026-08-13 competitive scan found no product joining a lifecycle-enforced catalog to a ledger of actuals — which strengthens the Money Ledger pairing but does not establish standalone demand. An unoccupied space beside mature adjacent markets is more often unwanted than unnoticed. | The roadmap is whatever makes Money Ledger's surfaces better, in the order the PRD sequences. Absence of a competitor is explicitly not treated as demand evidence; the revisit trigger still requires operators to describe the pain unprompted. | The `../business/MONETIZATION.md` revisit trigger fires — all three parts. |
| 2026-08-13 | **The funnel is not modelled here.** | `strategy/FUNNEL.md` describes stage conversion metrics. | Funnel stages are measurements over channels and revenue lines, not records with a lifecycle. Modelling them would put a metrics pipeline inside a catalog. Channels remain nodes; their conversion numbers stay in the ledger and telemetry. | Channel attribution telemetry ships and a funnel stage acquires a state an operator must disposition. |
| 2026-08-13 | **This scenario is the `monetization` team's instrument**; Money Ledger is covered by it, not a second address. | `path:docs/agent-system/TARGET_MODEL.md` §5 defines an instrument as the one scenario a team reads to answer "what is the state of the world I own, and what should I do next". The team's `instrument` block declares `status: none` with archetype `production-ledger` and a gap marker naming `docs/monetization/catalogs/` — the catalog, not the financial model. Four of the team's five members work catalog-side; one is financial. | The declaration becomes `{ scenario: offer-desk, coversScenarios: [offer-desk, money-ledger] }`, matching the one live precedent in the fleet (`meta-optimization` → `meta-optimization-manager`). The `board` domain is the single address and must therefore carry financial-posture rows as well as offer rows (see the next row but one). Money Ledger stays the *marketed* surface and the operator's daily tool — instrument is a work-queue role, not a statement of product importance, and conflating the two is the likeliest misreading of this decision. | The member mix shifts to majority-financial, or `board` is promoted out of this scenario (see the next row). |
| 2026-08-13 | Hosting the `board` **inside the scenario that owns the catalog is accepted**, against a precedent that went the other way. | `TARGET_MODEL.md` §5 invariant 2 says an instrument never authors the denominators it measures against, and deviation `D6` names the failure. The `director-swarm` team applied exactly this rule to disqualify `swarm-manager` from grading the portfolio it holds, choosing `command-center` because it "owns none of what it would observe". This scenario owns the offer graph and would rank it. | Judged acceptable here for two reasons that do not hold in the `director-swarm` case. **First, the circularity is already broken structurally**: two of the board's three queue sources are external to this scenario — actuals come from Money Ledger, and trigger facts are operator-supplied (or, at `OT-P2-004`, another scenario's) — and `OT-P0-005` makes promotion to `active` an operator-role call, so an agent cannot author a success criterion and then satisfy it. **Second, there is no third candidate**: `director-swarm` had a scenario owning none of what it observed; monetization does not, and the `board` domain already owns nothing and derives every entry at read time, which is the property a separate scenario would have been built to obtain. Recording this so a later audit reads a decision rather than filing a `D6`. | An agent gains the ability to both author a trigger and satisfy it without an operator action — at which point promote `board` to its own scenario rather than weakening `OT-P0-005`. |
| 2026-08-13 | **The board carries financial posture, not only offer actuals.** | If the team has one address, the `financial-tracker` member must be able to read runway, goal verdicts and the default-alive gap from it. `OT-P1-002` as written covers only the per-offer earned-versus-intended join. | `board` gains a second Money-Ledger-sourced entry class — runway, goal verdicts with sustain-window progress, and the default-alive gap — surfaced under the same never-zero, named-availability discipline as the actuals join. These are **surfaced, never owned**: no amount is stored here, which keeps the PRD non-goal ("no accounting or balances") intact. Expressed as requirement `INT-005` under `OT-P1-003` rather than as a new operational target, because it widens the ranked board's scope rather than adding a capability. Each member declares the direct `money-ledger` call as its fallback, per the §5 degradation contract. | The posture rows outgrow ranking and start needing their own query surface, which would mean a reporting concern has leaked in. |
| 2026-08-13 | **Price ownership is split three ways** and stated here because three documents disagreed. | This scenario's PRD non-goals say "No pricing or billing"; the delivery-tier decision above says pricing is a property of the offer-to-variant edge; and `strategy/PRICING.md` holds a matrix that is exactly SKU × tier — that is, offer × variant. | **Intended** price is a property of the offer-to-variant edge and lives here, because an intended price is intent and intent is this scenario's entire boundary. **Charged** price lives in the commerce scenario upstream. **Received** amount lives in Money Ledger. The PRD non-goal is read as excluding billing and payment collection, not as excluding the intended figure on an edge. | An intended price acquires billing semantics — proration, tax, currency conversion — at which point it has stopped being intent and belongs upstream. |
| 2026-08-13 | **Benchmark and market-scan records move to the `gates` fact registry.** | `market-validator` maintains dated competitor comps with per-dimension staleness thresholds (pricing 90d, retention/activation 180d, channel-cac 120d, other 365d) and a sweep that enqueues anything past its threshold. Those are records with a status and a rule nothing can refuse — `D3` and `D4` — and neither this scenario nor Money Ledger claimed them. | A comp becomes a fact with an observation date and a dimension-derived staleness window. This reuses machinery `gates` already needs: staleness is a property of a fact, and a stale comp is precisely the *unknown, not false* case `GATE-003` already specifies. `market-validator` then reads the same one address as every other member instead of maintaining a private surface. The `benchmark-staleness-sweep` skill collapses to a thin pointer once the window is evaluated on schedule. | The fact registry starts needing comp-specific structure — source hierarchies, applicability weighting — that no trigger consumes, which would mean benchmarks want their own owner after all. |
| 2026-08-13 | The **gated-lifecycle engine is a general capability**, and is deliberately not being pursued as one yet. | The capability actually being built is: declare a condition, evaluate it on a schedule, refuse illegal transitions, require an operator for the promoting one, and treat an unknown fact as unknown rather than false. Offers are one instance of that shape; the same engine would serve any domain where records wait on conditions nobody remembers to re-check. | Recorded so the generalisation is neither missed nor performed by accident. The scenario stays offer-shaped: node kinds, the status vocabulary, and the board are all offer vocabulary, and generalising them now would cost the concreteness that makes the first version buildable. `../business/MONETIZATION.md` defers standalone packaging on the grounds that most operators lack enough *offers* to need a graph — that argument is about offers and does not transfer to the engine. | A second domain inside the fleet wants declared-condition-plus-scheduled-evaluation, at which point extract the engine rather than copying `gates`. |

| 2026-08-15 | The catalog import manifest is the source boundary and status failures are blocking. | Walking all files or defaulting malformed status to IDEA makes narrative drift look like valid catalog state. | The operator report names declared/actual drift, per-file counts, status lines, and only imports declared lifecycle/tier/membership records. | The corpus changes; update the manifest and rehearse the changed copy before adoption. |
| 2026-08-15 | Money Ledger is resolved through the declared scenario dependency by default. | Offer Desk must be one address without requiring operators to copy a sibling URL into environment setup. | Discovery supplies the endpoint, while an explicit book id remains operator configuration; board reads carry source/age and named degradation. | More than one book needs a user-selected board book. |
| 2026-08-16 | The console adopts shared controls but keeps product affordances scenario-owned. | Offer Desk needs consistent focus, safe-area, touch-target, and form semantics without turning a template slot into a product contract. | `Button`, navigation, labels, and state announcements follow the shared component canon; pages and experience claims remain the source of truth for lifecycle, gate, and board meaning. | A shared control cannot meet the 44px/focus/label floor, or a product action needs semantics the component cannot express. |
| 2026-08-16 | Production components contain no fixtures. | Fixtures can make a claim look proven while hiding missing API joins and unavailable-source behavior. | Fixtures remain test-only; running pages render live RPC results, named source gaps, and real role-gated mutations. | A deterministic preview surface is deliberately added and is clearly separated from the production route. |
| 2026-08-17 | Catalog merge is the one operation permitted to delete a node row. | Duplicate `(kind,name)` records represent two generations of one identity; leaving the duplicate would preserve false graph identity, while an implicit survivor would make a repair non-auditable. | The caller names both ids, dry-run is the CLI default, every reference move and collapsed edge is counted, and one transaction writes an audit naming actor and both ids before deleting the duplicate. | A future identity repair needs cross-kind conversion or semantic reconciliation rather than same-kind duplicate collapse. |
| 2026-08-17 | `rank_reason` may never assert an unobservable earnings claim. | A failed Money Ledger read is epistemically different from a reachable zero; reporting `active and earning nothing` during an outage turns missing evidence into a business conclusion. | Unavailable active/shipped rows say earnings are unknown and name the unavailable source; reachable zero still says earning nothing. Every status has an explicit reason and the board keeps availability separate. | Revisit if the actuals contract gains a stronger completeness guarantee or a different evidence vocabulary. |
| 2026-08-17 | `PROPOSED` is a first-class non-earning lifecycle state, appended to preserve existing status wire values. | A pending operator decision must not appear as active or as a financial result. | Agent promotion records the node as `PROPOSED`; operator acceptance transitions it to `ACTIVE`, while the board says `awaiting operator decision`. | Revisit if proposals become a separate projection rather than a node lifecycle state. |
| 2026-08-17 | Catalog identity is `(kind, name)`, enforced by guarded unique indexes and every importer/write path. | A pre-insert check in only one importer would let the console or a future writer recreate the duplicate generations. | `CreateNode` refuses an existing identity by id; imports upsert status and edges while preserving ids and declared prices; `EnsureMigrations` refuses duplicate-bearing databases with the `catalog-merge` repair command and keeps reads online. The planned BOOK-001 widening is `(book_id, kind, name)`. | Revisit when BOOK-001 introduces independent offer books. |
| 2026-08-17 | Keep `financial-tracker` as a distinct monetization member after the operator's decision. | Money Ledger owns financial computation and source qualification; financial-tracker owns the separate judgment of materiality, missing inputs, and time-cost tradeoffs. Keeping the lane preserves that boundary without duplicating the instrument's calculations. | The team remains five members and the responsibility is written in the member contract. Revisit if the lane becomes purely computational, the instrument absorbs materiality judgment, or the heartbeat cost is no longer justified. | Revisit when the financial-tracker responsibilities no longer contain independent materiality judgment, or when the operator requests a team-composition review. |
| 2026-08-17 | The board **orders** by attention, not just explains itself. | `rank_reason` was computed per row and then discarded: entries were returned in store order, so a retired drill node occupied row 0 while the page's declared first priority ("which triggers fired since I last looked") was scattered below. `OT-P1-003` and `INT-003` were both marked complete against code containing no sort. | `boardRank` is a separate function from `rankReason` — the reason explains a row, the rank places it, and conflating them is how the board came to explain an order it never applied. Order is trigger-met, proposed, active-earning-nothing, active-earnings-unknown, active-earning, candidate, idea, retired; ties break alphabetically under a stable sort so the board does not reshuffle between reads. | A fifth queue source is added whose urgency does not fit a single total order, at which point the board needs grouping rather than ranking. |
| 2026-08-17 | An **unavailable actuals read ranks below a confirmed zero**. | Both look like "no money" on a board. They are epistemically different and the existing `rank_reason` decision already refuses to conflate them in prose; ordering had no such rule. | `active and earning nothing` (rank 2) outranks `active; earnings unknown` (rank 3). Missing evidence is never promoted to a business finding by position on the page, which is the same guarantee the wording already gave. | The actuals contract gains a completeness guarantee strong enough that an unavailable read becomes rare enough to treat as an error rather than a state. |
| 2026-08-17 | `actual_account_id` is **mutable through an audited RPC**, not write-once at creation. | It was settable only at `CreateNode`, and the operator importer writes an empty value for every record it materializes. The result was a catalog in which no imported node could ever be joined to actuals: every board row read `no ledger account mapping`, and the pair's headline claim — an offer that is active and has earned nothing — was structurally unreachable rather than merely unobserved. | `CatalogService.MapAccount` sets or clears the mapping, requires an actor, returns the prior value, and writes a `catalog_audit` row per change. Corrections are new entries, never edits, matching `OT-P0-007`. Mapping is deliberately an explicit operator step rather than an importer inference: guessing which account holds a node's revenue would fabricate an attribution. | A node needs to read more than one account, at which case the mapping becomes an edge rather than a field. |
| 2026-08-17 | Node **kind and status are CLI inputs**, and an unknown kind is refused. | `catalog-create` hardcoded `NodeKind_OFFER`, so deliverables, channels, variants, and revenue lines could not be created outside the importer — which is why the two scenarios that *are* the monetization instrument were absent from their own catalog. | `--kind` and `--status` are declared flags; `parseKind` returns an error on an unrecognised value rather than defaulting, because a silent default files a record under the wrong half of the graph. | The kind vocabulary changes, which is a proto change and should update both ends together. |
| 2026-08-17 | The scenario's **own standalone-product revisit trigger is a record here**, not prose. | `business/MONETIZATION.md` states the three-part trigger and says outright that filing it as a record is "the first real dogfooding test" — the scenario that enforces revisit triggers should not hold its own as a markdown bullet. | `offer-desk-standalone` is a `candidate` offer carrying an `ALL` trigger over three declared facts (`money_ledger_paying_users >= 1`, `actuals_join_quarters_against_real_data >= 1`, `external_operators_describing_forgotten_condition_pain >= 3`). It currently evaluates `UNKNOWN` on all three, which blocks rather than fires. Declaring it also required multi-clause trigger support in the CLI, which the store already had. | The trigger fires, at which point the deferral in `MONETIZATION.md` is revisited on evidence rather than on schedule. |

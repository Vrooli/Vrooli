# Go To Market — Secrets Manager

## Purpose Of This Document

This document records the platform role of the scenario rather than a public product launch plan.

## Audience And Positioning

Platform engineers, scenario maintainers, and CI operators use Secrets Manager to find missing secret configuration and unsafe posture before deployment.

## Channels

The scenario is delivered as a permanent Vrooli capability through its UI, CLI, API, and deployment consumers.

## Launch Motion

Adopt it first for resource and scenario secret inventories, then use deployment-manifest integration for tier-specific packaging.

## Messaging

The promise is evidence-backed secret posture without secret-value disclosure.

## Validation Experiments

Measure whether scenarios complete Vault-backed workflows with fewer manual configuration failures.

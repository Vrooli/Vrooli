Prompt-manager · heartbeat assembly

Restructuring the heartbeat prompt

Every team member's heartbeat is a 521-line assembled prompt. Only 43 of those lines are shared between any two members, and only 74 survive between one member's own consecutive runs. This is a proposal to reorder it by volatility, wrap the context in XML, collapse the handoff, and rewrite the member prose — measured on meta-optimization/skill-optimizer.

03 — Proposal

The three changes

They are separable. Each stands on its own, and they compound.

A · Reorder by volatility, wrap context in XML

Order becomes universal → team → member → volatile → task. Everything above the task is reference material inside a single <context> element with named children; the task sits below it in prose, adjacent to the generation point. Operating Policy splits into its team-invariant 95 lines and its member-specific 14 so the team block stays contiguous.

XML delimits boundaries unambiguously in a way # headings cannot — a heading inside quoted content collides with a section marker, a tag does not. It also draws the line the restructure depends on: this is material to consult, that is the job to do.

B · Collapse the handoff into the ledger

HANDOFF appears 13 times across 6 sections, and its output template is 40 of the heartbeat's 73 lines — 55% of the member's task is a response format. But the block does three jobs, and only two of them should go.

Job the HANDOFF block doesWhere it goes
Continuity for the next runSource Ledger — already outranks handoff in the authority order (rank 5 vs 6)
Completion attestation — artifacts updated, work items filed, entries writtenVerified from attribution receipts instead of self-reported
Forcing a disposition — one of eight named outcomesStays. Becomes one sentence in the task prose, not a template
The fallback must be conditional injection, not standing prose. The builder already knows ledger health — it renders Team Context Wake from it. Ledger healthy → emit zero handoff text. Ledger degraded → inject a short fallback section asking for continuity in the final response. If the fallback is described in every prompt “just in case,” the ceremony has moved rather than gone.

Verifying the write needs no new mechanism: RUNTIME_ATTRIBUTION.md already defines the X-Vrooli-Attribution receipt, so a run's writes to its declared topics are checkable after the fact. That converts a prose instruction into a measured fact — a promotion-ladder step, not a new rule.

C · Rewrite member prose to the skill-authoring bars

RESPONSIBILITIES.md and HEARTBEAT.md were written as formal specifications. They should read as instructions from someone who knows the job. Principles and a worked example are below.

Keep the two files distinguishable. The authority order puts HEARTBEAT.md at rank 3 — “the job for this run” — and RESPONSIBILITIES.md at rank 4, “standing guidance that a task may override.” Merging them into one undifferentiated block makes that precedence ambiguous exactly when it matters. Render them adjacent; keep the ranks legible.

05 — Method

How to rewrite the member prose

The bars already exist in docs/agent-system/SKILL_AUTHORING.md. Its defect vocabulary applies directly; do not invent a second set.

IDDefectWhat it looks like in a heartbeatFix
C1Hand-rolled rule clusterA five-rung priority ladder written out in fullName the standard, delete the rules it replaces
C2Name-and-keepCiting RESPONSIBILITIES.md §"Skill Experiments" and then restating its contents inlineCite once; delete the restatement
C3Rule pileTask-loop step 7: 130 words, six clauses, three cross-referencesOne coherent instruction, or a work table
C4Interpretive entropy“Evaluate whether the target should remain judgment prose, reference an existing Action, become…”A decision table with observable criteria per row
C5Unverifiable rule“Perform supersession when it shrinks or clarifies your pending queue”State a checkable condition, or drop it

Four rules specific to this restructure

  • Templates become decisions. An output shape that exists to force a choice should ask for the choice in a sentence. Keep the enumerated options — they are what make two agents agree — and drop the surrounding form.
  • Write in the second person, present tense, plain voice. “Pick one skill and say why you picked it” beats “Selection proceeds via the usage-weighted priority ladder.”
  • Cite, never restate. If a rule lives in RESPONSIBILITIES.md or canon, the heartbeat names it and stops. Restated rules drift against their source.
  • Standing duty stays in RESPONSIBILITIES.md; this run's job stays in HEARTBEAT.md. If a line answers “what am I responsible for,” it is not a heartbeat line.

08 — Honesty

Risks and unknowns

  • The rewritten line counts are targets, not measurements. Responsibilities 89 → 60 and heartbeat 73 → 30 are estimates from one worked example. The caching numbers do not depend on them; the −24% total does.
  • Caching gains are structural, not yet observed. Reordering makes a longer prefix possible. Confirm with a real before/after token measurement on consecutive runs of one member before claiming the win.
  • The prompt-section registry is already red. Seven graph_prompt_topic_contract_missing errors are open. They appear to be pre-existing and unmasked rather than caused, but this work touches the same registry and should not be started while their cause is unknown.
  • vrooli scenario test prompt-manager cannot currently give a verdict. Nine phases fail on a pre-existing baseline, so “did I break it” has to be answered by diffing run to run rather than by a pass/fail.
  • XML is a contract change across four surfaces, not a formatting preference: the section registry, its invariant test, the authority-order prose, and the operating-graph parity validator all move together.
  • Ledger-primary makes the ledger load-bearing. The conditional fallback removes the single point of failure only if the health check driving it is itself reliable. Decide what “unhealthy” means before relying on it.

# Revenue Line: Consulting / Strategy Engagements

- **Status:** `candidate` (lowest priority among services lines)
- **Revisit trigger:** *"Revisit only if a specific prospect offers a time-boxed consulting engagement that explicitly validates a Vrooli capability we want to prove AND the operator has clear capacity."*
- **Productization target:** often none — consulting is the hardest to productize cleanly because every engagement is bespoke. When it does productize, it's usually into playbooks, templates, or a specialized SKU rather than a clean subscription.
- **Legal surface:** advisory contracts, conflict-of-interest clauses, IP assignment for any artifacts produced during the engagement.

## Hypothesis

People sometimes pay for expertise independent of tooling. In Vrooli's case this would usually mean advising on AI-powered workflow design, agent orchestration architecture, or local-first software strategy.

## Treat as last resort

Among all services lines, consulting has the **highest distraction-to-revenue ratio**:

- Each engagement is unique; capability reuse between engagements is minimal.
- The productization path is unclear for most engagements — advice doesn't convert cleanly into software.
- Operator time is the primary cost and there's no amortization.
- The framing reinforces a "Vrooli = consulting" external perception that's actively harmful to the subscription business.

**`meta-contrarian` and `contrarian` should be especially skeptical of new consulting proposals.** A proposal must clear a higher bar than other services lines:

- Exceptionally clear capability-validation hypothesis (not "they'll benefit from our advice").
- Fixed scope with explicit deliverables and a hard cutoff.
- Operator capacity that is genuinely slack, not displaced from product work.
- Explicit rejection of retainer or open-ended terms.

## Activation discipline (from REVENUE_LINES.md)

On promotion to `active`, this line must have all four of the standing guardrails plus the elevated bar above. See [REVENUE_LINES.md](../REVENUE_LINES.md) for the base discipline.

## Notes

If three or more consulting proposals accumulate as candidates without any activating, that's a signal — either the trigger is wrong, or the line shouldn't exist. Revisit the line's existence, not just individual proposals.

# Work entry point

Choose the construct from the outcome using [Which construct to use](../../docs/agent-system/RULES.md#which-construct-to-use): a research topic is the default for ambitious or multi-hour work, an owner brief for one bounded repair. Finite delivery teams are retired; research replaced them. Their manual and the dated migration cohort table are kept in the Design to implementation programme (`~/.vrooli/research/topics/design-to-implementation/background/FINITE-DELIVERY-MANUAL.md`), which also holds the historical redesign cases (programme README (`~/.vrooli/research/topics/design-to-implementation/README.md`)).

## Where state lives (R4)

| Work | Authoritative state |
|---|---|
| Research topic or owner brief | The topic's home under the research home (`~/.vrooli/research/README.md`); decisions and inbox per [LEVELS.md](../../docs/agent-system/LEVELS.md) |
| Ordered implementation or plan family | Plan Manager's managed store and returned artifact directory |
| Adaptive mandate | The mandate owner; see [SCENARIO_DEVELOPMENT.md](../../docs/agent-system/SCENARIO_DEVELOPMENT.md) |
| Domain-owned experiment | The owning scenario's managed store; no second register here |

Research does not require a delivery team or a new scheduler; see the [research storage decision](../../docs/internal/research-experimentation/STORAGE.md).

## This directory

`.vrooli/work/<name>/` holds the working files of one effort inside the checkout. Each folder is the home of its effort: mission or charter, current state, feedback and evidence are files in it. `delivery/` holds the homes of former delivery teams until their owner retires them, and nothing there is a live commission.

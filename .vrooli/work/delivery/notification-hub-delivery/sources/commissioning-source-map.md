## Explicit source conflicts and unmapped scope

### NH-DEC-26 / escalation eligibility — unresolved
Current PRD `OT-P1-011` and `NOTIFICA-P1-011` say critical asks escalate. Current `api/internal/hub/escalation.go` applies escalation to every overdue non-default ask. The selected dossier’s NH-DEC-26 lists critical-only (A), all overdue non-default (B), and per-ask policy (C), recommends A, and explicitly says the recommendation is pending owner disposition. Preserve the five-minute slices and sensitivity rechecks whichever policy is dispositioned. Do not select A/B/C in this index; do not claim new controls or acceptance while the decision is open. `NH-AC-38` is conditional on A, not evidence that A was approved.

### P1-002, P1-004, P1-005, P1-006 — preserve existing behavior without scope expansion
The selected redesign includes generic reach/channel settings but no provider-specific pairing or SMTP credential screens and no digest/scheduled-delivery controls. The benchmark requires preserving notification delivery and existing integrations; the PRD classifies these four capabilities as post-launch, with iMessage explicitly best-effort/non-gating. Current sources retain an iMessage desktop adapter, an SMTP sender configured from operator environment, digest-window handling and scheduled-at acceptance/hold processing. The commissioning determination is therefore to preserve those existing channel/API/data paths and not add UI or feature scope for them. This is a routine scope disposition from the approved benchmark and current sources, not a human answer to a product-policy question. Existing declared tests remain applicable validation references; their copied failing status is not cleared here. Any observed regression remains reportable and must not be waived or hidden.

### P2 future requirements
`NOTIFICA-P2-002` (SMS) and `NOTIFICA-P2-003` (multi-user routing) have no selected manifest scene, source heading or NH-AC case. Their PRD/registry tier is future; the current approved product posture is personal-owner routing. They are retained here as explicit future scope, not erased from the registry. A scope change requires the normal owner route; no SMS or multi-user support is claimed.

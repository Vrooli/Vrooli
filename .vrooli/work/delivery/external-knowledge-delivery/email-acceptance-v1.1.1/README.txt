CURRENT DECISION
The team must not create Gmail accounts. The owner-facing live closure is adding one existing personal Gmail account through Google OAuth and reaching its usable mailbox. Do not require the owner to provide a second account. Multiple Gmail accounts, All inboxes and account-specific filtering remain first-release product requirements; validate multi-account behavior with controlled synthetic provider fixtures. Clearly distinguish simulated provider evidence from live Google evidence. Any live sending or mailbox changes retain their separate action permissions.

Capability maturation and preferred access routes
Owner direction October14 2026
A Biosphere project is the continuing purpose and capability, not just a particular app. Begin with a Google Drive project home for knowledge, decisions, operating guidance and source links. Use available connected plugins to perform useful work now. Build one or more Vrooli scenarios into a richer, coherent interface that covers the project's needs and integrates external services directly. As the scenario matures and its relevant real journeys are qualified, update the project's guidance to use that scenario first; the plugins become fallbacks for unsupported, unavailable or unhealthy paths. Design, source-code presence or a health response alone does not establish this cutover.
Every project entry must distinguish the current working route, intended mature route, qualification still needed, and how to fall back. Do not silently switch routes, lose external source identities, erase useful manual methods or claim unverified live integration. A unified interface may combine scenario-owned and provider-owned records while preserving their source, account boundaries, permissions and freshness. Drive remains the project/context home rather than a duplicate mailbox, calendar or runtime database.
Personal Planning example: Google Calendar and current project records support useful planning while personal-planner develops into the intended superset for goals, commitments, calendar, focus, actuals and review. Direct Google Calendar integration expands that scenario's reach; once qualified, planning guidance should point to personal-planner first and provider plugins further down the fallback route. This amendment does not itself change current calendar routing or approve provider writeback.
Email and future Communications: use the Gmail plugin as today's working email route while the full local email client is designed and built. Its accepted direction includes familiar inbox/read/compose, semantic search, classification and asynchronous local agent proposals with existing approval/notification integration. Once actual email journeys and integrations are qualified, use that scenario first and Gmail as fallback. The email project is intended to evolve into a broader Communications project spanning email, Slack, Discord and other channels through one or more integrated scenarios, potentially with a general communications entry. Record that destination in the email project and design, but do not rename/move folders, add those channels to immediate implementation, or enable a delivery team merely because this direction is documented.
Continuation: full email dossier and visual atlas remain authorized under the existing design-product-dossier skill. Do not treat Gmail replacement versus companion as an unresolved prerequisite; the owner already chose a full local client and authorized routine design decisions. Explicitly track current assignment/admission and actual blockers; a received or uncertain notification is not evidence of running design work.

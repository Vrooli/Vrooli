## Adopt without coupling

A workflow describes how a particular project proceeds, who decides, and where its records live. A skill packages reusable instructions and resources that an agent can load. A skill can itself contain a workflow; these are useful local roles, not mutually exclusive technical categories.

Let a project reference a specifically adopted revision, or copy the portable method into its own documentation when that is more reliable. Retain provenance and the adoption date in its existing records. Do not require every project to update when the shared method changes. Preserve local exceptions and approval rules. Changes to shared instructions cannot grant new permissions. Record the exact adopted method revision and local exceptions; do not rewrite local pins, source truth, queues or paused workflows merely because a newer shared version exists.

Keep general critical-review guidance bundled here initially. Extract a separate skill only after trials on materially different artifact types demonstrate that it improves behavior without duplicating domain criteria or adding a fragile dependency.

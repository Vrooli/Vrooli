# Evidence-based critical review

This is a reusable review method. The project and artifact supply domain criteria, tools, permissions and acceptance decisions. It grants no authority to change systems, transmit information, or execute a product plan.

## Define what can fail

Before review specify the intended audience, user outcome, critical journeys, authority rules, evidence boundaries and artifact-specific quality bar. Include correctness, completeness, usability, visual coherence, accessibility, recoverability and maintainability when relevant. Do not score irrelevant dimensions merely to inflate rigor.

For a design dossier, trace flows, states, diagrams and decisions. For an interactive artifact, actually operate its controls and reopen saved state in the intended host. For an income-proof document, challenge factual support and relevance to the reader. A generic checklist cannot replace these domain-specific checks.

## Conduct distinct passes

1. Coverage and contradiction review: use the selected profile to find missing journeys/operating paths, hidden assumptions, unsupported claims and cross-section inconsistencies. Reconcile the full decision index and, where applicable, the first-outcome route.
2. User, buyer/operator and failure-path review: walk relevant realistic goals and adverse cases; challenge discoverability, responsibility handoffs, recovery and whether the artifact solves the intended problem.
3. Presentation and delivery review: inspect the exact rendered artifact for hierarchy, visual cohesion, readability, domain-appropriate specimens, reader-contract/navigation failures and delivery defects. Verify the application handoff preserves approval and execution boundaries.

Use independent reviewers for substantive passes when available. Give reviewers the brief, sources and exact artifact, without the author's diagnosis or expected findings. Independence is limited when all reviewers share a model or context; state that honestly. If no independent reviewer is available, disclose self-review and deliberately change review lens.

## Record and resolve findings

Use locally appropriate identifiers. A finding needs location, observed defect, evidence or reproduction, consequence, severity, proposed remedy and resolution/retest result. Record the artifact revision, affected user goal, confidence and evidence type separately from severity and priority. For consequential findings, have someone other than the fixer verify the changed artifact and nearby dependencies when available. If retest is unavailable or inconclusive, keep closure unverified. Relevant source or build changes can invalidate earlier passes. Do not manufacture findings to prove a pass happened.

- Critical: invalidates trust or introduces serious safety/privacy/authority risk
- High: prevents a core outcome or leaves a major contradiction/unsupported decision
- Medium: materially impairs a secondary outcome, clarity or recovery
- Low: localized polish or limited inconvenience

These labels guide prioritization; they do not substitute for judgment. Revise root causes and inspect affected dependencies, not only the reported symptom. Retest the same case against the revised version. Preserve a concise audit of meaningful changes, including rejected critiques and why.

## Stop honestly

Agree a bounded review budget appropriate to complexity. Require multiple substantive passes and a final check on the revised artifact. A decision-ready candidate may contain consequential unresolved choices when each is framed with evidence, alternatives, a recommendation and consequences. Do not label the dossier verified-complete or ready for implementation while critical/high defects or required approvals remain unresolved. If the budget ends first, deliver the current candidate with precise status and the smallest next decision or work needed. Owner acceptance remains separate.

Do not equate zero discovered defects with proof of absence, or test count with exceptional quality. Require direct evidence for claims such as beautiful, usable, persistent, complete or current. Record what remains untested. Distinguish a valid, honestly bounded report from successful resolution of its intended question; preserve unresolved outcome status after an inconclusive result.

## Learn without bureaucracy

After delivery identify specific friction, likely causes, immediate workarounds and durable improvements. Prefer a small change demonstrated on another example over a broad new framework. Keep domain acceptance criteria project-owned. Improve a shared method only when the lesson transfers and adoption remains optional.
